::: zh
**三分损益** 是中国古代的生律方法：取一段律管或弦的长度为基准，减去三分之一（损）得到上方五度，加上三分之一（益）得到下方四度，如此交替相生，依次推出十二律。

它与西方的五度相生同源，但算的是管长与弦长；这样生出十二律之后，再往下相生仍不能回到出发的黄钟，而留下一个音差。这个不闭合，正是后来十二平均律要解决的问题。**三分损益给出了十二律，却没有给出自由转调。**

::: en
**Sanfen sunyi** is the ancient Chinese method of generating pitches: take the length of a pitch pipe or string, subtract one third (sun) to reach the fifth above, add one third (yi) to reach the fourth below, and repeat the alternation to produce the twelve lü in order.

It works on the same principle as Western Pythagorean tuning, but measures pipe and string lengths. After the twelve have been generated, the chain still fails to return to Huangzhong, leaving a comma; that gap is exactly what twelve-tone equal temperament later solved. **Sanfen sunyi produces twelve pitches but not free modulation.**
