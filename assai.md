::: zh
**很、十分（assai）** 是放在术语后面的程度词，用来把前面的意思推得更强：allegro assai 是「很快的快板」，adagio assai 是「很慢的柔板」。它本身不规定速度，只负责加码。

assai 与 [[glossary:molto]] 常可互换，但语气略有差别：molto 更直接、更口语，assai 在旧谱和意大利语声乐里更常见，有时带一点「足够、相当」的分寸感。**看到 assai 不要只做加法**——它加强的是术语的性格，若把 allegro assai 弹成拼命赶，就只剩速度没有快板；与 [[glossary:poco]] 的方向正好相反。
:::

::: en
**Assai** is an intensifier placed after a mark, pushing its meaning further: allegro assai is a very fast allegro, adagio assai a very slow adagio. It sets no speed of its own; it only adds weight.

Assai and molto are often interchangeable, but the tone differs: molto is blunter and more conversational, while assai is commoner in older prints and Italian vocal music, where it can carry a shade of "quite" or "enough". **Do not treat assai as mere addition** — it intensifies the character of the term, and an allegro assai driven at breakneck speed is speed without allegro.
:::
