::: zh
**悲怆地（patetico）** 来自希腊语 pathos「受苦、情感」，经 pathētikos 进入意大利语。它指**深沉而有强度的悲情**，不是现代英语里「可怜」的意思；声音要有挣扎的重量。

它与 mesto 都写悲伤，分工不同：mesto 偏**哀悼的暗色**，patetico 偏**情感的爆发与撕扯**。常见误解是把它演成自怜——过多的滑音与抽泣式的停顿反而削弱悲剧感。

贝多芬的《悲怆》奏鸣曲让这个词广为人知，但它并不只属于那一部作品。可与 [[glossary:appassionato]]、[[glossary:mesto]] 对读。
:::

::: en
**Patetico** comes from Greek pathos, "suffering, feeling", through pathētikos. It means **deep, intense sorrow**, not the modern English "pathetic"; the sound must carry the weight of struggle, phrases driven by inner tension.

With mesto it shares the subject of grief but divides the work: mesto leans towards **the dark colour of mourning**, patetico towards **outburst and tearing**. The usual mistake is to make it self-pitying — excess portamento and sobbing pauses weaken tragedy; pathos needs a frame and restrained intensity.

Beethoven's "Pathétique" Sonata made the word widely known, but it does not belong to that work alone. Compare appassionato and mesto.
:::
