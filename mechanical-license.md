::: zh
**机械复制权（mechanical license）** 是**把一首歌录成音频、并复制发行**的权利。它管的是**词曲**，不是某一次录音：你翻唱一首歌、压成唱片或上传流媒体，需要的是机械复制授权；如果你直接用原版录音，还需要**录音**授权。

它和同步权不同：机械复制权管「声音载体」，同步权管「音画结合」。在一些国家，机械复制权有强制许可与法定费率——符合条件就能录，不必逐首谈判；条件与费率因国家而异。常见误解是「翻唱不用授权」：翻唱仍需词曲授权，只是通常走法定或集体渠道。

::: en
**A mechanical licence** is the right to **record a song and reproduce it** in audio. It covers the **composition**, not a particular recording: covering a song and releasing it on a record or streaming service needs a mechanical licence; using the original recording also needs a **master** licence.

It differs from a sync licence: mechanical covers the sound carrier, sync covers music paired with picture. Some countries provide a compulsory mechanical licence at a statutory rate — if the conditions are met you may record without negotiating song by song; the terms and rates vary by country. The usual mistake is to think a cover needs no licence: it still needs the composition rights, usually through a statutory or collective route.
