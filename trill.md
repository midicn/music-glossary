::: zh
**颤音（trill）** 是主音与**上方邻音**的快速交替，谱面上写 tr 加一条波浪线。它和 **vibrato（振动）** 是两件事：vibrato 是同一个音的音高轻微起伏，trill 是两个不同音高的来回。

起始音在历史上不统一：巴洛克颤音常从上方邻音开始，古典之后更常从主音开始；结束时常带一个回音式的收尾。**颤音不是「越快越好」**：速度、均匀度与收尾，决定了它是装饰还是噪音。

::: en
**A trill** is a rapid alternation between the main note and the **note above**, written tr with a wavy line. It is not **vibrato**: vibrato is a small fluctuation of pitch on one note, a trill is a back-and-forth between two different pitches.

The starting note has varied historically: Baroque trills often begin on the upper auxiliary, later ones more often on the main note, and they frequently end with a turn-like close. **A trill is not "the faster the better"**: speed, evenness and the ending decide whether it ornaments or merely rattles.
