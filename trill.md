::: zh
**颤音（trill）**是主音与上方邻音的快速交替，常用 tr 或 tr 加波浪线标记。它与 **vibrato（振动）**不同：vibrato 是同一音高附近的连续起伏，trill 则交替两个音。

起始音、邻音的变化音和收尾方式依作品、时期及演奏传统而异，可能从上方邻音或主音开始。演奏时要核对谱面、调号和版本说明，不能只按年代或一套固定速度处理所有颤音。

::: en
**A trill** rapidly alternates a principal note with its upper neighbour, commonly marked tr or tr followed by a wavy line. It differs from **vibrato**, a continuous fluctuation around one pitch.

Starting note, inflection of the neighbour and ending vary with the work, period and performance practice. A trill may begin on the upper neighbour or principal note. Check the notation, key signature and edition rather than applying one starting rule or speed to every trill.
