::: zh
**二二拍（cut time）** 记作 **𝄵**，即在 C 上加一道竖线，正式名称是 **alla breve**。它表示每小节两个二分音符拍：虽然一小节仍等于四个四分音符，但**数拍单位从四分音符变成二分音符**，重音落在每小节的两个大拍上。

它常被当作「快一点的 4/4」，但记谱意义不止于速度。拍的单位变大，音乐的重心也跟着变：同样的音符在 cut time 里更强调两拍骨架，适合进行曲、快板交响曲与复调段落。

常见误解：看到竖线就以为只是提醒「演奏得快」。竖线首先改变的是拍的单位与分组，速度是随之而来的效果。
:::

::: en
**Cut time** is written **𝄵** — a C with a vertical stroke — and its formal name is **alla breve**. It gives two half-note beats to the bar: the bar still holds four quarter notes, but **the counting unit moves from the quarter to the half**, with the main accents on the two big beats.

It is often called "faster 4/4", yet the notation does more than speed things up. A larger beat changes the centre of gravity: the same notes stress a two-beat frame, which suits marches, fast symphonic movements and contrapuntal passages.

A common mistake is to see the stroke as a simple reminder to play fast. The stroke first changes the beat unit and grouping; the quicker pace follows from that.
:::
