::: zh
**音阶（scale）** 是把音高按高低排成的一级一级的梯子。真正决定它性格的不是「用了哪几个音」，而是**相邻两级之间的间距**：大调音阶是「全全半全全全半」，小调则是另一种排列。同样的七个音，换一种间距顺序，听起来就是另一种音乐。

音阶只是材料，不包含「哪个音是家」——那是调式与调性要回答的事。常见误解是把音阶与调式当同义词：**音阶说有哪些音、怎么排；调式说这些音围绕谁转。**

::: en
**A scale** is a ladder of pitches ordered by height. What gives it character is not "which notes are used" but **the distances between consecutive steps**: the major scale runs whole-whole-half-whole-whole-whole-half, the minor scale arranges them differently. The same seven notes in another order of steps sound like another music.

A scale is only material; it does not say which note is home — that is the job of mode and tonality. The usual mistake is to equate scale with mode: **a scale says which notes exist and in what order; a mode says what they revolve around.**
