::: zh
**低频陷阱（bass trap）** 是专门对付低频驻波与角落能量堆积的吸声结构。低频波长长，普通薄吸声材料几乎不起作用，需要足够厚度或多孔材料背后留空腔。

常见做法是把厚棉板斜跨墙角，或用膜式、亥姆霍兹共振结构针对特定频段；多只小陷阱分散摆放，通常比一只巨型陷阱更均匀。摆放前先测房间响应，确认问题在哪个频率。

常见误解是用均衡器拉平频响就能代替陷阱。均衡只能改直达声，驻波在房间不同位置仍然起伏；陷阱减少的是能量积累本身。
:::

::: en
**Bass traps** target low-frequency standing waves and energy building up in corners. Bass wavelengths are long, so thin absorbers barely affect them; effective traps need depth, or porous material with an air gap behind it.

A common build is a thick mineral-wool panel straddling a corner, or a membrane or Helmholtz resonator tuned to a specific band. Several smaller traps spread around the room usually give a more even result than one huge unit. Measure the room first to see which frequency is actually the problem.

The usual mistake is expecting EQ to replace traps. EQ changes the direct sound, while standing waves still vary from seat to seat; traps reduce the energy build-up itself.
:::
