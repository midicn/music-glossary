::: zh
**数字发行商（music distributor）** 把录音投递到流媒体平台与数字商店，处理元数据、UPC/ISRC、结算与分成。它通常按年费或抽成收费，**不取得母带所有权**，也不等于唱片公司或推广公司。

常见误解是「上了发行就等于有人帮你推歌」：发行只保证上架与收款通道，播放列表、宣传与营销通常另算。另一误区是把发行协议当版权转让。**看合同要问：覆盖哪些平台、抽成多少、下架与迁移是否自由。**

::: en
**A digital distributor** delivers recordings to streaming platforms and digital stores, handling metadata, UPC/ISRC codes, accounting and splits. It usually charges a fee or commission, **takes no ownership of the master**, and is not a label or a marketing agency.

**The usual mistake is to think distribution means promotion**: distribution secures delivery and a payment channel, while playlist pitching, publicity and marketing are usually separate. Another error is treating a distribution deal as a copyright transfer. **Ask which platforms are covered, what the cut is, and whether takedown and migration are free.**
