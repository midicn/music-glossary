::: zh
**加线（ledger line）** 是五线谱上下临时补画的短横线，用来安放超出谱表范围的音；加线的条数与位置决定音高。

加线只为当前音符服务，不会延长成新谱表。连续的高音区常改用 8va 或换谱号，连续的低音区用 8vb，以减少数线负担。

常见错误是数错线：第一条加线是与谱表相邻的那一条，线与间交替计算；上加线与下加线方向相反，不能凭距离猜音。
:::

::: en
**A ledger line** is a short line added above or below the staff to carry notes outside its range. The number and position of those lines fix the pitch.

Ledger lines belong to the notes they serve; they do not extend into a new staff. Long passages high above the staff are often rewritten with 8va or a clef change, and low passages with 8vb, to spare the reader the counting.

The usual error is counting the lines wrongly. The first ledger line is the one next to the staff, and lines and spaces alternate; upper and lower ledger lines run in opposite directions, so distance alone cannot tell you the note.
:::
