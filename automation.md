::: zh
**自动化（automation）** 是把参数的变化**录下来**：音量、声像、EQ、压缩、合成器旋钮，都可以随时间画成一条曲线，让软件在回放时自动执行。它让「手在推子上的一次动作」变成可重复、可修改的一部分。

它和 **MIDI 力度**不同：velocity 是每个音符的击键强度，automation 是**任意参数随时间的变化**。常见误解是「自动化只是音量渐强渐弱」——它可以让一个滤波器在副歌自动打开、让混响在句尾自动加深；自动化是混音的时间维度，不是补丁。

:::

::: en
**Automation** **records changes to parameters**: volume, pan, EQ, compression, synth knobs can be drawn as curves over time, and the software performs them on playback. It turns "one move of the hand on a fader" into a repeatable, editable part of the mix.

It differs from **MIDI velocity**: velocity is the strike strength of each note, automation is **any parameter changing over time**. The usual mistake is to think automation is only fade-ins and fade-outs — it can open a filter at the chorus or deepen reverb at a phrase ending; automation is the time dimension of mixing, not a patch.
