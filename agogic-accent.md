::: zh
**时值重音（agogic accent）** 是**用更长的时值**让一个音被听见：不靠更响，而靠「停留得更久」。它是重音的一种，和力度重音、节拍重音并列。

它常被忽略，却非常有效：一段旋律里稍微拉长某个音，听者立刻感到它「重要」。**常见误解是把重音等同于「更响」**——时值、音色、起音与和声都能制造重音；**只靠力度，音乐很快会变得粗糙。**

::: en
**An agogic accent** makes a note stand out **by lasting longer**: not louder, but staying. It is one kind of accent, alongside dynamic and metric accent.

It is often overlooked yet very effective: lengthen one note slightly in a melody and the ear immediately hears it as "important". **The usual mistake is to equate accent with "louder"** — duration, colour, attack and harmony all create accent; **relying only on dynamics soon makes the music coarse.**
