::: zh
**ben（充分地、很好地）** 是意大利语的**修饰语**，放在另一个术语前面加强它：**ben marcato** 是「充分强调」，**ben tenuto** 是「充分保持」。它不单独表示速度，也不单独表示力度。

它和 **molto** 不同：molto 是「很、非常」，ben 更接近「好好地、到位地」。**常见误解是把 ben 当成一个独立的力度记号**——它只是一个副词；**看到 ben，先看它修饰的是哪个词。**

::: en
**Ben** ("well, fully") is an Italian **modifier** placed before another term to strengthen it: **ben marcato** is "strongly marked", **ben tenuto** "fully held". It does not by itself mark a tempo or a dynamic.

It differs from **molto**: molto is "very", ben is closer to "well, properly". **The usual mistake is to read ben as a standalone dynamic** — it is only an adverb; **when you see ben, first see which word it modifies.**
