::: zh
**调号（key signature）** 是写在谱号与拍号之间的那组升号或降号，它说明这首曲子**默认使用哪些音**：比如两个升号通常对应 D 大调或 B 小调。调号让谱面不必在每个音上重复写升降记号。

**调号不等于调性**：D 大调与 B 小调共用同一个调号，要靠中心音与终止式才能分开；一首曲子还可以中途转调，调号不变而听觉的中心已经移动（或调号改变）。调号是**记谱的省事约定**，不是音乐的家。

::: en
**A key signature** is the group of sharps or flats between the clef and the time signature. It says **which notes the piece uses by default** — two sharps usually mean D major or B minor — so the score need not repeat an accidental on every note.

**A key signature is not a key**: D major and B minor share the same signature, and only the centre and the cadences tell them apart. A piece can also modulate while the signature stays put (or changes), so the heard centre moves even when the notation does not. A key signature is **a notational convenience, not the music's home**.
