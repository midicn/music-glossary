::: zh
**调（key）** 是「**主音 + 调式**」的组合：C 大调是「主音 C + 大调式」，A 小调是「主音 A + 小调式」。只说「C 调」在中文里常有歧义——它可能指 C 大调，也可能只是把主音定在 C；**完整的说法要同时给出主音与调式。**

它和**调号**不同：调号是谱面上的升降号，调是听觉上的中心与音阶结构。常见误解是把调号当调：C 大调与 A 小调共用一个调号，但它们是不同的调。调是音乐的家，调号只是门牌号的写法。

::: en
**A key** is the combination of **a tonic and a mode**: C major is "tonic C plus major mode", A minor "tonic A plus minor mode". Saying "the key of C" is ambiguous in Chinese — it may mean C major or merely a tonic on C; **a complete statement gives both the tonic and the mode.**

It differs from a **key signature**: the signature is the sharps and flats on the page, the key is the heard centre and scale structure. The usual mistake is to equate signature with key: C major and A minor share a signature but are different keys. A key is the music's home; the signature is only how the address is written.
