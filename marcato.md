::: zh
**强调地（marcato）** 来自意大利语 marcare「标记」，通行说法追溯到日耳曼语 markōn（划界、做记号）。它要求把音**一个个标出来**：起音明确，音与音之间有清楚的界。

marcato 要求强调、鲜明地演奏，具体的重音和起音方式依乐器与风格而定。piano marcato 可以同时要求较弱力度与清楚的强调；不能仅凭这个词决定必须断开每个音，或取消谱面已有的连奏线。

::: en
**Marcato** comes from the Italian marcare, "to mark"; the usual account traces it to Germanic markōn, "to mark a boundary". It asks that notes be **marked out one by one**: a clear onset and a clear edge between them.

Marcato asks for marked or emphatic playing; the appropriate accent and attack depend on instrument and style. Piano marcato can combine a quiet dynamic with clear emphasis. The word alone does not require separating every note or cancelling a written slur.
