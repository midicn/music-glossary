::: zh
**第一结尾（prima volta）** 是反复结构里**第一遍走的结尾**，谱上用「1.」和方括号标出。走到它的末端，会遇到反复记号，把演奏者送回开头或对应的 𝄆；第二遍则跳过这一房，改走第二结尾。

它不只是省纸的写法。古典奏鸣曲式的呈示部常在第一结尾里**转回主调**，好让反复顺利接回开头；第二结尾则留在新调，继续展开。**两个结尾承担不同的和声任务**，所以不能随意对调。

**常见误解**：反复后仍走第一结尾。正确顺序是第一遍走 1 房，反复后直接进 2 房。
:::

::: en
**The first ending (prima volta)** is the close taken on **the first pass** through a repeated section, marked with "1." and a bracket. At its end stands a repeat sign that sends the player back to the opening or to the matching 𝄆; on the second pass the player skips this ending and takes the second one.

It is more than a paper-saving device. In Classical sonata form the exposition's first ending often **turns back to the tonic** so the repeat can rejoin the opening smoothly, while the second ending stays in the new key and continues. **The two endings do different harmonic work**, so they cannot simply be swapped.

**A common mistake** is to play the first ending on both passes. The order is: first pass through ending 1, repeat, then go straight into ending 2.
:::
