::: zh
**主音化（tonicization）** 是**临时**把另一个和弦当成主音：给它配一个属和弦，让它短暂地「像家一样」站一下，然后回到真正的调。它和转调的区别是**时间与确认**：主音化不建立新的调性，只是借住。

它常由一个**副属和弦**（secondary dominant）完成：在 C 大调里，D 大和弦是 G 的属，G 是 C 的属——D 把 G 临时主音化。**常见误解是把主音化当成转调**：听它有没有被终止式确认，以及原来的主音有没有回来。

::: en
**Tonicization** treats another chord as a tonic **temporarily**: give it a dominant, let it feel like home for a moment, then return to the real key. It differs from modulation in **duration and confirmation**: tonicization borrows, it does not move the centre.

It is often done by a **secondary dominant**: in C major, a D major chord is the dominant of G, and G is the dominant of C — D tonicizes G for a moment. **The usual mistake is to hear tonicization as modulation**: listen for a cadence confirming the new key and for the old tonic returning.
