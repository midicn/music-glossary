::: zh
借用和弦（borrowed chord）是从同主音的另一调式借来的和弦，常见于大调向小调借 iv、降 VI、降 VII 与 iiø7。主音不变，音阶来源却暂时改变，因此这些和弦带有明显的调式色彩。

调式交替讲的是过程，借用和弦指的是结果。它和副属不同，副属指向另一个临时主音，借用仍围绕原主音；它也不要求解决，常作为色彩和弦停留或接下属。

分析时先确认主音没有移动，再看和弦的级数是否来自同主音调式。若新和弦之后出现新调的属与终止，中心已经搬家，那就不是借用，而是转调。
:::

::: en
A borrowed chord comes from the parallel mode on the same tonic. In a major key the usual borrowings are iv, ♭VI, ♭VII and iiø7; the tonic stays, but the scale source shifts for a moment, which gives these chords their modal colour.

Mixture names the process; borrowed chord names the result. It differs from a secondary dominant, which points to another temporary tonic, whereas a borrowing still circles the original tonic. It need not resolve and often stays as colour or leads to the subdominant.

To analyse one, first confirm that the tonic has not moved, then ask whether the chord's degree comes from the parallel mode. If a new dominant and cadence follow, the centre has moved and the event is a modulation, not a borrowing.
:::
