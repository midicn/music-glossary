## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 钢琴 |
| 英语 | piano |
| 意大利语 | pianoforte |
| 德语 | Klavier |
| 法语 | piano |

**piano** 是 **pianoforte** 的缩写：意大利语 **piano**（轻）+ **forte**（强），意思是「能轻能响」——这件乐器诞生时最大的卖点，是力度可以随触键变化。德语用 Klavier（键盘乐器），与英语的 piano 并不同源。

常见混淆：piano 在乐谱里还是「弱」的力度记号；同一个词，一件乐器与一个力度术语。中文「钢琴」与钢无关，是早期译名的沿用。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 钢琴 |
| English | piano |
| Italian | pianoforte |
| German | Klavier |
| French | piano |

**Piano** is short for **pianoforte**: Italian piano (soft) plus forte (loud) — "soft and loud", the selling point of the new instrument, whose dynamics respond to touch. German uses **Klavier** (keyboard instrument), a different root from English piano.

**Watch out**: piano is also the dynamic mark "soft"; one word, an instrument and a dynamic. The Chinese 钢琴 has nothing to do with steel; it is a retained early translation.
