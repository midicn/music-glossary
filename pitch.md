::: zh
**音高（pitch）** 是耳朵对声音频率的**排序感**：频率越高，听起来越高。但它不是频率本身——人耳对频率的感知是非线性的，两个音「听起来差多少」取决于它们的比例，而不是赫兹数的差。

音高也不是一个孤立的点：一个乐音包含基音与泛音，耳朵会在其中选出一个「主导」的音高。**同一个频率，在不同音色与响度下，听起来可以略有高低。** 所以音高是**听觉事件**，不是仪表读数。

::: en
**Pitch** is the ear's **sense of order** over frequency: the higher the frequency, the higher it sounds. But it is not frequency itself — hearing is non-linear, and how far apart two notes seem depends on their ratio, not on the difference in hertz.

Pitch is not an isolated point either: a musical tone contains a fundamental and overtones, and the ear selects one of them as the "dominant" pitch. **The same frequency can sound slightly higher or lower under a different timbre and loudness.** Pitch is an **auditory event**, not a meter reading.
