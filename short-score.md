::: zh
**缩写总谱（short score）** 把作品压缩到两三行谱表：常见写法是上行记高音织体、下行记低音，内声部择要保留。

它介于草稿与全总谱之间。作曲家先用缩写总谱定和声与声部走向，再据此配器；出版时也给指挥一个便于纵览的版本。

缩写总谱不是钢琴缩编谱。后者为钢琴演奏改写，前者仍按总谱思维组织，乐器提示与声部标记往往保留。
:::

::: en
**A short score** condenses a work onto two or three staves: typically the upper staff carries the treble texture, the lower staff the bass, and inner voices are kept only where they matter.

It sits between a sketch and a full score. Composers use one to fix harmony and voice leading before orchestrating, and publishers issue one to give conductors a compact overview.

A short score is not a piano reduction. The reduction is rewritten for two hands, whereas the short score still thinks in orchestral parts and often keeps instrument labels and voice markings.
:::
