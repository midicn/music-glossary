## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 大鼓 |
| 英语 | bass drum |
| 意大利语 | grancassa |
| 德语 | große Trommel |
| 法语 | grosse caisse |

**大鼓** 是管弦乐队中体积最大的鼓，鼓面大而低沉，通常没有固定音高，用软槌击奏，为乐队提供重音与低频声势。意大利语 grancassa 字面是「大箱」，德语 große Trommel、法语 grosse caisse 也都是「大鼓」的意思。

**常见混淆**：管弦乐大鼓与鼓组里的低音鼓（kick drum）不是一回事，后者常用踏板击打、多为单面；中文「大鼓」也常指中国的大鼓类乐器，需看语境。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 大鼓 |
| English | bass drum |
| Italian | grancassa |
| German | große Trommel |
| French | grosse caisse |

The **bass drum** is the largest drum of the orchestra, with a broad, deep head and usually no definite pitch; it is struck with soft mallets and gives weight and low-frequency power. Italian grancassa literally means "big box", while German große Trommel and French grosse caisse both mean "large drum".

**Watch out**: the orchestral bass drum is not the kick drum of a drum kit, which is played by pedal and often single-headed; Chinese 大鼓 can also name a Chinese barrel drum, so context matters.
