::: zh
**火热地（fuocoso）** 来自 fuoco，「火」。它要求音乐有燃烧般的热情与向前的推力，常与 con fuoco 通用。浪漫派作品尤其爱用它，把快速段落点成一团火。

fuocoso 的重点在内在温度，不在音量。**火可以低声燃烧**：一段轻奏的 fuocoso 仍要有节奏的灼热感；反过来，只有响度和速度而没有重音方向，只是吵闹。它靠近 [[glossary:appassionato]] 与 [[glossary:agitato]]，但 appassionato 更抒情，agitato 更不安，fuocoso 更强调燃烧的动能。
:::

::: en
**Fuocoso** comes from fuoco, "fire". It asks for burning warmth and forward thrust, and is often interchangeable with con fuoco. Romantic music especially favours it, turning fast passages into flame.

The point is inner temperature, not volume. **Fire can burn quietly**: a soft fuocoso still needs rhythmic heat, while loudness and speed without accentual direction are only noise. It stands near appassionato and agitato, but appassionato is more lyrical, agitato more restless, and fuocoso more purely kinetic.
:::
