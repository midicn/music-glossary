::: zh
**稍慢（meno mosso）** 是「比刚才慢一点」的指令，常出现在段落转换处：前一段激烈，后一段写 meno mosso，音乐就沉下来。它和 più mosso 是一对，都是**相对**于前一个速度的调整。

减速不等于「拖」：好的 meno mosso 会让音乐变得更宽、更有呼吸，而不是突然失去动力。**常见误解是把它当成 ritardando**——ritardando 是逐渐变慢，meno mosso 是到达一个新速度后**保持**。

::: en
**Meno mosso** means "a little slower than before", often at a section change: a turbulent passage gives way to meno mosso and the music settles. It is the pair of più mosso; both are adjustments **relative** to the previous tempo.

Slowing down is not dragging: a good meno mosso makes the music broader and more breathing, not suddenly powerless. **The usual mistake is to treat it as a ritardando** — a ritardando slows gradually, while meno mosso reaches a new tempo and **stays there**.
