::: zh
延伸和弦（extended chord）是在七和弦之上继续叠三度，加入九、十一或十三音的和弦。数字越过八度仍按音级计算，九音即高八度的二音，十三音即高八度的六音。它是这类和弦的总称，不是某一种固定结构。

实际演奏很少把音弹满。低音区容易浑浊，五音甚至根音常被省略，九音以上的色彩音却要保留；省略不等于不存在，耳朵仍按完整叠置来听。

它和加音和弦的分界在七音：延伸和弦默认包含七音，加音和弦则跳过七音，把九音或六音直接贴在三和弦上。判断时先看七音在不在，而不是只看有没有九音。
:::

::: en
An extended chord keeps stacking thirds above a seventh chord, adding a ninth, eleventh or thirteenth. The numbers keep counting past the octave: a ninth is the second degree raised by an octave, a thirteenth the sixth. The term names a family, not one fixed structure.

Players rarely sound every note. The low register turns muddy, so the fifth and even the root are often dropped while the upper colour notes remain. Omission does not erase them; the ear still hears a full stack.

The line between extended and added-tone chords is the seventh. An extension normally includes it; an added tone skips it and places a ninth or sixth directly on a triad. Listen for the seventh, not merely for a ninth.
:::
