::: zh
**主题变形（thematic transformation）**是让同一个主题在新的戏剧情境里换一副面孔：改性格、改节奏、改调式、改配器，甚至从抒情变成狰狞，却仍认得出它的来历。柏辽兹的固定乐思、李斯特与瓦格纳的主导动机，都把这种手法推到中心。

它与变奏不同：变奏守着主题的结构与和声骨架，只改表面；主题变形让主题适配新场景，段落长度、速度、调性都可变。它与动机发展的区别在尺度：变形保留主题的完整轮廓，发展则拆到动机。

**主题变形靠「认得出」成立。** 改得不够，戏剧性不足；改得太多，来历就断了。作曲家往往保留一个特征音程、节奏型或音色，作为追踪主题的线索。
:::

::: en
**Thematic transformation** lets one theme wear a new face in a new situation: a change of character, rhythm, mode, orchestration, even from lyric to grotesque, while its origins remain audible. Berlioz's idée fixe in the Symphonie fantastique, and the leading motives of Liszt and Wagner, place the device at the centre.

It differs from variation, which guards the theme's structure and harmonic frame and works on the surface. Transformation may reshape the theme to fit a scene, changing length, tempo and key. It differs from motivic development in scale: transformation keeps the theme's full outline, while development breaks it to the motive.

**Transformation works only if the theme is still recognised.** Change too little and the drama stalls; change too much and the thread snaps. Composers usually keep one signature interval, rhythmic figure or colour as a clue.
:::
