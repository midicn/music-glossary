::: zh
**虔诚地（religioso）** 来自拉丁语 religiosus，与 religio「宗教、敬畏」同源。它要求音乐进入**祈祷的姿态**：语速平稳，音量收敛，句子像被双手托着。

它不同于一般意义上的「安静」：核心是**敬畏与专注**，哪怕在强奏里也可以虔诚。**常见误解是把 religioso 处理成慢速加弱奏**——没有专注，慢与弱只会显得空。

它常出现在慢乐章、弥撒与安魂曲中，也见于歌剧的祈祷场景；可与 [[glossary:maestoso]]、[[glossary:sostenuto]] 对读。
:::

::: en
**Religioso** comes from Latin religiosus, related to religio, "religion, reverence". The music should take on **the posture of prayer**: an even pace, restrained volume, phrases held as if in both hands, in no hurry to finish.

It is not simply "quiet": at its centre are **reverence and attention**, and it can be devout even at full volume. **The usual mistake is to treat religioso as a default setting of slow plus soft** — without inward attention, slow and soft only sound empty.

It appears in slow movements, masses and requiems, and in prayer scenes of opera; beside maestoso and sostenuto it shows how majesty, sustain and devotion each do their work.
:::
