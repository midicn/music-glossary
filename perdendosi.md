::: zh
**渐失、消散（perdendosi）** 来自 perdersi，「迷失、失去自己」。它要求声音在减弱的同时失去清晰度与存在感，像远远散去，常用于极安静的收尾。它比一般的渐弱更主观：不只是音量下降，而是音乐「找不到自己」。

与 [[glossary:morendo]] 相比，perdendosi 更强调轮廓的瓦解：音色变薄、起音变模糊、节奏的边界松动，最后只剩一点余响。**它不是让演奏者放弃控制**，而是把控制转向更细的层面——弓速、气息、踏板都要更稳。它常与 [[glossary:calando]] 或 [[glossary:smorzando]] 并用，但很少与突弱同现；它要的是远去，不是收住。
:::

::: en
**Perdendosi** comes from perdersi, "to lose oneself". It asks the sound to lose clarity and presence as it fades, as if drifting out of earshot, and is common at very quiet endings. It is more subjective than a plain diminuendo: not just less volume, but music losing its bearings.

Beside morendo, perdendosi stresses the collapse of outline: thinner tone, blurred attacks, looser rhythmic edges, until only a trace remains. **It does not mean the player gives up control**; control moves to a finer level, with steadier bow speed, breath and pedal. It often joins calando or smorzando, and rarely appears with a sudden drop: the point is distance, not a stop.
:::
