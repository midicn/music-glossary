::: zh
**声部交错（voice crossing）** 是两个声部在某一刻**交换了高低位置**：本来在上方的声部走到下方，下方的声部走到上方。它不一定错，但会**削弱每条线的独立性**，让听者难以分辨谁是谁。

它和**声部超越（voice overlap）**不同：交错是「位置互换」，超越是一个声部越过另一个声部刚刚所在的位置。常见误解是「交错一律禁止」——在钢琴织体与爵士编配里它很常见；但在严格对位里，它需要谨慎处理。

::: en
**Voice crossing** is the moment two voices **swap their high-low positions**: the upper voice moves below, the lower moves above. It is not automatically wrong, but it **weakens the independence of each line** and makes it harder to hear who is who.

It differs from **voice overlap**: crossing exchanges positions, overlap is one voice moving past where another has just been. The usual mistake is to forbid it outright — it is common in piano textures and jazz arranging; in strict counterpoint, it needs care.
