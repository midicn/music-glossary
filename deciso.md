::: zh
**果断地（deciso）** 来自意大利语 decidere「决定」，拉丁语 decidere 由 de-「离」与 caedere「切」构成，原意是「切断」。它要求每个起音与节奏转折都**明确、不含糊、不拖泥带水**。

它描述**态度**，不是速度，也不等于响。deciso 可以是轻声的，只要发音点干净、时值交代清楚。**常见误解是把 deciso 弹成「用力砸」**——果断是判断清楚后的干脆，不是音量；用力过度反而让节奏变笨。

::: en
**Deciso** comes from the Italian decidere, "to decide"; Latin decidere joins de- ("off") and caedere ("to cut"), so the root sense is "cut off". Every attack and every rhythmic turn should be **clear, unambiguous, without dragging**.

It describes **attitude**, not speed, and not loudness. Deciso can be quiet as long as the onset is clean and the values are plainly told. **The usual mistake is to play deciso as "hit harder"** — decision is crispness after a clear judgement, not volume; too much force only makes the rhythm heavy-footed.
