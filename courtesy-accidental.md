::: zh
**提示性临时记号（courtesy accidental）** 是按规则本可不写、却为提醒演奏者而重复写出的升降号或还原号。

它常出现在跨小节线后的同一音、换了八度的重复音、调号刚变之后，或声部长期休止后重新进入处。它不改变音高，只防止误读。

各出版社做法不一：有的加圆括号，有的用小号符号，有的只靠排版间隔。看到多余记号，先判断它是否只是提醒，不要当成新的变化音。
:::

::: en
**A courtesy accidental**, also called a cautionary accidental, is a sharp, flat or natural repeated as a reminder even though the rules do not require it.

It appears after a barline when the same note returns, when a pitch repeats in another octave, after a key-signature change, or when a part re-enters after a long rest. It does not change pitch; it guards against misreading.

Publishers differ: some add parentheses, some use a smaller sign, some rely on spacing alone. When you meet an extra accidental, ask whether it is only a reminder before treating it as a new inflection.
:::
