::: zh
**提示乐句（cue）** 是分谱里用小号音符印出的其他声部片段，多出现在长休止之后，告诉演奏者「听到这里就该进入」。

提示常取自旋律声部或前一小节的进入句，并标出原乐器；它只供辨认，不要求本声部演奏，字号与正式音符有明显区别。

提示不是装饰。改写配器或删减段落后，提示也要跟着改，否则演奏者会按错误线索进入；它与排练记号一起，构成分谱的定位系统。
:::

::: en
**A cue** is a short passage from another part printed in small notes in a player's part, usually after a long rest, to signal that the entry is coming.

Cues are often taken from the melody or from the phrase just before the entry, and the source instrument is named. They are for recognition only, not for performance, and their smaller size keeps them distinct from real notes.

A cue is not decoration. When scoring is changed or bars are cut, the cues must change too, or the player will follow a false lead. Together with rehearsal marks they form the locating system of a part.
:::
