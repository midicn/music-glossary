::: zh
**母带权（master rights）** 是**某一次具体录音**的权利：谁拥有这段声音、谁可以授权它被复制、同步、采样或重混。它和**词曲版权**是两层权利：一首歌的词曲可能属于作者与出版方，录音却属于唱片公司或独立音乐人。

所以「翻唱」与「用原版录音」是两件事：翻唱只需词曲授权（机械复制权），使用原版录音还要母带授权（同步权或采样许可）。**常见误解是「我写了这首歌，就拥有它的录音」**——除非合同另有约定，录音的权利通常归出资制作的一方。看到「母带归谁」这句话，先分清它说的是声音还是作品。

::: en
**Master rights** are the rights in **one specific recording**: who owns this sound and who may license it to be copied, synchronized, sampled or remixed. They sit on a different layer from the **composition**: the song may belong to writers and a publisher, while the recording belongs to a label or an independent artist.

So "covering a song" and "using the original recording" are two different things: a cover needs the composition (mechanical), while using the original also needs the master (sync or sample clearance). **The usual mistake is to think writing the song owns the recording** — unless a contract says otherwise, the recording usually belongs to whoever paid for it. **When you see "who owns the master", first ask whether it means the sound or the work.**
