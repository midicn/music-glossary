::: zh
**插入（insert）** 把效果器串在通道的信号链里：整路信号都经过它，再往下走。压缩、EQ、齿音消除通常放在插入位，因为它们要处理这一轨的全部声音。

插入的顺序会改变结果：先修掉低频再压缩，与先压缩再修低频，听感不同。插入效果一般是全湿声，所以混响、延迟更适合放在发送与返回的路径上。

常见误解是插入和发送可以互换。插入改变整条通道，发送只取一部分信号；要共享空间或保留干声，用发送，压缩参 [[glossary:compressor]]。
:::

::: en
**An insert** places a processor directly in a channel's signal path: the whole signal passes through it before continuing. Compression, EQ and de-essing usually sit in inserts because they must act on everything that channel carries.

Insert order changes the result: cutting lows before compression sounds different from compressing first and cutting later. Insert effects are normally 100 percent wet with no dry blend, which is why reverb and delay are better placed on sends and returns.

The usual mistake is to treat inserts and sends as interchangeable. An insert changes the entire channel; a send takes only a portion. Use a send when the effect is shared or the dry signal must stay intact.
:::
