::: zh
**邻音（neighbor tone）** 是**离开一个音、到上方或下方的邻音、再回来**的非和弦音：上邻音、下邻音各有一种。它常出现在弱拍，给旋律加一点「绕」的装饰。

它和**经过音**不同：经过音是**朝一个方向走**，邻音是**离开再回来**。**常见误解是「邻音只是加花」**——重拍上的邻音会形成**倚音式的张力**；轻拍上的邻音则决定旋律的**灵活度**。

::: en
**A neighbour tone** leaves a note, moves to the **upper or lower neighbour**, and returns: an upper or lower auxiliary. It often sits on weak beats, adding a small turn to the melody.

It differs from a **passing tone**: a passing tone **moves in one direction**, a neighbour **leaves and returns**. **The usual mistake is to treat it as mere decoration** — on a strong beat a neighbour creates **appoggiatura-like tension**; on a weak beat it decides the melody's **agility**.
