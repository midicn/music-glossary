::: zh
**换谱号（clef change）** 是在同一行谱表的中途改换谱号，让音域继续落在五线之内，常见于大提琴、巴松、长号与声乐。

新谱号写在需要生效的音符之前，多落在小节线后或乐句转折处；如果音域又回到原区，可以再换回，也可以靠加线应付。

换谱号改变的是读法，不是音高。演奏者按新谱号读同一批线与间的位置；它常与加线、8va 配合，但三者不能互相替代。
:::

::: en
**A clef change** replaces the clef in the middle of a staff so that the range stays inside the five lines. It is common in cello, bassoon, trombone and vocal music.

The new clef is placed before the note where it takes effect, usually after a barline or at a phrase break. If the range returns, the old clef may be restored, or ledger lines may carry the passage instead.

A clef change alters how the staff is read, not the pitch. The player reads the same lines and spaces under a new sign; it often works with ledger lines and octave signs, but none of the three replaces another.
:::
