::: zh
**渐慢（ritardando，缩写 rit. 或 ritard.）** 是速度**逐渐放慢**的指示。它常出现在乐句结尾、段落收束或全曲终止之前，让音乐「落地」。

它和 **rallentando** 很接近，很多乐谱混用；细微差别在于 rallentando 有时更强调「放宽、变宽」，ritardando 更强调「变慢」。**常见误解是把渐慢当成「停下来」**——渐慢仍有方向，只是每一步比上一步更宽；如果慢到失去脉搏，音乐就死了。

::: en
**Ritardando (rit. or ritard.)** means **gradually getting slower**. It often appears at the end of a phrase, the close of a section or before the final cadence, letting the music "land".

It is close to **rallentando**, and many scores use them interchangeably; the nuance is that rallentando can stress "broadening" while ritardando stresses "slowing". **The usual mistake is to let a ritardando become a stop** — it still has direction, each step wider than the last; slow it until the pulse disappears and the music dies.
