::: zh
**室内乐（chamber music）** 是小编制的器乐合奏：**一人一个声部、没有指挥**，靠演奏者彼此听、彼此让。最常见的编制是弦乐四重奏（两把小提琴、中提琴、大提琴），也有钢琴三重奏、木管五重奏等。

它和管弦乐的区别不是「人少」，而是**关系**：管弦乐靠指挥统一，室内乐靠对话形成；每个人都能被听见，也都要为别人让出空间。**室内乐不是「小编制的交响曲」**——它把音乐从「作品」变成「几个人共同完成的一件事」。

::: en
**Chamber music** is instrumental ensemble music on a small scale: **one player to a part, no conductor**, with the players listening and yielding to one another. The most common group is the string quartet (two violins, viola, cello), alongside the piano trio and wind quintet.

It differs from orchestral music not in "fewer people" but in **relationship**: an orchestra is unified by a conductor, chamber music is formed by conversation; every player can be heard and must make room for the others. **Chamber music is not a "small-scale symphony"** — it turns music from a "work" into something several people accomplish together.
