::: zh
**叠接（elision）**指前一句的收尾同时充当后一句的开头：终止和弦既结束旧句，又启动新句，两句在同一点交接，句界被吞掉。听感上前句还没落地，后句已经出发，音乐因此被推着走。

叠接常见于古典与浪漫派的乐句之间，尤其在需要连续推进的段落：前句的终止音被后句当作起点，低音或旋律共用一音。它与「句句分明」相反，后者每句有独立收束与呼吸；叠接则故意不让耳朵休息。

**叠接不是没有句界，而是句界重叠。** 分析时仍要写出前句的终止与后句的起点，再看二者是否落在同一拍、同一音上。若只是后句紧接前句、并无共用，那是紧凑连接，不是叠接。
:::

::: en
**Elision** occurs when one phrase's close also begins the next: the cadential chord ends the old and launches the new, so the two meet at one point and the boundary vanishes. The first has barely landed before the second is under way, and the music is pushed forward.

It is common in Classical and Romantic music, especially where momentum must continue: one phrase's closing note becomes the next phrase's start, shared by bass or melody. It is the opposite of neatly separated phrasing, where every phrase gets its own close and breath.

**Elision does not remove the boundary; it overlaps it.** Still mark the first phrase's cadence and the second phrase's beginning, then ask whether they fall on the same beat and note. A phrase that merely follows closely, sharing nothing, is a tight link, not an elision.
:::
