::: zh
**和声（harmony）** 是音**同时响起**时形成的关系，以及这些关系怎样前后相接。它关注的是纵向的「叠」与横向的「走」：一个和弦是什么、它往哪里去、到达时是否解决。

和声与对位不同：**对位关心几条旋律怎样各自独立地走，和声关心它们同时响时构成什么。** 同一段音乐可以既有对位又有和声——巴赫的赋格是两条线在走，也是和声在支撑。**和声不是「伴奏」，它是音乐的重力系统。**

::: en
**Harmony** is the relation formed when notes **sound at the same time**, and how those relations move one to the next. It concerns both the vertical "stack" and the horizontal "walk": what a chord is, where it goes, whether it resolves on arrival.

Harmony differs from counterpoint: **counterpoint cares how several melodies move independently, harmony cares what they make when they sound together.** One passage can be both — a Bach fugue is lines moving and harmony supporting. **Harmony is not "accompaniment"; it is the music's system of gravity.**
