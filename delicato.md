::: zh
**精致地（delicato）** 要求触感细腻、色彩透明、句法考究。它不是「弱」，而是把注意力放在最小的细节上：一个装饰音的分量，一次换气的长短，和声变化时音色的微调。

在钢琴与室内乐里，delicato 常与 [[glossary:dolce]]、[[glossary:leggiero]] 一起出现，但三者侧重不同：dolce 偏甜，leggiero 偏轻，delicato 偏精。**精致最怕用力过猛**；一旦为了让人听见而加重，纹理就会变粗。它是控制出来的轻柔，不是退让出来的虚弱。
:::

::: en
**Delicato** asks for a fine touch, transparent colour and careful phrasing. It does not mean "weak"; it directs attention to the smallest details: the weight of an ornament, the length of a breath, a slight change of colour as the harmony turns.

In piano and chamber music it often appears beside dolce and leggiero, though the three differ: dolce leans sweet, leggiero light, delicato precise. **Delicacy suffers most from forcing**; add weight to be heard and the texture coarsens. It is lightness achieved by control, not weakness by default.
:::
