## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 钹 |
| 英语 | cymbals |
| 意大利语 | piatti |
| 德语 | Becken |
| 法语 | cymbales |

**钹** 是成对的金属打击乐器，管弦乐谱用复数 cymbals；意大利语 **piatti** 取「盘子」的形状，德语 **Becken** 与法语 **cymbales** 同出拉丁语 cymbalum。五语名称里只有中文用单字「钹」，其余多为复数，因为通常两片对击。

常见混淆：中文「钹」与「镲」在民间常混用，大小形制各地不一（通行说法）；管弦乐的 piatti 是双手对击的一对，suspended cymbal 是悬吊的单片。记谱只标击法，不标音高。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 钹 |
| English | cymbals |
| Italian | piatti |
| German | Becken |
| French | cymbales |

**Cymbals** are struck metal instruments played in pairs; orchestral scores use the plural. Italian **piatti** names their plate shape, while German **Becken** and French **cymbales** descend from Latin cymbalum. Among the five names only Chinese uses a single character, 钹; the others are plural because two plates normally clash together.

Watch out: Chinese 钹 and 镲 are often used interchangeably in folk practice, with sizes and shapes varying by region (a common usage); orchestral piatti are a hand-clashed pair, while a suspended cymbal is a separate single plate. Notation marks the stroke, not a pitch.
