::: zh
**房间模式（room mode）** 是房间内由墙面之间反射形成的驻波，特定频率在固定位置增强或抵消。轴向模式由一对平行墙决定，频率约为声速除以墙距的两倍；切向与斜向模式则涉及更多表面。

低频波长长，小房间的模式间隔大，容易在某个音高上轰鸣、在另一个位置消失。房间比例、音箱摆位与听音位都会改变激发程度；低频陷阱与多点测量比盲目加均衡更有效。

常见误解是以为只有大房间才有房间模式。任何封闭空间都有，只是频率与密度不同；小房间的问题往往更明显，也更难靠移动音箱完全避开。
:::

::: en
**Room modes** are standing waves formed by reflections between room surfaces, boosting or cancelling particular frequencies at fixed positions. Axial modes involve one pair of parallel walls, at roughly the speed of sound divided by twice the distance; tangential and oblique modes involve more surfaces.

Low frequencies have long wavelengths, so small rooms space their modes widely, booming on one note and disappearing at another seat. Room proportions, speaker placement and listening position all affect how strongly modes are excited. Bass traps and measurements at several points work better than blind equalisation.

The usual mistake is assuming only large rooms have modes. Every enclosed space does, though the frequencies and density differ; small rooms often show the problem more plainly and are harder to escape by moving speakers alone.
:::
