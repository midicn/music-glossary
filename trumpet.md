## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 小号 |
| 英语 | trumpet |
| 意大利语 | tromba |
| 德语 | Trompete |
| 法语 | trompette |

**小号** 是铜管族的高音乐器，靠嘴唇振动与活塞改变管长。中文「小号」的「小」是相对「大号」而言，不是音量的意思；英语 trumpet 与意大利语 tromba 同源，德语 Trompete、法语 trompette 都是这条词根的变体。

**常见混淆**：cornet（短号）与 trumpet 外形接近但管径与音色不同；中文里两者都常被笼统叫「小号」。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 小号 |
| English | trumpet |
| Italian | tromba |
| German | Trompete |
| French | trompette |

The **trumpet** is the high instrument of the brass family, changing tube length with valves while the lips vibrate. The Chinese 小号 means "small horn" relative to the tuba, not "small volume"; English trumpet and Italian tromba share a root, with German Trompete and French trompette as variants.

**Watch out**: the cornet looks close to the trumpet but differs in bore and colour; Chinese often calls both 小号 loosely.
