::: zh
**出版管理协议（administration deal）** 是作者保留版权份额，只委托管理方代为登记、授权、收款与对账的合同。管理方按收入收取**管理费**，常见为百分之十到二十，通常有固定期限，到期后权利回到作者手中。

它和出版转让的区别在于**不取得版权**；和共同出版的区别在于**不分份额**。常见误解是「管理方会像出版商一样推歌」：主动推广往往要另签协议或另付费用。**通识说明，不构成法律意见。**

::: en
**An administration deal** leaves the copyright share with the writer and appoints an administrator to register works, issue licences, collect income and account for it. The administrator charges an **administration fee** on income, commonly ten to twenty per cent, usually for a fixed term after which rights revert.

Unlike a publishing assignment it **takes no copyright**; unlike co-publishing it **takes no share**. **The usual mistake is to expect an administrator to pitch songs like a publisher**: active promotion is usually a separate deal or a separate fee. **This is general information, not legal advice.**
