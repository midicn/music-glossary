::: zh
**毅然决然地（risoluto）** 来自拉丁语 resolutus，是 resolvere「解开、定下」的过去分词。它要求**拿定主意之后的坚定**：起音清楚，节奏不游移。

它与 deciso 相近，但更重、更有推力；deciso 强调判断的干脆，risoluto 强调**决心已定**。**常见误解是把 risoluto 演成僵硬**——坚定不是把每个音钉死，句子仍要有呼吸。

它常出现在戏剧性强的段落，伴随附点、切分与厚重织体；可与 [[glossary:deciso]]、[[glossary:con-forza]] 对读，分清果断、坚决与有力。
:::

::: en
**Risoluto** comes from Latin resolutus, the past participle of resolvere, "to loosen, to settle". It asks for **firmness after a decision has been made**: clean onsets, a rhythm that does not wander, a direction stated once and kept.

It stands near deciso but is heavier and more driving; deciso stresses crisp judgement, risoluto stresses **a resolve already fixed**. **The usual mistake is to play it stiff** — firmness is not nailing every note down; the phrase still breathes, it simply refuses to hesitate.

It appears in strongly dramatic passages, often with dotted figures, syncopation and thick textures; beside deciso and con forza it shows the layers of decision, resolve and force.
:::
