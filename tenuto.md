::: zh
**保持（tenuto）** 要求把一个音奏足应有的时值，并在起音后维持住，谱面上用音符上方或下方的短横线标记。弦乐上是弓速均匀、弓压不松，钢琴上是把键按到底、不提前离键，管乐上是气息不断。

它常带一点**轻微的重量**，不是突强，而是让这个音在句子里「站住」；音色因此更饱满、更连，音头不一定更响。**常见误解是把 tenuto 当成「加长的断奏」或「重音」**：它首先是对时值的坚持，那条横线也不是 fermata 的延长。
:::

::: en
**Tenuto** asks the player to hold a note for its full value and keep it sounding, marked by a short horizontal line above or below the notehead. On strings it means an even bow speed with no release of weight; on the piano the key is held down to the last moment; in wind playing the breath does not stop.

It usually carries a **slight weight** — not a sharp accent, but a note that "stands" within the phrase. The colour is fuller and more connected, while the attack need not be louder. **The usual mistake is to treat tenuto as "a lengthened staccato" or as an accent**: it is first a commitment to duration, and its dash is not a fermata.
:::
