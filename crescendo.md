::: zh
**渐强（crescendo，缩写 cresc.）** 是音量**逐渐增大**的指示，谱面上也常用一条开口的「<」线表示。它常出现在**乐句向高潮推进**的地方，制造方向与期待。

它和**突强（sforzando）**不同：crescendo 是过程，sforzando 是一瞬间。**常见误解是把渐强弹成「一路加到最后」**——好的渐强有**层次与节制**，起点、拐点与终点都要设计；**从头响到尾，等于没有渐强。**

::: en
**Crescendo (cresc.)** means **gradually louder**, often drawn as an opening "<" hairpin. It usually pushes a phrase toward a climax, creating direction and expectation.

It differs from **sforzando**: crescendo is a process, sforzando a moment. **The usual mistake is to play it as "add volume all the way"** — a good crescendo has **levels and restraint**, with a designed start, turning point and arrival; **loud from beginning to end is no crescendo at all.**
