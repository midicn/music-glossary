::: zh
**优雅从容地（con grazia）** 与 grazioso 同源，字面「带着优雅」，常接在速度记号后，修饰速度的性格：线条匀称，重音不粗，句尾干净。

它与 grazioso 的差别多在句法位置；作为附加语，它提醒演奏者这个速度要**带着分寸**走，不赶也不拖。**常见误解是把它当成加装饰**——优雅来自比例，颤音与琶音救不回一句被弹粗的旋律。

在舞曲性格的乐章里尤其常见；可与 [[glossary:grazioso]]、[[glossary:leggiero]] 对读，分清优雅与轻盈。
:::

::: en
**Con grazia** shares its root with grazioso and literally means "with grace"; it usually follows a tempo mark and colours the character of the speed. It governs **bearing**: an even line, accents that do not rasp, phrases that close cleanly.

Its difference from grazioso lies mostly in syntax, not meaning; as a qualifier it warns that the tempo must move **with proportion** — neither hurried nor dragged. **The usual mistake is to hear it as an invitation to ornament** — grace comes from proportion and breath, and no pile of trills and arpeggios rescues a melody played coarsely.

It is especially common in movements of dance character; set beside grazioso and leggiero, it shows what grace and lightness each take care of.
:::
