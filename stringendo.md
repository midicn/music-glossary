::: zh
**渐紧渐快（stringendo）** 来自 stringere，「勒紧、压缩」。它不只是加速，还要求音乐同时变得更紧、更有压力，常与渐强一起出现，把乐句推向高点。速度与力度在这里是同一股劲。

它和 [[glossary:accelerando]] 的区别在于「紧」：单纯加速可以保持松弛，stringendo 却要让节奏的网格收缩、和声的张力增加。与 [[glossary:affrettando]] 相比，它更偏结构上的压缩，少了那点惊慌。**stringendo 需要终点**：没有目标的收紧会变成赶，收放之间要留出呼吸。它常与 [[glossary:agitato]] 的情绪重叠，但来源不同，一个来自织体，一个来自心情。
:::

::: en
**Stringendo** comes from stringere, "to tighten, to squeeze". It is not merely faster: the music must grow tighter and more pressured at the same time, often with a crescendo, driving the phrase to a peak. Speed and force are one impulse here.

It differs from accelerando in that tightness: a plain acceleration can stay relaxed, while stringendo contracts the rhythmic grid and raises harmonic tension. Against affrettando it is more structural compression and less panic. **Stringendo needs a destination**: tightening without a goal becomes mere rushing, and the release must leave room to breathe.
:::
