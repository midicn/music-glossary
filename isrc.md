::: zh
**ISRC（International Standard Recording Code）** 是**一段录音**的国际标准代码，由 12 个字符组成：国家码 + 登记机构 + 年份 + 序号。它标识的是**这一次录音**，不是这首词曲——同一首歌的录音室版、现场版、重混版各有自己的 ISRC。

它和 **ISWC**（词曲作品代码）、UPC（商品条码）不同：ISRC 管「录音」，ISWC 管「作品」，UPC 管「商品」。常见误解是「一个 ISRC 代表一首歌」——它代表的是录音；翻唱同一首歌，必须申请新的 ISRC。流媒体平台靠它把播放次数结算到正确的录音与权利人。

::: en
**An ISRC (International Standard Recording Code)** identifies **one recording**: twelve characters made of a country code, a registrant, a year and a designation. It names **this recording**, not the song — the studio version, the live version and the remix of one song each carry their own ISRC.

It differs from **ISWC** (the work code) and UPC (the product barcode): ISRC covers the recording, ISWC the composition, UPC the product. The usual mistake is to think one ISRC equals one song — it equals one recording; a cover needs a new ISRC. Streaming platforms use it to pay the right recording and rights holders.
