::: zh
**七和弦（seventh chord）** 是在三和弦上再叠一个三度，成为四个音：根音、三音、五音、七音。多出来的七音不稳定，**给和弦一个「想往前走」的方向**——属七和弦（大三和弦加小七度）尤其如此，它几乎天然指向主和弦。

常见类型有：属七、大七、小七、半减七、减七。名字由三音与七音共同决定，所以「小七」不等于「小和弦加任意七度」。七和弦可以转位，也可以继续叠成九、十一、十三和弦——**每多一层，色彩更浓，方向感也更复杂。**

::: en
**A seventh chord** adds one more third to a triad, making four notes: root, third, fifth and seventh. The seventh is unstable and **gives the chord a direction** — the dominant seventh (major triad plus minor seventh) especially, which almost naturally points to the tonic.

Common types include dominant, major, minor, half-diminished and diminished sevenths. The name depends on both the third and the seventh, so "minor seventh" is not "minor chord plus any seventh". Sevenths can be inverted and stacked further into ninths, elevenths and thirteenths — **each layer adds colour and complicates the direction.**
