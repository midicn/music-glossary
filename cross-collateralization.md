::: zh
**交叉抵扣（cross-collateralization）** 是把同一合约下多个项目或渠道的成本与收入**放在一起结算**：一个项目的亏损可以用另一个项目的收入抵掉，再计算是否回本与应付分成。

它常见于多张专辑、多个艺人与多地区的合约。常见误解是「每张唱片单独结算」：若合约写了交叉抵扣，赚钱的项目要先填平亏损项目。**谈合约要问：抵扣范围多大、是否跨艺人、是否跨渠道。通识说明，不构成法律意见。**

::: en
**Cross-collateralization** settles costs and income from several projects or channels **under one agreement**: a loss on one project can be offset against income from another before recoupment and further payments are calculated.

It is common in multi-album, multi-artist and multi-territory deals. **The usual mistake is to assume each release is accounted for separately**: where the clause applies, a profitable project must first cover the losses. **Ask how wide the offset runs: across albums, across artists, across income streams. This is general information, not legal advice.**
