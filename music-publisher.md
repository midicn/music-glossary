::: zh
**音乐出版商（music publisher）** 管理**词曲版权**：登记作品、发放机械与同步许可、收取版税，并为歌曲寻找翻唱、影视与广告使用机会。它签约的是作者，管理的是词曲，不是录音；唱片公司管录音，两者常被混为一谈。

出版合同里，作者通常把一部分份额或代理权交给出版方，形成**出版权分割**。常见误解是「出版商只拿钱不干活」：其价值在版权登记、许可渠道与催收；但作者也要看清期限、独家范围与转让条款。**通识说明，不构成法律意见。**

::: en
**A music publisher** administers **song copyrights**: it registers works, issues mechanical and sync licences, collects royalties, and seeks covers, film and advertising placements. It deals with writers and compositions, not recordings; a label handles recordings, and the two are often confused.

In a publishing contract the writer usually assigns a share or administration right to the publisher, creating a **publishing split**. The usual mistake is to think publishers take money and do nothing: their value lies in registration, licensing channels and collection. Still, read the term, exclusivity and assignment clauses. **This is general information, not legal advice.**
