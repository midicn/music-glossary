## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 低音单簧管 |
| 英语 | bass clarinet |
| 意大利语 | clarinetto basso |
| 德语 | Bassklarinette |
| 法语 | clarinette basse |

**低音单簧管** 是单簧管家族的低音乐器，通常为降 B 调，实音比记谱低大九度；意大利语 clarinetto basso、德语 Bassklarinette、法语 clarinette basse 都是「低音单簧管」的直译。它用单簧片，管身折返，喇叭口向上翻，在乐队里提供暗淡而柔和的低音木管色彩。

**常见混淆**：它常被与低音巴松（contrabassoon）混为一谈，但两者发声原理不同——低音单簧管是单簧片，音色偏暗而柔；低音巴松是双簧片，声音更厚重。中文「低音单簧管」也常被简称为「低音黑管」。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 低音单簧管 |
| English | bass clarinet |
| Italian | clarinetto basso |
| German | Bassklarinette |
| French | clarinette basse |

The **bass clarinet** is the low member of the clarinet family, usually in B flat and sounding a major ninth below its written notes. Italian clarinetto basso, German Bassklarinette and French clarinette basse all simply say "bass clarinet". It has a single reed, a folded body and an upward bell, and it supplies a dark, soft low woodwind colour.

**Watch out**: it is often confused with the contrabassoon, but the two work differently — the bass clarinet is single-reeded and softer-edged, the contrabassoon double-reeded and heavier.
