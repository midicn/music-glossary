::: zh
**旋律（melody）** 是一串按时间排列的音高，但它之所以是旋律而不是音阶练习，是因为它有**形状**：高低起伏的轮廓、级进与跳进的搭配、重复与变化，让人能把它记住、唱出来、认出来。

旋律不只是「音高的序列」：节奏、句法、重音与音色都参与它的身份。**同一串音高换一种节奏，可能就变成另一条旋律**；同一段旋律换一种配器，还是同一条旋律。判断旋律的关键是**可辨认性**，不是音的数量。

::: en
**A melody** is a succession of pitches in time, but it is a melody rather than a scale exercise because it has **shape**: a contour of rises and falls, a mix of steps and leaps, repetition and variation that let a listener remember it, sing it and recognise it.

A melody is not merely "a sequence of pitches": rhythm, phrasing, accent and timbre all belong to its identity. **The same pitches in another rhythm can become another melody**; the same melody in another orchestration is still the same melody. What matters is **recognisability**, not the number of notes.
