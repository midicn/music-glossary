::: zh
**数字低音（figured bass）** 在低音音符下方写数字，告诉演奏者这个低音上方要构成哪些音程：没有数字默认 5/3，6 表示第一转位，6/4 表示第二转位，7 表示七和弦。

数字是音程的简写，不是和弦代号：它只规定低音上方的音高结构，声部排列、重复音与连接由演奏者按对位规则补完。数字旁的升号、降号作用于该音程，而不是低音本身。

数字不是指法，也不是拍数。它既不管节奏，也不管用哪根手指，只描述和声；同一串数字可以配出完全不同的织体。
:::

::: en
**Figured bass** writes numbers under a bass note to show which intervals should sound above it: no figure means 5/3, 6 means first inversion, 6/4 second inversion, and 7 a seventh chord.

The figures abbreviate intervals, not chord symbols. They fix the pitch structure above the bass, while spacing, doubling and voice leading are supplied by the player from contrapuntal rules. A sharp or flat beside a figure alters that interval, not the bass note.

The numbers are neither fingerings nor beats. They say nothing about rhythm or which finger to use, only about harmony, and one progression can be realised in very different textures.
:::
