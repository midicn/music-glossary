::: zh
**混音总线（mix bus）** 是所有分轨与编组汇合的最后一条立体声总线，也叫主总线。这里的处理作用于整个混音：总线压缩、轻微 EQ、饱和，都在这一步。

它和编组总线不同：编组只汇总一组声音，混音总线汇总全部。总线压缩通常很轻，只求把各部分黏住；某件乐器要大动，应回到它的通道或编组。

常见误解是把混音总线当万能修补。总线处理放大已有平衡，不是替代平衡；推得越狠，越容易失去余量，也越难在母带挽回，收尾见 [[glossary:mixdown]]。
:::

::: en
**The mix bus** is the final stereo bus where all tracks and groups meet, also called the master bus. Processing here acts on the whole mix at once: bus compression, gentle EQ and saturation all belong to this stage.

It differs from a group bus: a group sums one family of sounds, while the mix bus sums everything. Bus compression is usually light, just enough to glue the parts together; if one instrument needs a big change, go back to its channel or group.

The usual mistake is to treat the mix bus as a universal repair shop. Bus processing magnifies an existing balance rather than replacing it; the harder it is pushed, the less headroom remains for mastering.
:::
