::: zh
**音程（interval）** 是两个音之间的距离。这个距离要用两把尺子同时量：**度数**数的是两音跨过几个音级名（C 到 E 是 C–D–E，三度），**音数**数的是中间隔了几个半音（C 到 E 是四个）。度数相同的音程可以宽度不同——C–E 是大三度（4 个半音），D–F 是小三度（3 个半音），大小音程的区别就从这里来。

常见误解是只数半音，或只数音级名。耳朵听到的「跳多远」是半音数，谱面写下的「几度」是音级名；两者一起，才说得清一个音程。比它多一个音的和弦，见 [[glossary:chord]]。

::: en
**An interval** is the distance between two notes, and it takes two rulers to measure it. **Degree** counts how many note names the two ends span (C to E is C–D–E, a third); **size in semitones** counts the half steps between them (C to E is four). The same degree can come in different widths — C–E is a major third (four semitones), D–F a minor third (three) — which is exactly where major and minor intervals come from.

The usual mistake is to count only semitones or only note names. **What the ear hears as a leap is the semitone count; what the score writes is the degree.** You need both to describe one interval. Add one more note and it becomes a [[glossary:chord|chord]].
