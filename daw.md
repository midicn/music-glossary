::: zh
**数字音频工作站（DAW）** 是录音、编辑与混音的软件宿主。它把多轨录音、剪辑、效果、自动化与导出收进一个工程；宿主本身不发声，声音来自话筒、乐器、采样库或软件音源。

工程里通常同时有音频轨与 MIDI 轨：前者存波形，后者存演奏指令，再由虚拟乐器发声。采样率与位深在建立工程时设定，决定录音的带宽与动态余量；缓冲大小则影响监听延迟。

常见误解：把 DAW 当成音源，或以为换一款宿主就会“音质更好”。在相同插件、电平与导出设置下，主流 DAW 的差别主要在 workflow，而不是声音本身。

::: en
A **digital audio workstation (DAW)** is the software host for recording, editing and mixing. It gathers multitrack capture, cutting, effects, automation and export into one project. The host makes no sound of its own: sound comes from microphones, instruments, sample libraries or software instruments.

A project usually holds both audio and MIDI tracks. Audio tracks store waveforms; MIDI tracks store performance instructions that virtual instruments turn into sound. Sample rate and bit depth are chosen when the project is created and set its bandwidth and headroom, while buffer size affects monitoring latency.

The usual mistake is to treat a DAW as a sound source, or to expect a different host to sound better. With the same plug-ins, levels and export settings, mainstream DAWs differ mainly in workflow, not in sound.
