::: zh
**集体管理（collective management）** 是由集体管理组织代表众多权利人统一发放许可、收取并分配版税的制度。它解决「使用者太多、逐一向每个人清权不现实」的问题，覆盖表演、机械复制与录音邻接权等。

集体管理组织与**表演权组织**不完全等同：后者是前者的常见类型，前者还可能管复制、转播与私人复制补偿。常见误解是「集体管理等于版权转让」：权利人仍保留版权，只授予组织管理权。**费率、分配规则与退出机制是评估重点。**

::: en
**Collective management** is a system in which a collective body licenses, collects and distributes royalties on behalf of many rights holders. It solves the problem that **individual clearance is impractical when users are numerous**, covering performance, mechanical reproduction and recording rights.

A collective management organization is not identical to a **performing rights organization**: the latter is a common type, while the former may also handle reproduction, retransmission and private-copying levies. **The usual mistake is to confuse collective management with copyright transfer**: owners keep their rights and grant only management. **Rates, distribution rules and exit terms matter most.**
