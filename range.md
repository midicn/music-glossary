::: zh
**音域（range）**是人声或乐器所能达到、或一段音乐实际使用的最低音到最高音的范围。说明移调乐器的音域时，要注明记谱音或实际音高；描述具体作品时，也要区分作品用到的范围与乐器的全部可用范围。

**常用音区（tessitura）**关注声部主要停留的位置；**音区（register）**还可涉及不同位置的音色与发声方式。两段声部即使最低音、最高音相同，若一段长期停留在高音区，演奏或演唱负担也可能不同。

:::

::: en
**Range** is the span from the lowest to the highest pitch available to a voice or instrument, or used in a musical passage. For a transposing instrument, state whether pitches are written or sounding, and distinguish the range of a work from the instrument’s full available range.

**Tessitura** concerns where a part mainly lies; **register** can also refer to changes in tone colour or production across the range. Two parts may share their highest and lowest notes yet place different demands on the performer if one remains high for much longer.
