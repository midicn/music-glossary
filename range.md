::: zh
**音域（range）** 是一件乐器或人声**从最低到最高能到的范围**：它回答「能到哪儿」，不回答「常待在哪儿」。音域可以是实音，也可以是记谱音（移调乐器要注明）。

它和**常用音区（tessitura）**不同：音域是极限，tessitura 是重心；它和**音区（register）**也不同：音区是音域内部的音色分层。常见误解是「音域越宽越好」——音域宽不等于音乐表现力强；真正决定难度的是 tessitura 与音区转换。

:::

::: en
**Range** is the span **from the lowest to the highest note available** on an instrument or voice: it answers "how far can it go", not "where does it usually sit". It can be sounding pitch or written pitch (transposing instruments must say which).

It differs from **tessitura**: range is the limit, tessitura the centre of gravity; it also differs from **register**: register is the colour layering inside the range. The usual mistake is to think a wider range is better — width is not expressiveness; what decides difficulty is tessitura and register changes.
