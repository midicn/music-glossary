::: zh
**终止权（termination right）** 有两层含义：一是合同约定的**解约权**，在期限届满、违约或条件成就时结束合作；二是部分法域（如美国）版权法赋予作者的**法定终止权**，可在转让多年后收回权利。

常见误解是「签了永久转让就永远拿不回」：法定终止权通常不可预先放弃，但须在**窗口期**内按规定通知行使。合同解约与法定终止是两套机制，不能混用。**通识说明，不构成法律意见。**

::: en
**Termination right** has two senses: a **contractual right to end a deal** on expiry, breach or a stated condition; and, in some jurisdictions such as the United States, a **statutory right of authors** to reclaim rights years after a transfer.

**The usual mistake is to think a perpetual assignment can never be undone**: statutory termination generally cannot be waived in advance, but it must be exercised with the required notice inside a **window period**. Contract termination and statutory termination are separate mechanisms and should not be conflated. **This is general information, not legal advice.**
