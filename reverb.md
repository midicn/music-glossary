::: zh
**混响（reverb）** 模拟声音在空间里的**反射**：直达声之后，墙面、地板与空气把声音不断反射回来，形成一条逐渐衰减的尾巴。它让干声听起来「在一个房间里」，也决定前后距离与空间感。

常见类型有**算法混响**（用延迟网络模拟空间）与**卷积混响**（用真实空间的脉冲响应）。参数常包括衰减时间、预延迟、干湿比与高频吸收。常见误解是把混响当成延迟：延迟是离散的回声，混响是密集的反射；混响越多不等于越高级，太多会把声音推远、变糊。

::: en
**Reverb** simulates the **reflections** of a space: after the direct sound, walls, floor and air send it back in a dense, decaying tail. It puts a dry sound "in a room" and decides distance and depth.

The common types are **algorithmic** (delay networks modelling a space) and **convolution** (a real room's impulse response). Parameters include decay time, pre-delay, dry/wet and high-frequency absorption. The usual mistake is to confuse reverb with delay: delay is discrete echoes, reverb dense reflections; **more reverb is not more sophisticated — too much pushes the sound away and blurs it.**
