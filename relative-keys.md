::: zh
关系调（relative keys）是调号相同、主音不同的大调与小调，例如 C 大调与 a 小调。关系小调的主音在大调主音下方小三度，音阶音基本相同，因此转调时几乎不用改调号。

关系大调与关系小调共享自然音阶，但小调常升高七音甚至六音，形成和声小调或旋律小调；这些变化音正是两调性格的分界。共同和弦很多，转调可以很平滑。

关系调不同于同主音调：前者共享调号，后者共享主音。判断关系调，看调号与主音；判断同主音调，看主音与调式。
:::

::: en
Relative keys are a major and a minor key with the same signature but different tonics, such as C major and A minor. The relative minor sits a minor third below the major tonic, and the two share nearly all their notes, so a modulation between them barely touches the signature.

They share the natural scale, but the minor often raises its seventh and sometimes its sixth to form harmonic or melodic minor; those altered notes mark the change of character. With so many common chords, the modulation can be very smooth.

Relative keys differ from parallel keys: the former share a signature, the latter a tonic. To identify relative keys, compare signature and tonic; to identify parallel keys, compare tonic and mode.
:::
