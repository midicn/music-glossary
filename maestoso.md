::: zh
**庄严地（maestoso）** 是表情兼速度术语：以**宏伟、庄重、有威仪**的气度演奏，常伴随宽广而不慌的速度。它多出现在交响曲、协奏曲与仪式音乐的开头或高潮。

maestoso 提示庄重、宏伟的性格，并不对应唯一拍速。它可与其他速度和力度记号共同出现；应结合乐句、和声节奏及具体作品理解。

:::

::: en
**Maestoso** is both expression and tempo: play with **majesty, stateliness and bearing**, usually at a broad but unhurried speed. It often opens or crowns a symphony, concerto or ceremonial piece.

Maestoso suggests a majestic character, not one prescribed tempo. Read it with any accompanying tempo or dynamic instructions and with the phrasing and harmonic rhythm of the particular work.
