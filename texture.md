::: zh
**织体（texture）**是音乐里「声部如何同时安排」的维度：有多少层声音、它们相距多远、谁在前谁在后、是一起移动还是各自独立。最粗的分法是三种：单声织体只有一条旋律线（可加八度重复）；复调织体有几条各自独立、地位相近的旋律；主调织体则是一条主旋律加伴奏和弦。织体是听觉上最先感到的东西之一，甚至比具体的音高更容易辨认。

织体不是「配器」。配器决定用什么乐器、什么音色，织体决定这些声音怎样叠在一起；同一段音乐换配器，织体可以不变（钢琴曲改成弦乐四重奏仍是主调织体），也会因为乐器分工而改变。织体还会随时间变化：一段音乐可以从单声开始，加入平行声部变成主调，再让内声部独立成复调。**听织体，先问这里有几层、各层在做什么。**

常见误解是把织体当成「音色」或「伴奏型」。音色是声音的质地，织体是声部之间的关系；同一个音色可以写出单声、复调或主调。另一个误解是以为织体一旦确定就固定不变；实际上织体的变化常是音乐推进的主要手段：加厚、减薄、让声部交错或分离，都会改变听者的注意力。**判断织体不要只听好不好听，而要听清每一层是否独立、是否被听见。**
:::

::: en
**Texture** is the dimension of music that concerns how voices are arranged at the same time: how many layers there are, how far apart they lie, which one leads and which follows, and whether they move together or independently. The broad division is threefold: monophonic texture has a single melodic line (possibly doubled at the octave); polyphonic texture has several independent lines of comparable standing; homophonic texture sets one melody against accompanying chords. Texture is among the first things the ear notices, sometimes more readily than the actual pitches.

Texture is not orchestration. Orchestration decides which instruments and colours are used; texture decides how those sounds are stacked. Change the instruments and the texture can stay the same (a piano piece for string quartet can remain homophonic), or it can change because the instruments divide the work differently. Texture also changes over time: a passage may begin monophonically, add parallel voices to become homophonic, then let an inner voice become independent and turn polyphonic. **To hear texture, ask first how many layers there are and what each layer is doing.**

The common misconception is to treat texture as timbre or as a type of accompaniment. Timbre is the colour of the sound; texture is the relationship between voices. One timbre can carry monophony, polyphony or homophony. Another misconception is that texture, once set, stays fixed. In fact changes of texture are a principal way music moves forward: thickening, thinning, crossing or separating voices all redirect the listener's attention. **Do not judge texture only by whether it sounds pleasant; hear whether each layer is independent and whether it can be heard at all.**
