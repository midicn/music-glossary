::: zh
**活跃地（vivo）** 来自拉丁语 vivus，「活的」。它要求音乐有生气、脉搏灵敏、发音有弹性，常作为 allegro 的补语：allegro vivo 比单纯快板更有精神，却不必然更快。

vivo 与 vivace 同源，但用法略有分工：vivace 常独立作速度标记，vivo 更多用来修饰既有的速度或性格。**活跃来自节奏的弹跳与清晰的起音**，不是把节拍器往上推；慢速的 vivo 也可以很活。它靠近 [[glossary:animato]]，区别是 vivo 更强调生命感而非单纯的「动起来」。
:::

::: en
**Vivo** comes from Latin vivus, "alive". It asks for spirit, a responsive pulse and springy articulation, and often qualifies allegro: allegro vivo has more life than a plain allegro without necessarily being faster.

Vivo and vivace share a root but divide the work: vivace often stands alone as a tempo, while vivo more often colours an existing speed or character. **Liveliness comes from rhythmic bounce and clear attack**, not from pushing the metronome; a slow vivo can still be alive. It is close to animato, but stresses life rather than mere motion.
:::
