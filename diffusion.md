::: zh
**扩散（diffusion）** 是把反射声打散到不同方向与时间，让房间保留能量与空间感，却不形成明显的梳状滤波或回声。它和吸声相反：吸声拿走能量，扩散重新分配能量。

常见器件是二维或三维扩散板，刻槽或方块按数学序列排列，作用频段与尺寸相关。多装在后墙、侧墙后段或天花，离听音位要有足够距离，否则听到的是近场散射而不是扩散。

常见误解是墙面粗糙就等于扩散。随机凹凸只能打散高频，真正扩散需要按频段设计；小房间也不宜滥用，先解决低频更实际。
:::

::: en
**Diffusion** scatters reflections across direction and time, keeping acoustic energy and a sense of space while avoiding strong comb filtering or echoes. It is the opposite of absorption: absorption removes energy, diffusion redistributes it.

Typical devices are two- or three-dimensional panels whose wells or blocks follow a mathematical sequence; their size sets the working frequency range. They usually go on the rear wall, the later part of side walls or the ceiling, far enough from the listener to scatter rather than simply reflect nearby.

The usual mistake is thinking any rough surface diffuses. Random bumps scatter only the top end; real diffusion is designed for a frequency range. Small rooms should solve bass first.
:::
