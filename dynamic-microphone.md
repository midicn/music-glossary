::: zh
**动圈麦克风（dynamic microphone）** 把线圈固定在振膜上，声波带动线圈在磁场中运动，从而产生电信号。结构简单、结实，不需要幻象供电，是现场与鼓组的常用选择。

它的振膜与线圈较重，高频细节和瞬态不如电容麦轻盈，但耐高声压、抗摔、对湿度和温度不敏感。许多动圈麦采用心形指向，近讲时低频会因近场效应而增强，可利用也可校正。

常见误解是动圈麦只能录响的东西。优质动圈同样能录人声与吉他音箱，只是需要更干净的前级增益；先确认声源与摆位，再判断它是否合适。
:::

::: en
A **dynamic microphone** attaches a coil to a diaphragm. Sound moves the coil through a magnetic field and induces a signal. The simple, sturdy construction needs no phantom power, which makes it a common choice for live work and drums.

Its diaphragm and coil add mass, so high-frequency detail and transients are less airy than a condenser's, but it tolerates high sound pressure, rough handling and changing humidity. Many models use a cardioid pattern; close work adds low end through proximity effect, which can be used or corrected.

The usual mistake is limiting dynamics to loud sources. Good models record vocals and guitar amps too, though they need clean preamp gain. Check the source and placement before deciding whether one fits.
:::
