::: zh
**和声不协和（harmonic dissonance）** 是**和弦层面**的不协和：不是单个音程的刺耳，而是整个和弦在调性里**不稳定、需要解决**。属七、减七、增六都属于这一类。

它和**音程不协和**不同：一个音程可以协和，放进和弦里却可能不协和；反之亦然。常见误解是「不协和就是难听」——不协和是方向与张力的来源；没有它，和声就没有「回家」的欲望。

::: en
**Harmonic dissonance** is dissonance at the **chord level**: not the harshness of a single interval but the **instability of a whole chord within a key**, asking for resolution. Dominant sevenths, diminished sevenths and augmented sixths belong here.

It differs from **interval dissonance**: an interval may be consonant yet dissonant inside a chord, and the reverse. The usual mistake is to equate dissonance with ugliness — dissonance is the source of direction and tension; without it, harmony has no desire to come home.
