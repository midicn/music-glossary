::: zh
**双音（double-stop）** 是弦乐上**两根弦同时发声**：左手指分别按住两根弦，弓同时接触两条弦拉出两个音。它可以是三度、六度、八度这类协和音程，也可以是二度、七度这样紧张、会「打架」的音程；三根或四根弦同时响时通常写成和弦，实际演奏常改为快速滚奏。

双音让声音变厚，也让问题暴露：**音准是两音之间的关系**，不只是单音准不准；弓要在两根弦之间保持均匀的接触与压力，否则一条弦会发虚。**常见误解是把「先后拉出的两个音」也叫双音**——double-stop 的关键是同时发声；在吉他上它指同时拨两根弦，在钢琴上则通常说「双音」或「八度」，不说 double-stop。
:::

::: en
**A double-stop** sounds **two strings at once**: the left hand stops a note on each string and the bow contacts both, producing two pitches together. The interval may be consonant — a third, sixth or octave — or tense, like a second or seventh that rubs; when three or four strings sound, it is usually written as a chord and played as a quick roll.

The texture thickens, and so do the problems: **intonation is now a relationship between two notes**, and the bow must keep even contact and pressure across both strings or one will fade. **The usual mistake is to call two notes played one after another a double-stop** — the point is simultaneity; on the guitar it means plucking two strings together, while on the piano one speaks of double notes or octaves, not double-stops.
:::
