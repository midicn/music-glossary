::: zh
**声音设计（sound design）** 是为影像、游戏与音乐创造声音的工作：用合成、采样、录音与效果处理，做出画面需要、现实里却不存在的声音——飞船、怪兽、魔法、界面提示。它既包含音色创作，也包含编辑、分层与空间安排。

流程从声音 brief 开始：这个声音要多大多近、什么材质、什么情绪；再用几层素材叠加——低频冲击、中频主体、高频细节——用混响、失真、调制把它粘成一个整体。游戏音频还要考虑交互与随机变化，避免重复。

常见误解是声音设计就是找现成音效库。库是起点，真正的设计在挑选、变形与组合；同一素材经过变速、共振、颗粒化，可以变成另一种东西。

::: en
**Sound design** is the craft of creating sound for picture, games and music: using synthesis, sampling, recording and processing to make what a scene needs but reality does not offer — spaceships, monsters, magic, interface cues. It covers timbre creation, editing, layering and spatial placement.

Work usually starts from a sound brief: how big or close the sound should feel, what material it suggests, what emotion it carries. Layers are stacked — low-end impact, midrange body, high-frequency detail — and glued with reverb, distortion and modulation. Game audio also needs interaction and variation so sounds do not repeat.

The usual mistake is to treat sound design as browsing a sound-effects library. A library is a start; design lies in choosing, transforming and combining, and one source can become something else through pitch shifting, resonance or granular processing.
