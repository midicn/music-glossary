::: zh
**铝带麦克风（ribbon microphone）** 用一条极薄的波纹铝带悬在磁隙中，声波让铝带振动并切割磁力线。铝带本身质量轻，瞬态自然，音色常被形容为温暖、平滑。

它的原生指向多为双向（8 字形），前后拾音、侧面抑制；输出低，需要增益干净且噪声低的前级。铝带怕风、怕摔，强气流可能把它吹变形；老式无源铝带接入幻象电源有风险，有源型号则另当别论。

常见误解是铝带只能录柔和的声音。摆远一点，它很适合弦乐、铜管与房间声；关键在于避免强风与粗暴操作，而不是把它锁在玻璃柜里。
:::

::: en
A **ribbon microphone** suspends a very thin corrugated metal ribbon in a magnetic gap. Sound sets the ribbon moving through the field, and its low mass gives natural transients and a smooth, warm tone.

Most ribbons are naturally bidirectional, picking up front and rear while rejecting the sides. Output is low, so they want a clean, quiet preamp with plenty of gain. The ribbon fears wind and drops; a strong gust can stretch it, and phantom power on older passive models is risky, though active ribbons differ.

The usual mistake is reserving ribbons for soft material. Placed at a little distance they suit strings, brass and room sound. Avoiding wind and rough handling matters more than keeping them locked away.
:::
