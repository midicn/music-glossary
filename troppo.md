::: zh
**过度、太（troppo）** 意为「过多、过分」，本身很少单独作记号，多出现在 ma non troppo（但别过分）与 non troppo（不太）里。它指向越过分寸的那一侧：allegro ma non troppo 仍是快板，只是拒绝把快板推到极限。

troppo 与 [[glossary:non-troppo]] 是一体两面：前者是危险的方向，后者是刹车。**ma non troppo 比 non troppo 语气缓和**，带一点「可以放开，但要有节制」的意思，常见于浪漫派作品。判断是否过度，不能只看速度表：音色变紧、句法被吞、低音失去支撑，都是 troppo 的信号。它与 [[glossary:molto]] 方向相反，一个加码，一个设限；放在 [[glossary:allegro]] 旁边，分寸尤其明显。
:::

::: en
**Troppo** means "too much" and is rarely a marking on its own; it appears in ma non troppo, "but not too much", and non troppo, "not too". It names the side that overshoots: allegro ma non troppo is still an allegro, merely one that refuses the extreme.

Troppo and non troppo are two faces of one idea: the danger and the brake. **Ma non troppo is gentler than non troppo**, carrying a hint of "you may let go, within measure", and is common in Romantic scores. Judge excess by more than the metronome: a tight tone, swallowed phrasing or a collapsed bass all signal troppo. It pulls against molto, one adding, one capping, and the difference shows plainly beside allegro.
:::
