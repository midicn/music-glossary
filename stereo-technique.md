::: zh
**立体声录音技术（stereo technique）** 用两支或多支麦克风，把声源在时间与强度上的差异记录下来，重放时形成左右展开的空间。常见路线有同点式、近同点式与间隔式，差别在于时间差与电平差各占多少。

同点式如 XY 与 M/S 几乎没有时间差，单声道兼容好、声像稳定；间隔式如 A/B 时间差明显，宽度大但中间可能发空。ORTF 介于两者之间。选择要看声源规模、房间反射与最终载体，还要留一条单声道检查。

常见误解是以为麦克风越多立体感越强。多支麦克风会带来相位叠加与梳状滤波，先想清楚要时间差还是电平差，再决定摆法。
:::

::: en
**Stereo technique** uses two or more microphones to capture differences in arrival time and level, so playback opens a left-right space. The main families are coincident, near-coincident and spaced, differing in how much time and level difference they rely on.

Coincident pairs such as XY and mid-side have almost no time difference, giving stable images and good mono compatibility. Spaced pairs such as A/B rely on time difference, offering width but sometimes a hollow centre. ORTF sits between them. Choose by ensemble size, room reflection and final format, and keep a mono check.

The usual mistake is assuming more microphones create more space. Extra mics bring phase summing and comb filtering; decide whether you want time or level differences before placing them.
:::
