## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 低音提琴 |
| 英语 | double bass |
| 意大利语 | contrabbasso |
| 德语 | Kontrabass |
| 法语 | contrebasse |

**低音提琴** 是提琴族里体积最大、音区最低的成员，在管弦乐中常担任和声的根音。它按四度定弦，实际发音比记谱低一个八度；英语 double bass 的「double」通行解释是它常与低音声部作八度齐奏。

**常见混淆**：低音提琴与大提琴都是低音弦乐器，但大提琴按五度定弦、夹在膝间演奏，低音提琴则更大、音区更低。英语口语里的 bass 也常指电贝斯，upright bass 才是这件乐器。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 低音提琴 |
| English | double bass |
| Italian | contrabbasso |
| German | Kontrabass |
| French | contrebasse |

The **double bass** is the largest and lowest member of the violin family, usually carrying the root of the harmony in an orchestra. It is tuned in fourths and sounds an octave below written pitch; the "double" in its English name is commonly explained by its habit of doubling the bass line at the octave.

**Watch out**: the double bass and the cello are both low bowed strings, but the cello is tuned in fifths and held between the knees, while the double bass is larger and lower. In speech, "bass" often means the electric bass; "upright bass" is this instrument.
