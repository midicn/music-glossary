::: zh
**持续地（sostenuto）** 要求把声音**保持住、撑满时值**，常带有宽广、不急于走的意味。作为速度标记，它通常比 Andante 更稳、更宽；作为表情标记，它要求长音不能提前衰减，句尾不能塌。

在钢琴上，sostenuto 还指**中踏板**：踩下时只延长此刻按住的音，之后弹的音不受影响。**常见误解是把 sostenuto 当成「慢」**——它首先要求的是「撑住」，速度可以中等，甚至偏快，只要声音不断、不塌。

::: en
**Sostenuto** asks for the sound to be **held for its full value**, often with a sense of breadth and no hurry. As a tempo mark it is usually steadier and broader than Andante; as an expression mark it demands that long notes do not decay early and phrase endings do not sag.

On the piano, sostenuto also names the **middle pedal**: it sustains only the notes held at the moment of pedalling, leaving later notes unaffected. **The usual mistake is to read sostenuto as "slow"** — its first demand is "hold", and the speed can be moderate or even brisk as long as the sound does not break or sag.
