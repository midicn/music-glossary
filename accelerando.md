::: zh
**渐快（accelerando，缩写 accel.）** 是速度**逐渐加快**的指示：不是突然换档，而是在一段时间内持续加速。它常出现在乐句的末尾、段落转换或高潮之前，用来制造推动力。

渐快要和 **più mosso** 区分：più mosso 是「到达一个更快的新速度」，accelerando 是「持续加速的过程」。**常见误解是把渐快弹成「越弹越急」**——好的渐快有方向、有节制，句尾仍然站得住；如果只是抢，音乐就散了。

::: en
**Accelerando (accel.)** means **gradually getting faster**: not a sudden gear change but a continuous increase over a stretch of music. It often appears at the end of a phrase, at a transition or before a climax, to build drive.

Keep it apart from **più mosso**: più mosso arrives at a new, faster tempo; accelerando is the process of speeding up. **The usual mistake is to turn an accelerando into "rushing"** — a good one has direction and restraint and still stands at the end of the phrase; mere hurry falls apart.
