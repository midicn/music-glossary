## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 巴松管 |
| 英语 | bassoon |
| 意大利语 | fagotto |
| 德语 | Fagott |
| 法语 | basson |

**巴松管** 是低音双簧片木管乐器，管身对折成 U 形，长度虽大却便于持奏，常担任低音与和声的支撑。意大利语 fagotto 字面是「一捆柴」，形容它拆开后的样子；德语 Fagott、法语 basson、英语 bassoon 都指同一件乐器。

**常见混淆**：巴松管不是「大型双簧管」那么简单，它的圆锥管与对折结构带来独特的低音；比它低一个八度的倍低音巴松管（contrabassoon）是另一件乐器。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 巴松管 |
| English | bassoon |
| Italian | fagotto |
| German | Fagott |
| French | basson |

The **bassoon** is the bass double-reed woodwind; its tube is folded back on itself in a U shape, making a long instrument manageable, and it usually supports the bass and harmony. Italian fagotto literally means "a bundle of sticks", describing the jointed tube; German Fagott, French basson and English bassoon name the same instrument.

**Watch out**: the bassoon is not simply a scaled-up oboe — its conical, folded bore gives it a distinct low register; the contrabassoon, an octave lower, is a separate instrument.
