::: zh
**分割节奏（divisive rhythm）**把较大的时间单位逐层等分：全音符分为二分、四分、八分，节拍层级由此建立。西方记谱与小节体系主要靠它组织时间。

分割产生稳定的脉搏和规则重音，但表层仍可用切分、连音与交错重音打破。进行曲、圆舞曲的骨架都是分割性的，听觉上的复杂并不取消底层的等分。

**分割节奏不是「简单节奏」。** 它描述时间如何被划分，不描述表面是否热闹；复节奏的每一层也各自分割。与附加节奏对照，一个向下分，一个向上加。分析时问：单位被分成几份，重音落在哪一层。
:::

::: en
**Divisive rhythm** divides a larger time span into equal smaller units: the whole note into halves, quarters and eighths. The metrical hierarchy of Western notation and the bar is largely built this way.

Division produces a steady pulse and regular accents, yet the surface can still be disturbed by syncopation, tuplets and cross-accents. A march or a waltz is divisive at its base; surface complexity does not cancel the underlying equal division.

**Divisive rhythm is not "simple rhythm".** It describes how time is divided, not how busy the surface is, and each layer of a polyrhythm is divided too. Set against additive rhythm, one divides downwards and the other adds upwards. Ask how many parts the unit is divided into, and which level carries the accent.
:::
