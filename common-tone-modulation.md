::: zh
共同音转调（common-tone modulation）是抓住一个在两调中都存在的音，让它持续或反复，把旧调与新调缝在一起。这个音在旧调里是一个级数，到了新调又变成另一个级数，耳朵跟着它完成转身。

它常与减七和弦或增六和弦合用：共同音保持不变，其他声部半音移动，和弦身份悄然改变。与共同和弦转调不同，这里共享的是一个音，不是一个完整和弦，因此过渡更隐蔽。

判断它仍是转调，要看新主音是否站住。若共同音只是装饰性的持续音，和声没有建立新中心，那就只是经过，不是转调。
:::

::: en
Common-tone modulation seizes a pitch that belongs to both keys and keeps it sounding or repeating, stitching the old key to the new. The note is one scale degree in the old key and another in the new, and the ear follows it through the turn.

It often works with a diminished seventh or augmented sixth: the common tone stays while the other voices move by semitone, and the chord quietly changes identity. Unlike pivot-chord modulation, the shared element is a single note, not a whole chord, so the join is harder to hear.

To call it a modulation, the new tonic must still establish itself. If the common tone is only a decorative pedal and no new centre forms, the music has passed through, not moved.
:::
