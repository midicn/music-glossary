::: zh
**花舌（flutter-tongue）** 是管乐的演奏法：一边吹气，一边让舌头**快速滚动**（像发滚动的「r」），气流被舌头打断，声音就带上一层颤动与粗粝。长笛、竖笛与铜管都能用，20 世纪作曲家尤其偏爱它。

它和 **tremolo** 不同：tremolo 是音与音的重复或交替，花舌是**气流本身的颤动**，音高不变。花舌也不是「抖喉」：铜管的「growl」是同时哼唱造成的粗糙，花舌则来自舌头。两种「粗」，来源不同。

::: en
**Flutter-tonguing** is a wind technique: while blowing, the tongue **rolls rapidly** (like a rolled "r"), breaking the airstream so that the sound gains a flutter and a gritty edge. Flute, recorder and brass can all do it, and twentieth-century composers loved it.

It differs from **tremolo**: tremolo repeats or alternates notes, flutter-tongue **flutters the airstream itself** while the pitch stays put. It is not a throat growl either: the brass "growl" comes from humming while playing, flutter-tongue from the tongue. Two kinds of roughness, two sources.
