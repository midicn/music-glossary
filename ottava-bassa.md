::: zh
**低八度记号（ottava bassa）** 写作 8vb 或 8va bassa，标在谱表下方，表示虚线覆盖的音符低八度演奏。

它主要替低音区省去加线，低音提琴、钢琴左手与管风琴踏板声部常用。虚线结束处即回到记谱音高，必要时写 loco 提醒。

旧谱有时把 8va 写在下方也表示低八度，读谱要看位置与上下文；现代记谱以 8vb 更清楚，也避免与上方的 8va 混淆。
:::

::: en
**Ottava bassa**, written 8vb or 8va bassa, is placed below the staff and tells the player to sound the covered notes an octave lower than written.

Its main job is to remove ledger lines from the low register, so it appears in double bass, piano left hand and organ pedal parts. Where the dotted line ends, the written pitch returns; loco may be added for clarity.

Older scores sometimes put 8va below the staff to mean the same thing, so read the position and the context. Modern practice prefers 8vb, which is unambiguous and cannot be confused with 8va above.
:::
