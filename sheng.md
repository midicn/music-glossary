## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|

| 英语 | sheng |
| 意大利语 | — |

**笙**是中国的簧管乐器，竹管插在笙斗上，管内的簧片在气流中自由振动而发声，属自由簧类。可以吹单音，也可以同时吹几个音构成和声，是民乐吹管组里少数能奏和音的乐器。常见圆笙、方笙与加键笙。

**常见混淆**：笙是自由簧，靠簧片自身振动，不需要唢呐那样的双簧苇哨；二者发声原理不同。通行说法认为，笙是手风琴、口琴等自由簧乐器的远祖。意、德、法没有另立的专名，通常径用拼音 sheng。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 笙 |
| English | sheng |
| Italian | — |
| German | — |
| French | — |

The **sheng** is China's reed mouth organ. Bamboo pipes sit in a wind chest, and a small reed inside each pipe vibrates freely in the airstream — the free-reed principle. It can sound one note or several at once, making it one of the few wind instruments in Chinese ensembles that can play chords. Round, square and keyed forms are common.

**Watch out**: the sheng is a free-reed instrument, driven by the reed's own vibration, and needs no double reed like the suona; the two work on different principles. The usual account makes the sheng a distant ancestor of free-reed instruments such as the accordion and harmonica. Italian, German and French have no established native name and use sheng.
