::: zh
**固定音型（ostinato）**是一段短小的音型——几个音、一个节奏型，或两者结合——在同一音高位置上原样反复，贯穿一段音乐。它可以放在低音（固定低音）、中间声部或高音，可以是旋律、和弦进行或纯节奏。帕赫贝尔《卡农》的低音、拉威尔《波莱罗》的节奏型、流行音乐里的循环贝斯，都是固定音型。**它的力量来自重复**：听者很快抓住它，然后开始注意它上方发生的变化。

固定音型与「主题」不同：主题是要被发展、被回答的完整陈述，固定音型则故意不发展，保持原样，让别的东西在它上面变化。它可以是背景（恰空、帕萨卡利亚的低音），也可以是动力（摇滚鼓组的节奏型），还可以是结构骨架（整首曲子按同一个循环组织）。**固定音型越短、越稳定，上方声部的自由就越容易被听出来。**

常见误解是把固定音型当成「重复的旋律」，以为只要旋律重复就算。固定音型通常原样反复、位置稳定；如果每次重复都移调、加花或改变节奏，那更接近变奏或模进。另一个误解是以为固定音型只属于早期音乐；从巴洛克到极简主义、摇滚与电子舞曲，它都是最常见的组织手段之一。**判断固定音型，听它是否在同一层上持续回来，而不只是听它是否重复。**
:::

::: en
**An ostinato** is a short figure — a few notes, a rhythmic pattern, or both — repeated unchanged at the same pitch level through a passage. It may sit in the bass (a ground bass), an inner voice or the top, and it may be melodic, harmonic or purely rhythmic. The bass of Pachelbel's Canon, the rhythmic pattern of Ravel's Boléro and the looping bass of a pop song are all ostinatos. **Their power comes from repetition**: the ear grasps the figure quickly and then attends to what changes above it.

An ostinato differs from a theme. A theme is a complete statement meant to be developed and answered; an ostinato is deliberately not developed, so that something else can change over it. It can be a background (the bass of a chaconne or passacaglia), a driving force (a rock drum pattern), or a structural frame (a whole piece organised around one loop). **The shorter and more stable the ostinato, the more clearly the freedom of the upper parts can be heard.**

The common misconception is to call any repeated melody an ostinato. An ostinato normally repeats unchanged and keeps its place; if each return is transposed, decorated or rhythmically altered, it is closer to variation or sequence. Another misconception is that ostinato belongs only to early music. From the Baroque to minimalism, rock and electronic dance music, it is one of the most common organising devices. **To identify an ostinato, hear whether it keeps returning on the same layer, not merely whether something repeats.**
