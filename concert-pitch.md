::: zh
**实际音高（concert pitch）** 是实际听到的音高，也是不同移调乐器之间对照的基准。总谱写成实际音高时，所有声部共用一套音高，分析纵向关系最直接。

这个词还有第二层意思：调音标准。今天常用 A4 = 440 Hz，巴洛克乐团可能用 415 Hz，音乐会现场也可能定在 442 Hz。所以实际音高既相对于移调记谱，也相对于历史音高标准。

两个意思常被混为一谈：移调是记谱与发声的差，音高标准是频率的差。排练时说「请按实际音高演奏」，通常指前者，而不是要求改调音。
:::

::: en
**Concert pitch** is the pitch actually heard and the reference against which transposing instruments are compared. In a concert-pitch score every part is written at the same pitch level, which makes vertical relationships easiest to analyse.

The term has a second sense: a tuning standard. A4 = 440 Hz is common today, Baroque bands may use 415 Hz, and concerts sometimes tune to 442 Hz. Concert pitch is thus relative both to transposed notation and to historical standards.

The two senses are often merged. Transposition is a gap between notation and sound; a pitch standard is a gap in frequency. "Play at concert pitch" in rehearsal usually means the former, not a request to retune.
:::
