::: zh
**渐弱（diminuendo，缩写 dim.）** 是音量**逐渐减小**的指示，谱面上也常用一条收拢的「>」线。它常出现在**乐句收尾、回声或情绪退去**的地方，让音乐「落地」。

它和**渐慢**不同：渐弱只改音量，不改速度；两者常一起使用，但是两件事。常见误解是把渐弱弹成「突然消失」——好的渐弱有方向与余韵，声音要一直「在」，只是越来越轻。

::: en
**Diminuendo (dim.)** means **gradually softer**, often drawn as a closing ">" hairpin. It usually closes a phrase, echoes a gesture or lets an emotion recede, letting the music "land".

It differs from **ritardando**: diminuendo changes only volume, not speed; the two often appear together but are different things. **The usual mistake is to let it vanish suddenly** — a good diminuendo has direction and afterglow, the sound stays "there" while growing softer.
