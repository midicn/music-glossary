::: zh
**均衡器（EQ）**调整不同频段的增益，用提升或衰减改变频谱平衡。常见类型有**参量 EQ**（频率、增益、Q 可调）、**图示 EQ**（固定频段推子）与**搁架 EQ**（处理高频或低频的较宽范围）。

提升与衰减都可用于解决问题或塑造音色，并无统一的“只做减法”规则。调整时应结合素材和混音目的，比较时尽量匹配处理前后的响度，以免把整体变响误判为更清晰。

:::

::: en
**An equalizer (EQ)** changes gain across frequency bands, using boosts or cuts to alter spectral balance. Common types include **parametric EQ** (adjustable frequency, gain and Q), **graphic EQ** (fixed-band faders) and **shelving EQ** (broad treatment of high or low frequencies).

Both boosts and cuts can correct problems or shape tone; there is no universal rule requiring only subtractive EQ. Choose settings for the source and mix goal, and match processed and unprocessed loudness as closely as practical when comparing them.
