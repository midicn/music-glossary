::: zh
**均衡器（EQ）** 调整不同**频段**的音量：提升或衰减某一段频率，从而改变音色、清晰度与空间感。常见类型有**参量 EQ**（频率、增益、Q 可调）、**图示 EQ**（一排固定频段推子）与搁架 EQ（只调高/低频段的整体）。

它不是「音质增强器」：EQ 只能改变已有频率的比例，不能凭空造出缺失的信息。常见误解是「每个频段都提升一点会更好听」——那通常只是更响；真正的 EQ 是做减法：先去掉掩蔽与浑浊，再决定要不要补。

:::

::: en
**An equalizer (EQ)** changes the level of **frequency bands**: boost or cut a range to alter tone, clarity and space. Common types are **parametric** (frequency, gain and Q adjustable), **graphic** (a row of fixed-band faders) and shelving (shaping the whole top or bottom).

It is not a "quality enhancer": **EQ changes the balance of what is already there; it cannot invent missing information. The usual mistake is to boost a little everywhere — that usually just makes things louder; real EQ is subtractive**: remove masking and mud first, then decide what to add.
