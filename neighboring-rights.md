::: zh
**邻接权（neighboring rights）** 是**与版权相邻**的一组权利，通常归表演者、录音制作者与广播组织：表演者控制其表演的录制与使用，录音制作者控制录音的复制与传播。它保护**表演与录音**，不是词曲作品本身。

所以一首歌可能同时叠着词曲版权、表演者权与录音制作者权。常见误解是「买了词曲授权就能用原版录音」：使用录音还要处理邻接权。各国名称与保护期不同，签约时要知道自己转让的是哪一层权利。**通识说明，不构成法律意见。**

::: en
**Neighbouring rights** (related rights) sit **next to copyright** and usually belong to performers, record producers and broadcasters: performers control the recording and use of their performances, producers control reproduction and communication of recordings. They protect **performances and recordings**, not the song itself.

One track can therefore carry composition copyright, performers' rights and producers' rights at once. **The usual mistake is to think a composition licence covers the original recording**: using the recording also requires neighbouring-rights clearance. Names and terms vary by country, so know which layer you are assigning. **This is general information, not legal advice.**
