::: zh
**更快（più mosso）** 是「比刚才更快」的指令，通常出现在同一个乐章内部：前面标 Andante，后面写 più mosso，演奏者就要把速度提上去。它比 con moto 更明确——**con moto 是「多一点动感」，più mosso 是「确实更快」。**

提速不是瞬间跳档：好的 più mosso 会让听众感到音乐「自己跑起来」，而不是被突然推了一把。**在合奏里，提速的节点必须由一个人统一，否则各声部会各自加速。**

::: en
**Più mosso** means "faster than before", usually inside the same movement: Andante followed by più mosso tells the player to pick up speed. It is more definite than con moto — **con moto is "a little more drive", più mosso is "actually faster".**

The change is not a gear shift: a good più mosso makes the music seem to start running on its own rather than being pushed. **In an ensemble the moment of acceleration must be agreed by one person, or the parts drift apart.**
