::: zh
**M/S 立体声（mid-side）** 用一支正面朝向声源的「中」麦克风，和一支侧向的 8 字形「侧」麦克风，再把两路信号矩阵成左右声道。左右声道由中、侧两路相加与相减得到，因此侧通道决定宽度。

它的好处是单声道兼容极佳：只取中信号就是完整的单声道，侧信号可随时衰减。后期还能调整中侧比例改变宽度，不必重新摆麦。侧麦克风必须是 8 字形，且与中麦尽量同点；两支麦的极性、时间对齐要事先核对。

常见误解是以为 M/S 只是相位游戏。它依赖真实的侧向拾音，若侧麦指向不对或位置偏离，解码后声像会偏斜甚至抵消。
:::

::: en
**Mid-side** uses a forward-facing mid microphone and a sideways figure-8 side microphone, then matrixes the two signals into left and right. The two output channels are the sum and difference of mid and side, so the side channel sets the width.

Its great advantage is mono compatibility: the mid signal alone is a complete mono picture, and the side can be faded at will. Width can also be changed later by adjusting the ratio, without moving microphones. The side mic must be figure-8 and should sit as close to the mid as possible; check polarity and time alignment first.

The usual mistake is treating mid-side as a phase trick. It depends on real sideways pickup; a wrongly aimed or displaced side mic skews or even cancels the decoded image.
:::
