## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 萨克斯管 |
| 英语 | saxophone |
| 意大利语 | sassofono |
| 德语 | Saxophon |
| 法语 | saxophone |

**萨克斯管** 是单簧片木管乐器，管体虽以黄铜制成，却靠簧片振动发声，因此归入木管而非铜管。它由阿道夫·萨克斯在十九世纪中叶创制，常见高音、中音、次中音与上低音四种；意大利语 sassofono、德语 Saxophon、法语 saxophone 均用其姓氏。

**常见混淆**：看到铜质管身就把它当铜管乐器是常见误会；萨克斯管与单簧管都用单簧片，但前者圆锥管、后者圆柱管，音色与超吹音程也不同。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 萨克斯管 |
| English | saxophone |
| Italian | sassofono |
| German | Saxophon |
| French | saxophone |

The **saxophone** is a single-reed woodwind: although its body is made of brass, the reed generates the sound, so it belongs with the woodwinds rather than the brass. Adolphe Sax devised it in the mid-nineteenth century; the soprano, alto, tenor and baritone are the common members, and Italian sassofono, German Saxophon and French saxophone all use his name.

**Watch out**: a brass body tempts listeners to call it brass; the saxophone and clarinet both use a single reed, but the sax has a conical bore and the clarinet a cylindrical one, so their tone and overblown intervals differ.
