::: zh
**逃音（escape tone）** 是**级进进入、跳进离开**的非和弦音，通常还**反向折回**：走一步过去，跳一步回来，像探出头又缩回。它常出现在弱拍，是旋律里最轻巧的装饰之一。

它和**换音（cambiata）**方向相反：cambiata 是跳进进入、级进离开，逃音是级进进入、跳进离开。常见误解是把两者当成同一种——进出方式决定听感；逃音更像「试探」，换音更像「冲出去再收」。

::: en
**An escape tone** is a non-chord tone **entered by step and left by leap**, usually turning back: one step out, one leap home, like a head poking out and withdrawing. It often falls on a weak beat and is one of melody's lightest ornaments.

It is the mirror of a **cambiata**: a cambiata is **entered by leap and left by step**, an escape tone entered by step and left by leap. The usual mistake is to treat them as one — the entry and exit decide the feel; an escape tone tests, a cambiata rushes out and pulls back.
