::: zh
**总线（bus）** 是**多路信号汇合**的一条通道：把鼓、贝斯、人声等分别送到不同的 bus，再在 bus 上统一做 EQ、压缩或音量控制。它可以是**编组总线**（drums bus、vocal bus），也可以是**效果发送总线**（reverb bus、delay bus）。

它和「轨道」不同：轨道承载单个声音，总线承载一组声音的合计。常见误解是把总线当成「多出来的一轨」——总线的意义是共享处理与整体控制；在总线上压一下，等于同时压了这一组里的所有声音。

::: en
**A bus** is a channel where **several signals are summed**: drums, bass and vocals can each be sent to their own bus for shared EQ, compression or level control. It can be a **group bus** (drums, vocals) or an **effects send bus** (reverb, delay).

It differs from a track: a track carries one sound, a bus carries the sum of a group. The usual mistake is to treat a bus as "one more track" — the point is shared processing and overall control; compressing the bus compresses every sound in that group at once.
