::: zh
**持续音（pedal point）**是一个长时间保持或反复的音，通常落在低音，像踏板踩住不放。它不随上方的和声变化而移动，因此会和经过的和弦产生协和或冲突：在属持续音上，上方可以走出一连串离调和弦，最后仍落回属和弦并解决到主；在主持续音上，上方可以短暂地游走，再落回主。**持续音是「以不变应万变」的手法：低音不动，和声在上面走。**

持续音不限于低音。它也可以出现在高音或中间声部（高音持续音、内声部持续音），只是低音最常见，也最容易听出来。它可以由管风琴的脚踏键、低音提琴的空弦、定音鼓的滚奏或合成器的长音实现。「踏板」一词原指管风琴的脚键盘，后来才引申为任何声部上的持续音。持续音常出现在曲子的开头、结尾或段落的交接处，用来积累张力或确认调性。

常见误解是把持续音理解成「任何长音」。一个长音如果只是时值长、但和声随之改变，它仍是普通的长音；**持续音的关键是它在和声变化中保持同一音高**，成为上方声部的参照。另一个误解是以为持续音必须绝对静止；它也可以由滚奏或快速反复构成，只要音高不变。听持续音时，注意低音是否不肯走，以及上方和声如何围绕它制造紧张与解决。
:::

::: en
**A pedal point** is a note held or repeated for a long stretch, usually in the bass, like a foot holding one organ pedal down. It does not move with the harmony above, so it forms consonances or clashes with the chords that pass over it: over a dominant pedal the upper parts may wander through remote harmonies and still resolve through the dominant into the tonic; over a tonic pedal they may stray briefly and return home. **A pedal point is the art of staying put while everything else moves**: the bass stands still and the harmony travels above it.

A pedal need not be in the bass. It can appear in the top voice or an inner voice (an inverted or inner pedal), but the bass is the most common and the easiest to hear. It may be played by an organ pedal, an open string on a double bass, a timpani roll or a sustained synth note. The term comes from the organ pedalboard, and only later came to mean a sustained note in any voice. Pedal points often mark openings, endings or the joints between sections, where they build tension or confirm a key.

The common misconception is to call any long note a pedal point. A long note whose harmony changes with it is still an ordinary long note; **the defining point is that the pedal keeps the same pitch while the harmony changes**, becoming a fixed reference for the parts above. Another misconception is that a pedal must be completely static. It can also be sustained by a roll or fast repetition, as long as the pitch stays the same. When listening, notice whether the bass refuses to move, and how the harmony above builds tension and release around it.
