::: zh
**渐慢放宽（rallentando，缩写 rall.）** 是速度**逐渐放慢**的指示，和 ritardando 接近。很多乐谱把两者当同义词；若要说细微差别，rallentando 更常带**「放宽、舒展」**的意味——不只是慢下来，而是让句子变宽。

**常见误解是把它当成「拖」**：放宽不等于失去张力，长音仍要撑住，低音仍要走。判断 rallentando 是否成立，听的是「宽」有没有出来，而不是速度掉了多少。

::: en
**Rallentando (rall.)** means **gradually getting slower**, close to ritardando. Many scores treat the two as synonyms; if there is a nuance, rallentando more often carries a sense of **broadening** — not merely slowing, but letting the phrase widen.

**The usual mistake is to turn it into dragging**: broadening is not losing tension; long notes must still be held and the bass must still move. **Judge a rallentando by whether the breadth arrives, not by how much speed is lost.**
