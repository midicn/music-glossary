::: zh
**字画（word painting）** 是词画的一种更具体的形态：用音乐**直接模仿单个词的含义**——「笑」用快速上行，「哭」用下行半音，「飞翔」用高音与快速音型。它在文艺复兴牧歌与巴洛克声乐里最典型。

它和**词画（text-painting）**的关系是「具体与一般」：word painting 更强调**逐词模仿**，text-painting 可以更自由地处理整句情绪。**常见误解是「字画很幼稚」**——用得好，它能在瞬间把词义变成身体感受；**用滥了，才变成猜谜。**

::: en
**Word painting** is a more specific kind of text-painting: music that **directly mimics the meaning of a single word** — laughter runs upward, weeping falls in semitones, flight takes a high register and rapid figures. It is most typical of the Renaissance madrigal and Baroque vocal music.

Its relation to **text-painting** is specific to general: word painting stresses **word-by-word mimicry**, while text-painting can shape the emotion of a whole phrase more freely. **The usual mistake is to dismiss word painting as naive** — used well, it turns meaning into bodily sensation in an instant; **overused, it becomes a puzzle.**
