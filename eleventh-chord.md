::: zh
十一和弦（eleventh chord）是在九和弦上再叠三度，得到十一音。自然十一度与三音只差半音，叠在大三和弦上会撞出尖锐的小九度，因此常升高为升十一音，或省去三音。

小十一与属十一较常见。小十一的小三度与十一度相距大九度，冲突较缓；属十一常省去三音，听起来接近挂留和弦。十一音是否出现要看具体 voicing，不能只凭和弦符号。

它和挂留和弦的差别在七音与九音：挂留只替换一个和弦音，十一和弦则保留七音、九音，把十一音作为延伸。加十一音又是另一回事，后者没有七音与九音。
:::

::: en
An eleventh chord stacks one more third above a ninth chord. The natural eleventh lies a semitone from the third, and over a major triad the two clash as a minor ninth; players therefore raise it to a sharp eleventh or omit the third.

Minor and dominant elevenths are the common forms. In a minor eleventh the minor third and the eleventh are a major ninth apart, so the friction is gentler; a dominant eleventh often drops the third and sounds close to a suspension. Whether the eleventh sounds depends on the voicing, not on the symbol alone.

It differs from a suspended chord in keeping the seventh and ninth: a suspension replaces one chord tone, while an eleventh chord extends the stack. An added eleventh is different again, since it has neither seventh nor ninth.
:::
