::: zh
**前置放大器（preamp）** 把麦克风或乐器的**微弱信号**放大到线路电平，好让后面的转换器或处理器工作。它不仅提供增益，也会带来**音色染色**：变压器、电子管、晶体管各有性格。

它和**DI 盒**不同：DI 主要做阻抗与平衡转换，话放负责放大；它和功率放大器也不同：话放放大的是电压信号，不是驱动音箱。常见误解是「话放越贵越好」——匹配与增益结构更重要；话放的第一任务是干净，第二才是味道。

::: en
**A preamp** brings a **faint microphone or instrument signal** up to line level so converters or processors can work. It supplies gain and also **colour**: transformers, tubes and transistors each have a character.

It differs from a **DI box**: a DI handles impedance and balancing, a preamp amplifies; it differs from a power amp too: a preamp raises voltage, not speaker drive. The usual mistake is to assume the most expensive preamp is best — matching and gain structure matter more; the first job is cleanliness, the second flavour.
