::: zh
**柔美、甜美地（dolce）**是音乐中的表情记号。

它常涉及起音、音色和乐句的连贯。不同乐器可以采用不同方法，不能把‘更慢触键’或‘更多踏板’当作通用规则；也要保留谱面标出的力度与声部平衡。

:::

::: en
**Dolce means sweetly or gently.** It is an expression marking in music.

It often concerns attack, colour and continuity of phrase. Different instruments require different means: slower key motion or extra pedal is not a universal recipe. Preserve written dynamics and balance between parts.
