::: zh
**同步权（sync license）** 是**把音乐与画面同步使用**的权利：电影、电视剧、广告、游戏、短视频里出现一段音乐，都需要同步授权。它通常要**两层**：词曲的同步权 + 录音的同步权；两层可能属于不同的人，必须分别谈。

它和机械复制权不同：机械复制权管「录成音频」，同步权管「音画结合」。**常见误解是「买了版权库的曲子就能随便用」**——版权库给你的通常是一份限定用途的授权，平台、地域、期限、是否可商用都写在合同里。同步权很少是法定费率，几乎都是逐次谈判。

::: en
**A sync licence** is the right to **pair music with picture**: a film, TV series, advert, game or short video that uses a piece of music needs sync clearance. It usually takes **two layers**: sync for the composition and sync for the master; the two may belong to different owners and must be negotiated separately.

It differs from mechanical: mechanical covers recording to audio, sync covers music with picture. **The usual mistake is to think a production-library track can be used for anything** — a library usually grants a **limited** licence, with platform, territory, term and commercial use written into the contract. Sync is rarely a statutory rate; it is almost always negotiated case by case.
