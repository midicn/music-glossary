::: zh
**斜向进行（oblique motion）** 是一个声部**保持不动**、另一个声部移动。它制造的是**「一个锚点 + 一条移动的线」**：持续音、固定低音、长音铺底，都是斜向进行的常见形态。

它和反向、同向都不同：斜向只有一个声部在动。**常见误解是「不动的声部就是偷懒」**——保持音恰恰是控制和声重力的手段；斜向进行让移动的声部更容易被听见。

::: en
**Oblique motion** keeps **one voice still** while the other moves. It creates **"an anchor plus a moving line"**: a pedal, a ground bass or a sustained note under a melody are common forms of oblique motion.

It differs from contrary and similar motion: only one part moves. **The usual mistake is to think the still voice is lazy** — holding a note is precisely how harmonic gravity is controlled; oblique motion makes the moving line easier to hear.
