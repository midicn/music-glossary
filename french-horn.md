## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 圆号 |
| 英语 | French horn |
| 意大利语 | corno |
| 德语 | Horn |
| 法语 | cor |

**圆号** 是铜管乐器，管身盘成圆圈，用左手操作三个或四个阀键，右手在喇叭口内调节音色与音准。英语称 French horn，但现代管弦圆号主要经德国、奥地利工匠改良，德语只叫 Horn（森林号 Waldhorn 为其前身），「French」是英语里的旧称。

**常见混淆**：French horn 并非法国所产；而 English horn（英国管）也不是号，而是 F 调中音双簧管。两个带国名的名字都容易误导。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 圆号 |
| English | French horn |
| Italian | corno |
| German | Horn |
| French | cor |

The **French horn** is a brass instrument whose tubing is coiled into a circle; the left hand works three or four valves while the right hand inside the bell adjusts tone and intonation. English calls it French horn, but the modern orchestral horn was largely developed by German and Austrian makers, and German simply says Horn (its ancestor the Waldhorn, "forest horn"); "French" is an English convention.

**Watch out**: the French horn is not French, and the English horn is not a horn at all but an alto oboe in F. Both national labels mislead.
