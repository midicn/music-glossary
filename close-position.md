::: zh
**密集排列（close position）** 是把和弦的各音**尽量挤在窄音区**：高音与内声部之间不超过一个八度，声音集中、厚实、有压迫感。它在钢琴中音区、合唱与铜管和声里非常常见。

它和**开放排列**相对：密集排列让和弦的**色彩融合**，开放排列让每个音更独立。常见误解是「密集排列更简单」——音挤在一起时，稍微一个错音或声部交错就会很明显，控制要求更高。

::: en
**Close position** packs a chord's notes into a **narrow register**: the top voice and the inner parts lie within an octave, giving a focused, thick, pressing sound. It is common in the piano's middle register, in choral writing and in brass harmony.

It contrasts with **open position**: close voicing **blends the chord's colour**, open voicing makes each note more independent. The usual mistake is to think close position is easier — when notes are packed together, one wrong note or a voice crossing is immediately audible, so control matters more.
