::: zh
**辅助发送（aux send）** 是通道上可调电平的分路：旋钮决定送多少信号进入辅助总线，再由返回混回。它让一条轨道同时进入主输出、混响、延迟与监听。

辅助发送通常成组出现：推子前用于舞台或耳机监听，推子后用于效果；多个通道送到同一条 aux，就共享效果器与空间感。

常见误解是把 aux send 当成主输出。辅助总线的声音仍要经返回才进入混音；只开 aux 不开返回，等于送进没有出口的支路，监听可参 [[glossary:monitoring]]。
:::

::: en
**An aux send** is a level-controlled tap on a channel: a knob decides how much signal enters an auxiliary bus, which then returns to the mix. It lets one track feed several destinations at once, such as the main output, a reverb, a delay and a cue mix.

Aux sends usually come in groups. Pre-fader sends feed stage or headphone monitoring; post-fader sends feed effects. When several channels feed the same aux, they share one processor and one sense of space.

The usual mistake is to treat an aux send as an alternative main output. The aux bus still has to come back through a return; sending without returning puts the signal on a branch with no exit.
:::
