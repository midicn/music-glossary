::: zh
**进行曲风（marziale）** 是表情术语：像**军队行进**一样，节奏稳定、重拍清楚、音色有金属感。它常出现在二拍子或四拍子的段落，铜管与打击乐尤为典型。

它和 **maestoso** 不同：maestoso 是庄重，marziale 是**行进**；marziale 也不等于「快」——进行曲通常在中速。常见误解是把 marziale 弹成「越响越像」——威武来自稳定的步伐与重音，不是音量。

::: en
**Marziale** is an expression mark: like a **military march** — steady rhythm, clear downbeats, a metallic edge. It often appears in duple or quadruple time, with brass and percussion as the typical colour.

It differs from **maestoso**: maestoso is stately, marziale is **marching**; marziale is not "fast" either — marches usually sit at a moderate pace. The usual mistake is to play it louder to sound more martial — the authority comes from a steady step and accent, not volume.
