**唱片公司（record label）** 是签约艺人、出资制作并推广录音的公司。它的核心资产通常是**母带**：合约往往约定录音版权归公司，艺人按销售或播放领取**艺人版税**。它不等于出版商——唱片公司管录音，出版商管词曲。

常见误解是「签约就等于公司包办一切」：预付、录音与宣传费用多从艺人版税中扣回，回本前艺人可能拿不到额外分成。厂牌服务与**360 合约**又分别改变了母带归属与收入范围。看合约先问：母带归谁、版税率多少、公司负责哪些环节。

::: en
**A record label** signs artists, funds recordings and markets them. Its core asset is usually the **master**: contracts often assign the recording copyright to the label, while the artist earns an **artist royalty** on sales or streams. It is not a publisher — the label handles recordings, the publisher handles songs.

**The usual mistake is to think a deal means the company pays for everything**: advances, recording and marketing costs are typically recovered from the artist's royalties, so little may arrive until recoupment. Label-services deals and 360 deals have changed master ownership and income scope. Ask who owns the master, what the rate is, and what the label actually does.
