::: zh
**和声节奏（harmonic rhythm）**指和弦更换的速率与位置，它可以与旋律节奏、织体节奏完全不同。同一段旋律，每小节换一个和弦与每拍换一个和弦，紧张度和呼吸感会相差很远。

加快和声节奏常把音乐推向终止或高潮，放慢则常表示到达、展开或悬停。终止处的四六与属和弦往往持续更久，让和声节奏放慢、解决更有分量。分析时应标出每个和弦的起点，而不只是列出和弦名称。

**和声节奏不是和弦的时值总和**，也不是鼓点节奏；它问的是「多久换一次和声」。一个和弦持续多小节可以形成极慢的和声节奏，频繁换和弦也未必热闹，可能只是经过性写法。
:::

::: en
**Harmonic rhythm** is the rate and placement at which chords change. It can be independent of melodic and textural rhythm: the same tune with one chord per bar or per beat feels completely different in tension and breath.

A faster harmonic rhythm often drives towards a cadence or climax, while a slower one suggests arrival, development or suspension. At a cadence the six-four and the dominant are often held longer so that the resolution carries weight. Analysis should mark where each chord begins, not merely list them.

**Harmonic rhythm is not the sum of chord durations**, nor is it the drum pattern; it asks how often the harmony changes. A single chord held for several bars can create an extremely slow harmonic rhythm, and frequent chord changes are not necessarily exciting — they may be merely passing.
:::
