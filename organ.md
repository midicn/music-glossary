## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 管风琴 |
| 英语 | organ |
| 意大利语 | organo |
| 德语 | Orgel |
| 法语 | orgue |

**管风琴** 是靠气流使音管发声的键盘乐器，规模从家用小琴到教堂巨构不等。英语 **organ** 来自希腊语 organon（工具、器官），意大利语 **organo**、德语 **Orgel**、法语 **orgue** 同出一源；中文「管风琴」点明「以管发声」，与簧风琴相区别。

**常见混淆**：管风琴与簧风琴（harmonium）都带键盘，常配踏板，但管风琴用气流激发音管，簧风琴用自由簧片；前者通常固定安装，后者便于携带。中文「风琴」一词笼统，容易把两者混为一谈。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 管风琴 |
| English | organ |
| Italian | organo |
| German | Orgel |
| French | orgue |

The **organ** is a keyboard instrument that makes sound by driving air through pipes, ranging from small house instruments to cathedral giants. English organ comes from Greek organon (tool, organ); Italian organo, German Orgel and French orgue share the root. The Chinese 管风琴 names the pipes and distinguishes it from the reed organ.

**Watch out**: organ and harmonium both have keyboards and often pedals, but the organ drives air through pipes while the harmonium uses free reeds; the former is usually fixed, the latter portable. The loose Chinese term 风琴 easily blurs the two.
