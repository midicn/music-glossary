::: zh
**上行挂留（retardation）** 是**向上解决**的挂留音：它同样经历准备—挂留—解决，但最后不是下行，而是**上行**到目标音。它比下行挂留少见，因此更有「提起」的感觉。

它和**挂留音（suspension）**通常指下行解决，retardation 是它的上行变体。常见误解是把两者混为一谈——方向不同，重力与情绪就不同；上行挂留更像「抬起」，下行挂留更像「落下」。

:::

::: en
**A retardation** is a suspension that resolves **upward**: it passes through preparation–suspension–resolution, but the final step rises to the target instead of falling. It is rarer than the descending suspension and feels more like a lift.

It is the rising variant of the **suspension**, which usually resolves down. **The usual mistake is to treat them as the same** — direction changes weight and feeling; a retardation lifts, a suspension falls.
