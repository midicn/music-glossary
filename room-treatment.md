::: zh
**房间声学处理（room treatment）** 是用吸声、扩散与低频控制改变房间的声学反应，让监听位置听到更接近录音本身的声音。它处理的是房间内的反射与共振，不是隔音。

顺序通常是先摆音箱与听音位，再处理第一反射点、后墙与墙角：中高频用吸声控制早期反射，后墙用扩散保留空间感，墙角放低频陷阱。对称布置比堆材料更重要；处理后要用测量与试听复核。

常见误解是把房间贴满吸音棉。过度吸声会让声音发干、低频更突出；处理不足则混响与梳状滤波干扰判断。
:::

::: en
**Room treatment** changes how a room responds acoustically, using absorption, diffusion and low-frequency control so the listening position hears closer to the recording itself. It addresses reflections and resonances inside the room, not sound isolation.

The order is usually speakers and listening position first, then the first reflection points, rear wall and corners: absorption for early reflections, diffusion on the rear wall to keep some space, bass traps in corners. Symmetry matters more than piling up material, and measurement plus listening should confirm the result.

The usual mistake is covering every surface with foam. Too much absorption dries the sound and can make bass seem stronger; too little leaves reverb and comb filtering in the way of judgement.
:::
