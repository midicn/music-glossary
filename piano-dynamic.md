::: zh
**弱（piano，缩写 p）** 是力度记号，表示**轻、弱**，与 forte（强）相对。**p 不是固定的分贝数**：室内乐的 p 与交响乐的 p 完全不在一个声压级；同一件乐器在不同音区，p 的实际响度也不同。

它也不等于「虚」：好的 piano 仍然有**核心与投射**，只是音量收住。常见误解是把 p 当成「越轻越好」——轻到失去音色，音乐就散了；力度是比例，不是绝对值。

:::

::: en
**Piano (p)** is the dynamic mark for **soft**, paired with forte. **P is not a fixed decibel level**: the p of chamber music and the p of a symphony sit at completely different sound pressures, and the same instrument's p differs between registers.

It is not "weak" either: a good piano still has **core and projection**, only at a lower volume. The usual mistake is to make p as soft as possible — softness that loses colour falls apart; dynamics are proportions, not absolutes.
