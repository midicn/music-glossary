::: zh
**乐段（period）**通常由两个乐句组成：前句（antecedent）提出一个未完成的说法，后句（consequent）把它接住并收束。前句常停在属和弦上，形成半终止「悬着」的感觉；后句回到主调，用正格终止落地。两句的旋律往往同头异尾：开头相同，结尾不同，像一问一答。这是古典时期最常见、也最容易被听出来的句法单元。

乐段不是「两个乐句的统称」。真正的乐段要求后句对前句作出回应：材料上有呼应，和声上有收束，长度上常保持平衡。若两句各自独立、互不呼应，那只是并列的乐句，不构成乐段。乐段也可以扩展：前句之后插入延续，后句之前加扩充，或把后句改写成更长的收束。**在奏鸣曲式里，乐段是主题群的基本构件。**

常见误解是把乐段和乐句混为一谈，或者以为只要有两个乐句就是乐段。乐句是单句，乐段是句子之间的关系；两句之间没有问答、没有和声上的呼应，就只是接连出现。另一个误解是以为乐段必须严格对称；实际上作曲家常用不平衡的句长、变化的终止式或扩充来避免方正，让问答更有张力。**听乐段时，重点听第二句如何回答第一句，而不只是听它停在哪里。**
:::

::: en
**A period** is usually built from two phrases: an antecedent proposes something unfinished, and a consequent takes it up and closes it. The antecedent often stops on the dominant, forming a half cadence that leaves the ear suspended; the consequent returns to the tonic and lands on an authentic cadence. The two phrases frequently share a beginning and differ at the end, like a question and its answer. This is the most common and most audible phrase structure in Classical music.

A period is not simply "two phrases". A real period requires the second phrase to answer the first: the material must correspond, the harmony must close, and the lengths are often balanced. Two independent phrases that do not respond to each other are merely successive phrases, not a period. A period can also be expanded: a continuation may be inserted after the antecedent, the consequent may be extended, or the closing may be stretched. **In sonata form the period is a basic building block of the thematic group.**

The common misconception is to confuse period with phrase, or to assume that any two phrases make a period. A phrase is one sentence; a period is a relationship between sentences. Without answer, without harmonic response, there is only succession. Another misconception is that a period must be strictly symmetrical. Composers often use uneven lengths, varied cadences or expansion to avoid squareness and sharpen the question-and-answer tension. **When hearing a period, listen to how the second phrase answers the first, not merely where it stops.**
