::: zh
**有力地（con forza）** 字面「带着力量」，forza 源自拉丁语 fortis「强」。它要求**结实的声音与明确的走向**：起音有芯，低音托得住，句子有推进感。

它不等于响。**常见误解是把 con forza 当成一路砸下去**：音量有了，音乐反而变钝；力量来自支撑与共鸣，不是手臂的蛮力。它常与 con brio、energico 相邻，但更强调**质感**。

在戏剧性段落里它常是**局部标记**：几小节就够，不必整段维持。可与 [[glossary:con-brio]]、[[glossary:energico]] 对读。
:::

::: en
**Con forza** literally means "with force"; forza comes from Latin fortis, "strong". It asks for **firm sound and a clear direction**: an onset with a core, a bass that carries, phrases that push forward.

It does not mean loud. **The usual mistake is to read it as banging all the way through** — the volume arrives and the music goes blunt; force should come from support and resonance, not from arm strength. It often appears near con brio and energico, but con forza cares more about **substance** than about brilliance.

It tends to be a local mark, placed at a dramatic turn and needed for a few bars rather than a whole section. Compare con brio and energico to separate brilliance from energy.
:::
