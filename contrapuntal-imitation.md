::: zh
**模仿（imitation）**是一个声部先说出短乐思，另一个声部随后在另一音高上照样接住，可相隔几拍，也可紧接重叠。严格模仿保持音程与节奏，自由模仿只取轮廓；按固定音程与时间距离贯穿全曲的严格模仿，就是卡农。

模仿是复调写作的发动机：赋格的主题与答题、经文歌的声部进入、巴赫创意曲的两声部追逐，都靠它建立。进入越密，重叠成**紧接**（stretto），张力越大；进入时改变音程或节奏，则成为变形模仿。

**模仿与模进不同：模进是一个声部自己往上或往下搬，模仿是另一个声部把话接过去。** 判断时听同一轮廓是否换了声部；若始终在同一声部，只是模进或重复。
:::

::: en
**Imitation** happens when one voice states a short idea and another takes it up at a new pitch, after a delay or overlapping. Strict imitation keeps the intervals and rhythm; free imitation keeps only the outline. When it runs through a piece at fixed interval and distance, it becomes a canon.

Imitation is the engine of polyphony: the subject and answer of a fugue, the entries of a motet, and the chasing voices of a Bach invention depend on it. Closer entries overlap in **stretto** and raise the tension; alter the intervals or rhythm at entry and the imitation is transformed.

**Imitation differs from sequence: a sequence moves one voice up or down, while imitation hands the idea to another voice.** Listen for the outline changing voices; if it stays in one part, it is sequence, not imitation.
:::
