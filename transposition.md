::: zh
**移调（transposition）** 是把整段音乐按同一音程搬到另一调，音程关系与旋律轮廓不变，只有绝对音高改变。

记谱上有两种用法：为移调乐器写作时，谱面音高与实音相差固定音程；把总谱改为实际音高，或把实际音高谱改为移调谱，也靠移调完成。

移调不是转调。转调发生在作品内部，改变调性中心；移调整段搬家。移调时调号、临时记号与音域都要重算，只改调号必然出错。
:::

::: en
**Transposition** moves a whole passage by the same interval into another key. Intervals and melodic shape stay the same; only absolute pitch changes.

Notation uses it in two ways. Music for a transposing instrument is written a fixed interval from sounding pitch, and converting a score to concert pitch, or the reverse, is also transposition.

Transposition is not modulation. Modulation changes the tonal centre inside a work; transposition moves the whole thing. Key signature, accidentals and range all have to be recalculated, and changing the signature alone will produce errors.
:::
