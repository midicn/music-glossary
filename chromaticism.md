::: zh
**半音体系（chromaticism）** 是使用**调外音**：在一个七声音阶之外，加入升或降的音，让和声变密、旋律变滑、调性变不安。它可以是装饰性的经过音，也可以是结构性的半音和声——19 世纪的和声扩张，很大程度上就是半音体系不断加深。

它和**无调性**不同：半音体系仍然可以有一个中心，只是把中心的引力拉松；无调性则取消中心。**常见误解是把「有半音」当成「无调性」**——半音是材料，调性是关系；材料变了，关系不一定消失。

::: en
**Chromaticism** is the use of **notes outside the scale**: adding sharps or flats to a seven-note scale makes harmony denser, melody slippier and tonality less settled. It can be a decorative passing tone or a structural chromatic harmony — the nineteenth-century expansion of harmony is largely chromaticism deepening.

It differs from **atonality**: chromaticism can still have a centre, only with its gravity loosened; atonality removes the centre. **The usual mistake is to hear "chromatic" as "atonal"** — chromaticism is material, tonality is relationship; change the material and the relationship need not disappear.
