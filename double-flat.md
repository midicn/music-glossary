::: zh
**重降号（double flat）** 由两个降号组合成一个记号，把音降低两个半音，也就是一个全音：重降 B 等于 A，重降 E 等于 D。

它出现在降号调与变化和声里：G♭ 大调的降七级是 F♭，再降一次就成重降 F；某些增六和弦与减七和弦的拼写也会用到重降。写重降是为了让音级关系清楚，而不是故意为难读谱。

别把重降当成两个降号分别生效：它是一个记号、一次降低全音，且只作用于本小节同一音高。演奏时若只盯着琴键，容易把它与等音的另一个音名混为一谈，应先认音级再找键。
:::

::: en
A **double flat** is a single sign built from two flat signs; it lowers a note by two semitones, a whole tone: B double flat equals A, E double flat equals D.

It appears in flat keys and chromatic harmony. The lowered seventh of G♭ major is F♭, and flattening it again gives F double flat; some augmented-sixth and diminished-seventh spellings also use double flats. The sign keeps degrees legible rather than making the score harder on purpose.

Do not treat it as two flats acting separately. It is one sign, one whole-tone lowering, valid for that pitch for the rest of the bar. Players who watch only the keyboard confuse it with its enharmonic twin, so read the degree before finding the key.
:::
