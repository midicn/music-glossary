::: zh
**反复记号**是两条竖线加两个点（𝄆 … 𝄇），表示「把这两点之间的段落再来一遍」。如果没有开头的反复记号，就从头反复；结尾常配**第一结尾与第二结尾**：第一遍走第一结尾，第二遍跳过它走第二结尾。

反复记号通常指定一个段落重奏，**D.C.** 则要求从全曲开头重新开始。古典奏鸣曲式的呈示部常标反复；是否改变装饰、力度或其他细节，要看作品风格、谱面和演奏惯例。

::: en
**A repeat sign** is two barlines with two dots (𝄆 … 𝄇): play the passage between the dots once more. Without an opening sign, repeat from the beginning; the end often pairs with **first and second endings** — take the first ending on the first pass, skip it and take the second on the repeat.

A repeat sign normally repeats a specified passage, whereas **D.C.** directs a return to the start of the piece. Classical sonata expositions often have repeats. Changes of ornament, dynamics or other details depend on the work, score and performance practice.
