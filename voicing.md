::: zh
**声部排列（voicing）** 是**同一个和弦的音怎样分配**：哪些音放在低音、哪些放在内声部、哪些放在高音；是叠紧（close position）还是拉开（open position）；有没有重复音、有没有省略音。

它决定的是**同一个和弦的「重量」与「颜色」**：C 大三和弦可以听起来厚重、空洞、明亮或浑浊，取决于排列。**常见误解是「和弦对了就好听」**——和弦名称只说明音高集合，排列才决定它在具体段落里是否成立。

::: en
**Voicing** is **how a chord's notes are distributed**: which note is in the bass, which in the inner parts, which on top; whether the chord is stacked close or spread open; whether notes are doubled or omitted.

It decides the **weight and colour of the same chord**: a C major triad can sound thick, hollow, bright or muddy depending on the voicing. **The usual mistake is to think the right chord is enough** — the chord name only names a pitch set; the voicing decides whether it works in that passage.
