::: zh
**活跃地（animato）** 出自意大利语 anima「灵魂、气息」，动词 animare 意为「赋予生命」。它要求音乐**有神、有生气、往前活**，而不是把音符走完。

严格说它描述**语气**：同样的速度，animato 的演奏更挺拔、更有弹跳。谱面上它常跟在速度标记之后（如 andante animato），此时通行做法是略微推进，但推进只是结果，不是定义。**常见误解是把 animato 直接当成「更快」**——只加速度而不换气口与重音，听上去只会更赶，不会更活。

::: en
**Animato** derives from the Italian anima, "soul, breath"; the verb animare means "to give life". The mark asks for music that is **spirited, alive, pressing forward**, not merely getting the notes played.

Strictly it describes **character**: at the same speed an animato reading stands taller and springs more. It often follows a tempo mark (andante animato), and the usual practice is then to move a little, but the motion is a result, not the definition. **The usual mistake is to treat animato as "faster"** — add speed without changing breath and accent and it sounds rushed, not alive.
