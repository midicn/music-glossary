::: zh
**梳状滤波（comb filtering）** 是同一个声音的直达信号与一个短延迟副本叠加后，频响出现等间距的峰与谷，形状像梳子。延迟越短，梳齿越宽；第一个谷出现在延迟半个周期的频率上。

它常来自两支麦克风拾到同一声源、桌面或调音台的早期反射，以及极短的延迟效果。听感是空洞、发薄或某些音高特别突出，且随听音位置移动而变化。处理办法是调整距离与时间对齐、减少重复拾音，或改变反射面。

常见误解是以为均衡器能修好梳状滤波。固定位置的谷可以硬提，但换一个听音点峰谷就移位；先解决时间与路径，再考虑均衡。
:::

::: en
**Comb filtering** appears when a direct sound sums with a short delayed copy, producing evenly spaced peaks and notches like the teeth of a comb. A shorter delay widens the spacing; the first notch falls where the delay equals half a cycle.

It commonly comes from two microphones hearing one source, early reflections off a desk or console, and very short delay effects. The result sounds hollow, thin or oddly coloured, and it shifts as the listener moves. Remedies include changing distance and time alignment, reducing duplicate pickup, or treating the reflecting surface.

The usual mistake is expecting an equaliser to repair it. A fixed notch can be filled, but move the listening point and the pattern moves; fix timing and paths first, then consider EQ.
:::
