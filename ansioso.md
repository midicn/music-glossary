::: zh
**焦虑地（ansioso）** 是表情术语：演奏要带**不安、急切、悬着**的语气。它不等于「快」：慢速也可以焦虑，靠的是**不稳定的重音、短促的呼吸与不解决的和声**。

它和 **agitato** 接近，但 agitato 更强调「激动、搅动」，ansioso 更强调心理上的悬疑与担忧。常见误解是把它弹成「更响更快」——焦虑来自控制中的不稳，不是失控。

::: en
**Ansioso** is an expression mark: play with **unease, urgency, suspense**. It is not "fast": a slow passage can be anxious through **unstable accents, short breaths and unresolved harmony**.

It is close to **agitato**, but agitato stresses agitation, ansioso psychological suspense and worry. The usual mistake is to play it louder and faster — anxiety comes from instability under control, not from losing control.
