::: zh
**力度（dynamics）** 是乐谱上标记的强弱：从 p（piano，弱）到 f（forte，强），以及 crescendo（渐强）与 diminuendo（渐弱）。它标记的是**相对关系**，不是固定的分贝数——同一首曲子的 f，在室内乐里和在交响乐团里响度完全不同；同一个 p，在钢琴上和在长笛上也不一样。力度记号给演奏者的是一把比例尺，不是一个音量旋钮。

常见误解：把力度当成音响的音量。录音制作里另有一个 velocity（MIDI 按键速度），那是输入设备记录的数值，也不是分贝——同一个词在两种语境里指两件事。

::: en
**Dynamics** are the loudness marks written in a score: from p (piano, soft) to f (forte, loud), plus crescendo (growing louder) and diminuendo (growing softer). They describe **relationships, not fixed decibel values** — the f of a chamber piece and the f of a symphony are at completely different sound levels, and the same p sits differently on a piano and on a flute. A dynamic mark gives the player a scale of proportions, not a volume knob.

The usual mistake is to treat dynamics as audio volume. In recording there is also MIDI velocity, a number captured from how fast a key is struck — not decibels either. The same word means two different things in the two contexts.
