::: zh
**回音（turn）** 是围绕主音的四音装饰：**上邻音—主音—下邻音—主音**（或反向）。谱面上常用一个像横放的「S」的记号，可写在主音上方、后方或中间。

它和**波音**不同：波音只折一次，回音要**绕一圈**；它和**颤音**也不同：回音有固定的四个音，颤音是多次交替。**常见误解是「回音只是加花」**——它改变旋律的**重心与落点**；放在主音前后，听感完全不同。

::: en
**A turn** is a four-note ornament around the principal note: **upper neighbour–main–lower neighbour–main** (or the reverse). Its sign looks like a sideways S and may sit above, after or through the main note.

It differs from a **mordent**: a mordent folds once, a turn **goes around**; it differs from a **trill** too: a turn has four fixed notes, a trill alternates many times. **The usual mistake is to treat it as mere decoration** — it changes the melody's **weight and landing point**; placed before or after the main note, it sounds completely different.
