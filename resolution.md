::: zh
**解决（resolution）**是不协和或紧张的音按要求走向稳定目标，通常以级进完成。挂留下行到和弦音，属七的导音上行到主音，不协和音程转入协和音程，都是解决；它兑现的是先前的预期。

解决不等于终止：终止是乐句层面的收束，解决可发生在任何一拍与声部；一个终止常含多次解决，一次解决未必构成终止。解决也可被推迟、规避或替代：延留推迟它，意外进行绕开它，等音改写引它去别处。

**解决的关键是「预期被兑现」。** 若听觉尚未建立倾向，音走到哪里都不叫解决；若倾向明确却被故意违背，得到悬置或冲击。判断时先问耳朵在等什么，再看音乐给了什么。
:::

::: en
**Resolution** is the move from a dissonance or tension to a stable target, usually by step. A suspension falls to a chord tone, a leading tone rises to the tonic, a dissonant interval turns consonant: each fulfils an expectation already formed.

Resolution is not a cadence. A cadence closes a phrase; a resolution can occur on any beat and in any voice. A cadence may contain several resolutions; one resolution need not make a cadence. It can be delayed, avoided or replaced: a retardation postpones it, a deceptive move sidesteps it, an enharmonic turn leads it elsewhere.

**The point is that expectation is kept.** Without an established tendency, a note moving anywhere is not a resolution; with a clear tendency deliberately denied, the result is suspension or shock. Ask what the ear waits for, then hear what the music gives.
:::
