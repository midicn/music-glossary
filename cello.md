## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 大提琴 |
| 英语 | cello |
| 意大利语 | violoncello |
| 德语 | Violoncello |
| 法语 | violoncelle |

**大提琴** 是提琴族的低音乐器，夹在两膝之间竖奏。cello 是 violoncello 的缩写，而 violoncello 又是 violone（大提琴的前身）的指小形式——**一个「小」字，套在「大」乐器上**，名字的历史与乐器的尺寸正好相反。

**常见混淆**：cello 与 double bass 都是低音弦乐器，但定弦与音区不同；中文「大提琴」对应 cello，「低音提琴」对应 double bass。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 大提琴 |
| English | cello |
| Italian | violoncello |
| German | Violoncello |
| French | violoncelle |

The **cello** is the low instrument of the violin family, held between the knees and played upright. Cello is short for violoncello, which is itself a diminutive of violone, the cello's ancestor — **a "little" suffix attached to a large instrument**: the name's history runs opposite to the instrument's size.

**Watch out**: cello and double bass are both low strings but differ in tuning and register; Chinese 大提琴 is the cello and 低音提琴 the double bass.
