::: zh
**主题与变奏（theme and variations）** 是把一个主题反复改写：和声骨架或旋律轮廓保留，节奏、音色、织体、调式、速度逐次改变。变奏可以装饰（加花）、改变性格（从舞曲变成进行曲）、改变调式（大调变小调），甚至只留下低音线（固定低音变奏）。

比较变奏时，可分别跟随旋律轮廓、低音、和声与句长，指出每段保留和改变的材料。

:::

::: en
**Theme and variations** rewrites one theme repeatedly: the harmonic frame or melodic outline stays, while rhythm, colour, texture, mode and tempo change. A variation may decorate, change character (a dance becomes a march), change mode (major to minor), or keep only the bass line (a ground).

Compare melodic contour, bass, harmony and phrase length to identify what each variation retains or changes.
