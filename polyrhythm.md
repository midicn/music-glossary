::: zh
**复节奏（polyrhythm）**是同一时间跨度被不同声部按不同等份分割，并同时发声，例如三对二、五对四。每层自身均匀，最小公倍数决定冲突循环的长度。慢速时能听出拍点交错，快速时更像一种律动色彩。

它可写成连音组，也可让一层按基本拍、另一层按三连音或五连音。非洲鼓乐、爵士与当代作品常用它制造推力；合奏者往往先找公分母，再把两层对齐。

**复节奏不是交错节奏的同义词。** 复节奏强调不同等分同时进行；交错节奏强调重音分组与节拍骨架的冲突，可以只出现在单一声部。它也与复合拍号不同：后者是每小节含两种拍子，而非两层同时分割。
:::

::: en
**Polyrhythm** divides the same span of time into different equal parts in different voices, as in three against two or five against four. Each layer is even, and their least common multiple sets the cycle. Slow, the attacks are clear; fast, the result is more a colour or groove.

It can be notated with tuplets, or with one layer on the beat and another in triplets or quintuplets. West African drumming, jazz and metal use it for drive; performers find a common denominator and align the layers to it.

**Polyrhythm is not a synonym for cross-rhythm.** Polyrhythm stresses different equal divisions sounding together; cross-rhythm stresses a conflict of accent grouping with the metre, and can occur in a single line. Nor is it a composite metre, where one bar contains two metres, not two simultaneous divisions.
:::
