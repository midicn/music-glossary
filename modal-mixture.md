::: zh
**调式交替（modal mixture）**是在同主音的平行大调与小调之间借用和弦或音级。C 大调可以借 C 小调的 iv、降 VI、降 VII、降 III；小调也可以借大调的 I、IV、V。主音不变，改变的是调式色彩。

借用和弦常出现在下属功能与终止之前。降 VI 与降 VII 可下行接属，iv 中的降六级常与属音形成半音叹息，皮卡迪三度则可视为终止处的调式交替。它们让音乐在不离开主调的情况下变暗或变亮。

**借用和弦不等于转调。** 新调没有建立，也没有新主音的终止式确认；它也不等于半音经过和弦，后者是横向的声部运动。判断时看主音与终止式是否仍守住原调，而不是只看出现了几个变化音。
:::

::: en
**Modal mixture** borrows chords or scale degrees between parallel major and minor keys sharing a tonic. C major may take iv, ♭VI, ♭VII or ♭III from C minor; a minor key may take I, IV or V from the major. The tonic stays put, while the modal colour changes.

Borrowed chords usually appear in subdominant areas and before cadences. ♭VI and ♭VII may descend to the dominant, the ♭6 of iv sighs against 5, and a Picardy third is mixture at the final cadence. They darken or brighten the music without leaving the key.

**Mixture is not modulation.** No new tonic is established and no cadence confirms a new key; nor is it a chromatic passing chord, a horizontal voice-leading event. Judge by whether the tonic and cadences still hold the old key, not by the number of accidentals.
:::
