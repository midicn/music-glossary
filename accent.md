::: zh
**重音记号（accent）** 让某一个音在听感上突出。谱面上常见的是「>」或「^」，但它**不一定只是更响**：重音可以通过更长的时值、更硬的音头、更早的起音、甚至音色的变化来做出。钢琴上加重音常是「更快地落下」，弦乐上常是「更快的弓速」。

要区分两种重音：**节拍重音**由拍子位置自动产生（小节第一拍天然更重），**表情重音**由记号或演奏者加上去。**把每个重音都弹成「更响」，是业余演奏最典型的浪费。**

::: en
**An accent** makes one note stand out. It is usually written as ">" or "^", but it **need not be simply louder**: an accent can be made with a longer duration, a harder onset, an earlier attack, even a change of colour. On the piano it is often "a faster fall of the key"; on strings "a faster bow".

Keep two kinds apart: **metric accent** arises from position in the bar (the first beat is naturally heavier), while **expressive accent** is added by a mark or a player. **Playing every accent as "louder" is the most typical waste of an amateur.**
