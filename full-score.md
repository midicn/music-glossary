::: zh
**大总谱（full score）** 收齐全部声部：木管、铜管、打击乐与弦乐各成一组，按惯例次序排列，是作品最完整的记谱形态。

它与缩编谱相对：缩写总谱把织体压到两三行，声乐谱只留人声与钢琴，大总谱则保留每个声部的独立走向，配器与内声部都能查证。

大总谱可以是移调谱，也可以是实际音高谱。读之前先看乐器表与谱号，别把移调乐器的记谱音直接当成实音。
:::

::: en
**A full score** contains every part: woodwind, brass, percussion and strings, each in its own group and in the customary order. It is the most complete written form of a work.

It is defined against reductions. A short score compresses the texture onto two or three staves and a vocal score keeps only voices and piano, while a full score preserves each part's independent line, so scoring and inner voices can be checked.

A full score may be transposed or in concert pitch. Read the instrumentation list and the clefs first, and do not mistake a transposing instrument's written note for its sounding pitch.
:::
