::: zh
**用弓杆奏（col legno）** 是把弓**翻转过来，用木杆接触琴弦**。它有两种做法：**battuto** 是用弓杆击弦，声音短、干、像木头敲击，常用来制造骨架般的节奏；**tratto** 是用弓杆在弦上拖，弓毛仍部分接触琴弦，得到持续而粗糙、带沙沙声的线条。

两种写法的音色差别很大。常见误解是把 col legno 当成一种声音：谱上只写 col legno 时，通行做法是奏 battuto 的击弦；而「杆加毛」的 tratto 更安静、更难控制，也更容易损伤弓杆，所以用得少。它不改变音高，却把音高的存在感削薄。
:::

::: en
**Col legno** turns the bow over and brings its **wooden stick** into contact with the strings. There are two forms: **battuto**, striking the string with the stick — short, dry, like a wooden tap, often used for skeletal rhythms; and **tratto**, drawing the stick across the string while the hair still partly touches it, which gives a sustained, rough, scraping line.

The two sound quite different. The usual mistake is to treat col legno as one colour: when the score says only col legno, the common practice is battuto; the "stick plus hair" tratto is quieter, harder to control and more damaging to the bow, so it is rarer. Pitch is not changed, but its presence is thinned.
:::
