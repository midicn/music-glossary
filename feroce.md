::: zh
**凶猛、猛烈地（feroce）**是音乐中的表情记号。

节奏与音头的鲜明对比可能有助于表达这一性格，但它不允许失去音准、节奏或合奏平衡。具体的起音方式与音色要依乐器、力度和作品风格决定。

::: en
**Feroce means fiercely.** It is an expression marking in music.

Strong rhythmic or attack contrasts may convey the character, but the word does not license inaccurate rhythm, intonation or ensemble balance. The appropriate attack and colour depend on instrument, dynamics and style.
