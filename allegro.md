::: zh
**快板（Allegro）** 表示明亮、活泼的速度，常约每分钟 120–156 拍。词源是意大利语 allegro（愉快、开朗），所以它首先是**性格**，其次才是速度：Allegro 不只是「快」，还要有精神。

常见误解是把它当成一个固定 BPM。同一个 Allegro，在莫扎特与马勒里可以差很远；同一首曲子的 Allegro，不同指挥也能给出完全不同的气质。**先问这段音乐在说什么，再决定它该多快。**

::: en
**Allegro** marks a bright, lively tempo, often around 120–156 beats per minute. The word comes from Italian allegro, "cheerful, bright", so it names a **character** first and a speed second: Allegro is not only fast, it must have spirit.

The usual mistake is to treat it as a fixed BPM. One Allegro can differ enormously between Mozart and Mahler, and two conductors can give the same Allegro completely different temperaments. **Ask what the music is saying before deciding how fast it should go.**
