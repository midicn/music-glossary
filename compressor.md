::: zh
**压缩器（compressor）** 在信号超过**阈值**（threshold）时，按**比率**（ratio）把超出的部分压下去，从而**缩小动态范围**。它还有启动（attack）与释放（release）两个时间参数，决定「多快开始压」与「多快松手」。

它和限制器不同：限制器通常比率极高、只处理峰值，用来防削波；压缩器更常用于塑造音色与稳定音量。常见误解是「压缩＝让声音变大」——压缩本身是减小动态，变大来自补偿增益；压得越多，越需要用增益补回来，也越容易失去生命力。

::: en
**A compressor** turns down whatever passes a **threshold**, by a **ratio**, reducing **dynamic range**. It also has attack and release times, deciding how fast it clamps and how fast it lets go.

It differs from a limiter: a limiter usually uses a very high ratio and catches only peaks to prevent clipping; a compressor is more often used to shape tone and steady level. The usual mistake is to think compression makes things louder — compression reduces dynamics; loudness comes from makeup gain. **The more you compress, the more gain you need back, and the easier it is to lose life.**
