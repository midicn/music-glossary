::: zh
**加倍（doubling）** 是让两个声部**同时奏同一条线**，常见于八度、同度或不同乐器之间。它的作用不是增加「音符」，而是**加厚音色、提高音量、改变质感**：弦乐八度加倍让线条更亮，木管加倍让色彩更突出。

它和**和声的重复音**不同：加倍是同一条旋律的复制，重复音是和弦内部的声部安排。常见误解是「加倍越多越好」——加倍过多会让线条变糊、低频堆积；配器的判断在于加什么、加在哪、加多少。

::: en
**Doubling** has two parts play **the same line** at once, usually at the octave or unison, or across different instruments. Its job is not to add "more notes" but to **thicken colour, raise volume, change texture**: octave doubling brightens a string line, wind doubling makes a colour stand out.

It differs from **doubling a note inside a chord**: doubling copies one melodic line, while chord doubling arranges voices within the harmony. The usual mistake is to think more doubling is better — too much blurs the line and piles up the low end; the judgement is what to double, where, and how much.
