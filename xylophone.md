## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 木琴 |
| 英语 | xylophone |
| 意大利语 | xilofono |
| 德语 | Xylophon |
| 法语 | xylophone |

**木琴** 由一排硬木音条组成，下方常配金属共鸣管，用琴槌击奏。英语 **xylophone** 来自希腊语 xylon（木）与 phōnē（声音），意大利语 **xilofono**、德语 **Xylophon**、法语 xylophone 都是同一词根的转写——名字本身就在说「木头的音」。

常见混淆：木琴与马林巴常被当成一件乐器；马林巴音条更宽、音区更低、音色更柔，木琴则更短更亮（通行说法）。中文「木琴」有时泛指木条击奏类乐器，专业语境应加以区分。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 木琴 |
| English | xylophone |
| Italian | xilofono |
| German | Xylophon |
| French | xylophone |

The **xylophone** has a row of hard-wood bars, usually with metal resonator tubes below, struck with mallets. English xylophone comes from Greek xylon (wood) and phōnē (sound); Italian xilofono, German Xylophon and French xylophone transcribe the same root — **the name itself says "wood sound"**.

**Watch out**: xylophone and marimba are often treated as one instrument; the marimba has wider, lower, softer bars, the xylophone shorter and brighter ones (a common usage). Chinese 木琴 can also loosely mean any bar-struck instrument, so professional writing distinguishes them.
