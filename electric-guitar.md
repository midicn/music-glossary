## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 电吉他 |
| 英语 | electric guitar |
| 意大利语 | chitarra elettrica |
| 德语 | E-Gitarre |
| 法语 | guitare électrique |

**电吉他** 是依靠电磁拾音器把弦振动转成电信号的拨弦乐器，通常需要放大器与音箱才能发出我们熟悉的声音。意大利语 chitarra elettrica、德语 E-Gitarre、法语 guitare électrique 都是「电的吉他」。它 1930 年代进入实用阶段，1950 年代实心琴体定型，成为流行音乐的核心乐器之一。

**常见混淆**：电吉他不是「加了拾音器的木吉他」——实心电吉他的琴体不靠共鸣箱发声，音色主要由拾音器、木材与电路决定；它也常与电贝斯混为一谈，两者外形相近，但音域、弦粗与乐队角色不同。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 电吉他 |
| English | electric guitar |
| Italian | chitarra elettrica |
| German | E-Gitarre |
| French | guitare électrique |

The **electric guitar** is a plucked instrument whose magnetic pickups turn string vibration into an electrical signal, usually needing an amplifier and speaker to produce its familiar sound. Italian chitarra elettrica, German E-Gitarre and French guitare électrique all say "electric guitar". It became practical in the 1930s and settled into the solid body in the 1950s, becoming central to popular music.

**Watch out**: it is not an acoustic guitar with a pickup added — a solid-body electric does not rely on a resonating box, and its tone depends largely on pickups, wood and circuitry. It is also not an electric bass, despite the similar shape.
