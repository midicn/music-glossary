::: zh
**突然地（subito）** 要求变化来得没有过渡：subito piano 是一下子转轻，subito forte 是一下子转响，subito accelerando 是立刻提速。它标记的是切换，不是渐变。

subito 常与 [[glossary:sforzando]]、[[glossary:forte]] 等力度手段配合，但**突然不等于随意**：好的突转在发生前已有伏笔，和声、织体或呼吸会把它托住。它与 poco a poco 正相反；若把 subito 弹成提前渐强，效果就被抹平了，听众也不会被真正震住。
:::

::: en
**Subito** demands a change without transition: subito piano turns soft at once, subito forte loud at once, subito accelerando picks up speed immediately. It marks a switch, not a slope.

Subito often works with sforzando and forte, but **sudden is not arbitrary**: a good lurch has been prepared by harmony, texture or breath, so that it lands rather than merely startles. It is the exact opposite of poco a poco; play it as an early crescendo and the effect is wiped out.
:::
