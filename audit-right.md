::: zh
**审计权（audit right）** 是合同赋予权利人**查账**的权利：在约定条件下检查对方的销售记录、报表与付款计算，确认版税是否少付。它通常限定频率、提前通知期限与可查年份。

常见误解是「报表看着对就不用查」：分成口径、扣项与汇率都可能出错。行使审计权往往要先书面通知，费用一般由主张方先付，查出错漏再按合同分担。**通识说明，不构成法律意见。**

::: en
**An audit right** lets a rights holder **inspect the books**: under agreed conditions, examine sales records, statements and payment calculations to check whether royalties were underpaid. It usually limits how often an audit may occur, the notice required and which years are open.

**The usual mistake is to trust a statement that looks right**: split definitions, deductions and exchange rates can all go wrong. An audit normally starts with written notice, and the requesting party usually pays first, with costs shared under the contract if errors are found. **This is general information, not legal advice.**
