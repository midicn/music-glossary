::: zh
**交响诗（tone poem）** 是**单乐章**的标题性管弦乐，音乐跟随一首诗、一幅画或一个故事展开，由李斯特在十九世纪四五十年代命名并确立。它常用**主题变形**：一个核心动机随情节改变性格、速度与配器。

它和标题交响曲的区别在结构：交响诗不套四乐章框架，却常暗含奏鸣曲式、变奏或回旋的轮廓。理查·施特劳斯把叙事推得更远，斯美塔那《我的祖国》由六首交响诗组成。听的时候抓住核心动机的变形与场景切换处的配器变化。

**常见误解**：以为交响诗就是「有故事的交响曲」，或以为标题已经把音乐解释完了。它不是给情节配乐，标题只提供联想的方向；序曲、狂想曲同样是单乐章，却各有来历。

::: en
**A tone poem**, or symphonic poem, is a **single-movement** orchestral work with a title drawn from a poem, painting or story. Liszt named and established the genre in the 1840s and 1850s. **Thematic transformation** is its typical device: one motif changes character, tempo and scoring as the narrative moves.

Unlike a programmatic symphony, it avoids the four-movement frame, yet often implies sonata form, variation or rondo beneath the surface. Strauss pushes narrative further; Smetana's Má Vlast is a cycle of six tone poems. Listen for the transformation of the core motif and for changes of scoring at each scene.

**A common misconception** treats the tone poem as a symphony with a story, or as music whose title explains it. It is not a soundtrack: the title only sets a direction, and the music must stand on its motives, harmony and structure. Overtures and rhapsodies are single-movement too, but have their own histories.
:::
