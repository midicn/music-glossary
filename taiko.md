## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 太鼓 |
| 英语 | taiko |
| 意大利语 | taiko |
| 德语 | Taiko |
| 法语 | taiko（tambour japonais） |

**太鼓** 是日语对鼓的泛称，读作 taiko；在英语和中文语境里，它常特指日本祭典与舞台上的大型鼓组，以及以这种鼓为核心的合奏。德语、法语、意大利语多直接借 **taiko**，法语有时补充 tambour japonais。**同一个词，在日语里是「鼓」的总称，在外语里却常被当成一种乐器**（通行说法）。

**常见混淆**：太鼓不是单一鼓种——长胴的 nagado-daiko、缔太鼓 shime-daiko、桶胴的 okedo-daiko 形制与音高都不同；「太鼓」也不等于「日本鼓乐」这一体裁，后者常称 wadaiko 或 taiko ensemble。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 太鼓 |
| English | taiko |
| Italian | taiko |
| German | Taiko |
| French | taiko (tambour japonais) |

**Taiko** is the Japanese word for drum in general; in English and Chinese it often refers specifically to the large drums of Japanese festivals and stage ensembles, and to the ensemble practice built around them. German, French and Italian generally borrow **taiko**, with French sometimes adding tambour japonais. **The same word is a generic term for drums in Japanese but is treated abroad as one instrument** (a common usage).

**Watch out**: taiko is not a single drum type — the long-bodied nagado-daiko, the shime-daiko and the barrel-bodied okedo-daiko differ in construction and pitch; nor is taiko the same as the ensemble genre, often called wadaiko or taiko ensemble.
