::: zh
**电容麦克风（condenser microphone）** 用一个薄振膜与背板构成电容，声波让振膜移动、电容量改变，再经电路转成电信号。它的振膜轻，瞬态与高频细节通常比动圈更细。

多数电容麦需要 48 伏幻象供电来驱动内部电路，有的还带衰减、低切与指向切换。它灵敏度高，录安静声源轻松，但在近距离强声压前容易过载；潮湿环境也可能让振膜出问题。

常见误解是把电容麦等同于人声麦。它同样适合原声乐器、房间声与合唱；真正要判断的是声源动态、摆放距离与是否需要供电，而不是标签。
:::

::: en
A **condenser microphone** forms a capacitor between a thin diaphragm and a backplate. Sound moves the diaphragm, changes capacitance, and the circuit turns that change into a signal. The light diaphragm usually captures transients and high-frequency detail more finely than a moving coil.

Most condensers need 48-volt phantom power for their internal electronics, and many offer pads, low-cut filters and switchable patterns. High sensitivity makes quiet sources easy to record, yet close loud sources can overload them, and damp conditions may trouble the diaphragm.

The usual mistake is treating condensers as vocal microphones only. They suit acoustic instruments, room sound and choirs just as well. Judge the source, working distance and power requirement rather than the label.
:::
