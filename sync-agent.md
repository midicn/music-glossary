::: zh
**同步代理（sync agent）** 替权利人把音乐推给影视、广告与游戏项目，并代表其谈判同步许可：寻找机会、报价、处理合同与发票，通常按成交额抽佣。代理的是**授权谈判**，不是版权本身，也不等于发行商。

一首歌的同步常需词曲与录音两层授权，代理可能只代表其中一层，所以要确认他代表谁、能签哪一层。常见误解是「代理发来报价就等于能用」：最终仍要拿到写明用途、地域、期限与媒体的书面许可。**通识说明，不构成法律意见。**

::: en
**A sync agent** pitches music to film, advertising and game projects on behalf of rights holders and negotiates sync licences: finding opportunities, quoting fees, handling contracts and invoices, usually for a commission. The agent handles **licensing negotiations**, not the copyright itself, and is not a distributor.

A sync often needs both composition and master clearance, and an agent may represent only one layer, so confirm whom they represent and what they can sign. **The usual mistake is to treat a quote as permission**: a written licence stating use, territory, term and media is still required. **This is general information, not legal advice.**
