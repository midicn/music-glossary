::: zh
**艺人版税（artist royalty）** 是唱片公司按合同付给录音艺人的分成，通常按**录音净收入**的约定比例计算。它不同于词曲版税：后者来自作品，前者来自母带；制作人可能另有分成，会先从同一笔收入里扣。

常见误解是「版税率乘以流水就是到手」：合同常先扣包装、分销与宣传费用，还要**回本**，且实体、下载与流媒体的费率可能不同。**看合约要问：基数是什么、扣项有哪些、回本前后是否同一费率。**

::: en
**An artist royalty** is the share a label pays the recording artist under contract, usually a percentage of **net recording receipts**. It differs from a song royalty: the latter comes from the composition, the former from the master. Producers and mixers may hold their own points, deducted from the same income.

**The usual mistake is to multiply the rate by gross revenue**: contracts often deduct packaging, distribution and marketing first, then require **recoupment**, and rates may differ across physical, download and streaming. **Ask what the base is, which deductions apply, and whether the rate changes after recoupment.**
