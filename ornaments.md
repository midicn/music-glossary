::: zh
**装饰音（ornaments）** 是写在主音旁边或上方的小音符与符号：倚音、波音、回音、颤音、滑音等。它们改变旋律的**细节与语气**，有时也承担**和声张力**。

它不是「可有可无的花边」：在巴洛克与古典音乐里，装饰音是**演奏者必须补全的语法**，甚至影响和声的解决。常见误解是「装饰音越少越干净」——删掉装饰音，某些乐句会失去重心；装饰音不是装饰，是句子的一部分。

::: en
**Ornaments** are the small notes and signs beside or above the main note: appoggiaturas, mordents, turns, trills, slides and more. They shape the melody's **detail and manner**, and sometimes carry **harmonic tension**.

They are not "optional frills": in Baroque and Classical music ornaments are **grammar the performer is expected to complete**, and can affect how the harmony resolves. The usual mistake is to think fewer ornaments are cleaner — remove them and some phrases lose their weight; ornaments are not decoration but part of the sentence.
