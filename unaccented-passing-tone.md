::: zh
**弱拍经过音（unaccented passing tone）** 是**落在弱拍上**的经过音：它级进连接两个和弦音，主要起**流畅与方向**的作用，不强调不协和。

它和**强拍经过音**不同：弱拍经过音是「经过」，强拍经过音是「强调」。常见误解是「经过音都可有可无」——没有弱拍经过音，级进会变成断裂的跳进；它不抢耳，却决定句子顺不顺。

::: en
**An unaccented passing tone** is a passing tone **on a weak beat**: it connects two chord tones by step and mainly supplies **flow and direction**, without stressing the dissonance.

It differs from an **accented passing tone**: the unaccented one passes, the accented one stresses. The usual mistake is to think passing tones are dispensable — without them, stepwise motion becomes broken leaps; it does not grab the ear, but it decides whether the phrase flows.
