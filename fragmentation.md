::: zh
**动机分裂（fragmentation）**是把动机越切越短：先取前半，再取两音，最后只剩一个音程或节奏细胞。材料变少，重复变密，重心从旋律转到推力，紧张上扬。它是展开部最常用的手段之一。

分裂常与模进、模仿、紧接结合：碎片在不同音高与声部间传递，调性随之游移；缩到最小时，往往接上属准备，推向再现。贝多芬《第五交响曲》第一乐章的发展部，就把四音动机切成更小单位反复冲击。

**分裂不等于减值。** 减值缩短的是每个音的时值，动机的完整轮廓仍在；分裂缩短的是单位本身，只保留动机的一部分。判断时看材料是「变快」还是「变少」；两者常同时发生，概念不同。
:::

::: en
**Fragmentation** cuts a motive into ever smaller pieces: first its head, then two of its notes, finally a single interval or rhythmic cell. There is less material and more repetition, and the weight shifts from melody to drive. It is common in development sections.

Fragmentation often joins sequence, imitation and stretto: the shards pass through keys and voices, and at their smallest they usually reach a dominant preparation that pushes into the recapitulation. Beethoven's Fifth Symphony, first movement, is a classic case, hammering the four-note motive in ever smaller units.

**Fragmentation is not diminution.** Diminution shortens the note values while the motive's full outline remains; fragmentation shortens the unit itself and keeps only part of the motive. Ask whether the material has become faster or smaller; the two often coincide but are different ideas.
:::
