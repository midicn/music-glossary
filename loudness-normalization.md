::: zh
**响度归一（loudness normalization）** 是把不同曲目的**感知响度**拉到同一目标：流媒体平台常用 LUFS 作为标准，播放时自动调整，让用户不必每首手动调音量。

它和**峰值归一**不同：峰值归一保峰值，响度归一保**听感**；它和**压缩**也不同：归一不改变内部动态，只改整体电平。**常见误解是「母带做得越响，平台上就越响」**——平台会把过响的曲目**压下来**；**响度战争在归一时代已经失去意义。**

::: en
**Loudness normalization** brings tracks to the same **perceptual loudness** target: streaming platforms often use LUFS and adjust on playback, so listeners need not change volume for every song.

It differs from **peak normalization**: peak normalisation preserves peaks, loudness normalisation preserves **the listening impression**; it differs from **compression** too: normalisation changes overall level, not internal dynamics. **The usual mistake is to think a louder master is louder on a platform** — the platform **turns loud tracks down**; **the loudness war is pointless in a normalised world.**
