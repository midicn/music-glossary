::: zh
**很、非常（molto）** 是最常见的程度词，放在术语前面把它的性格推得更远：molto allegro、molto espressivo、molto sostenuto。它本身没有速度，也没有表情，只是一个放大器。

molto 与 [[glossary:assai]] 接近，与 [[glossary:poco]] 方向相反。**连续堆叠程度词并不会让音乐更清楚**：molto molto 式的写法在乐谱里极少，因为演奏者需要的是一个可执行的性格，而不是形容词比赛。用它时要问：被加强的那个词，原先是否已经站住？
:::

::: en
**Molto** is the commonest intensifier, placed before a mark to push its character further: molto allegro, molto espressivo, molto sostenuto. It has no speed or expression of its own; it is an amplifier.

Molto is close to assai and opposite to poco. **Stacking intensifiers does not make music clearer**: "molto molto" is very rare in scores, because a player needs an executable character, not a contest of adjectives. Before adding molto, ask whether the word being intensified already stands on its own.
:::
