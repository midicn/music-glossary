::: zh
**乐句（phrase）**是旋律里像「一句话」的单位：它有自己的起点、走向和收束，收束处通常出现终止式，或至少有一个清楚的停顿。谱面上的连音线、换气记号、和声节奏都能提示乐句的边界，但最终要靠耳朵判断：哪里听起来像说完了，哪里只是换气。一个乐句常见四小节，也可以更短或更长。

乐句不是「按小节切分」。小节是节拍的容器，乐句是意义的单位；一个小节可以含两个短句，一个乐句也可以跨过小节线、从小节的弱拍开始。**判断乐句要看旋律的走向、和声的落点与节奏的松紧，而不是数小节。**乐句的收束有强有弱：停在主和弦像句号，停在属和弦像逗号，停在意想不到的和弦上则像问号或省略号。

常见误解是把乐句等同于「四小节」或「换气的地方」。四小节只是常见样式，不是定义；许多乐句是五小节、七小节，甚至故意让句长不对称。另一个误解是以为乐句必须由旋律单独构成；实际上和声、低音、织体与配器都在划分乐句。**听一段音乐时，先找它在哪里呼吸、在哪里落地，比数小节更接近乐句本身。**
:::

::: en
**A phrase** is the unit of music that behaves like a sentence: it has a beginning, a direction and a close, and the close usually brings a cadence or at least a clear pause. Slurs, breath marks and harmonic rhythm can suggest where phrases begin and end, but the ear has the final say: some points sound finished, others merely sound like a breath. A phrase is often four bars long, but it can be shorter or longer.

A phrase is not a bar-count. Bars are containers of metre; phrases are units of meaning. One bar may hold two short phrases, and one phrase may cross a barline and begin on a weak beat. **To hear phrases, follow the melodic direction, the harmonic landing points and the tightening or loosening of rhythm rather than counting bars.** Phrase endings differ in weight: a close on the tonic is a full stop, a close on the dominant a comma, and a close on an unexpected chord a question mark or an ellipsis.

The common misconception is to equate a phrase with four bars or with the place where a singer breathes. Four bars are a common shape, not a definition; many phrases run to five or seven bars, and composers often make phrase lengths deliberately uneven. Nor is a phrase necessarily made by the melody alone: harmony, bass, texture and orchestration all help to mark it. **When listening, ask where the music breathes and where it lands; that comes closer to the phrase than counting bars.**
