::: zh
**三和弦（triad）** 是三个音按三度叠起来：根音、三音、五音。三音决定性质——根音到三音是大三度就是**大三和弦**，小三度就是**小三和弦**；五音再决定是**增**还是减。四种性质——大、小、增、减——是和声语言的四个基本词。

三和弦可以转位：把根音移到上面，最低音换成三音或五音，和弦名称不变，重力与色彩变了。三和弦不是「三个音随便叠」：它特指三度叠置的结构，二度或四度叠出来的音簇是另一种东西。

::: en
**A triad** is three notes stacked in thirds: root, third and fifth. The third decides the quality — a major third above the root makes a **major triad**, a minor third a **minor triad** — and the fifth then makes it **augmented** or diminished. Major, minor, augmented and diminished are the four basic words of harmonic language.

Triads can be inverted: move the root above the third or fifth and the name stays the same while the weight and colour change. A triad is not "any three notes": it specifically means a stack of thirds; clusters built from seconds or fourths are something else.
