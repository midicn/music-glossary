::: zh
**节奏（rhythm）** 是声音在时间里的组织：音有多长、什么时候出现、哪里重、哪里轻。它比「拍子」宽——拍子是稳定的脉冲，节奏是在脉冲上实际发生的事；它也比「速度」宽——同样的节奏型，慢速与快速可以是两种性格。

常见误解是把节奏等同于「音符时值的组合」。真正的节奏还包括**重音、发音方式与留白**：同样四个音，连奏与断奏、重读与轻读，节奏感完全不同。

::: en
**Rhythm** is the organisation of sound in time: how long notes last, when they begin, where the weight falls and where it lifts. It is broader than "beat" — the beat is a steady pulse, rhythm is what actually happens on it; it is broader than "tempo" too — the same rhythmic pattern at a slow and a fast speed has two characters.

The usual mistake is to equate rhythm with "a combination of note values". Real rhythm also includes **accent, articulation and silence**: the same four notes legato or staccato, stressed or light, feel completely different.
