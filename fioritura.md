::: zh
**花音（fioritura）** 是旋律上的**华丽装饰**，字面意思是「开花」：在主干旋律上加入快速音阶、琶音与装饰音，让句子「开花」。它常见于歌剧咏叹调与器乐协奏曲。

它和**花腔**接近，但 fioritura 更强调**装饰的段落性**，coloratura 更强调**声乐的技巧类型**。**常见误解是「花音越多越华丽」**——花音要**长在句子的骨架上**；没有骨架的加花，只是音阶练习。

::: en
**Fioritura** is **florid embellishment** of a melodic line — literally "flowering": fast scales, arpeggios and ornaments added to the main melody so the phrase blossoms. It is common in opera arias and instrumental concertos.

It is close to **coloratura**, but fioritura stresses **the ornamental passage**, while coloratura stresses **a vocal technique type**. **The usual mistake is to think more fioritura is more brilliant** — it must **grow from the phrase's skeleton**; embellishment without a skeleton is just a scale exercise.
