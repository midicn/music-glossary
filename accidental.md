::: zh
**变音记号（accidental）** 是写在音符旁边的升号（♯）、降号（♭）或还原号（♮），临时把一个音升高或降低半音，或取消之前的升降。它和**调号**不同：调号管整首曲子的默认升降，变音记号只管**当前小节内、同一音高**的后续音符。

常见误解是「变音记号只管一个音」：它从记号出现的位置起，在本小节内对同一音名、同一八度持续有效，直到小节线或新的记号。**写谱的人要记住：它是临时的，但临时也有范围。**

::: en
**An accidental** is a sharp (♯), flat (♭) or natural (♮) written beside a note to raise or lower it by a semitone, or to cancel an earlier sign. It differs from the **key signature**: the signature sets the default for the whole piece, an accidental governs **the rest of the bar at that pitch**.

The usual mistake is to think an accidental affects only one note: from the sign onward it applies to the same note name in the same octave for the rest of the bar, until the barline or another sign. **It is temporary, but its temporary scope has boundaries.**
