::: zh
**仓促加快（affrettando）** 来自 affrettare，「催促、赶忙」。它要求速度向前赶，并带一点急切甚至不安的语气，常见于歌剧与戏剧性段落。它与 [[glossary:accelerando]] 同为加速，但后者语气中性，affrettando 则暗示有什么在推着音乐走。

这种加速通常幅度不大、时间不长，像呼吸突然变浅；**赶得太早会把后面的高潮提前用掉**。它常与 [[glossary:stringendo]] 相邻使用：affrettando 偏情绪上的催促，stringendo 偏织体与力度的收紧，两者可以叠加，也可以分工。若谱上只写 affrettando，仍要守住基本拍，让加速有层次；在 [[glossary:agitato]] 的段落里，它的急切尤其明显。
:::

::: en
**Affrettando** comes from affrettare, "to hurry". It asks the music to press forward with a shade of urgency, even anxiety, and is common in opera and dramatic passages. It accelerates like accelerando, but where accelerando is neutral, affrettando implies something driving the music on.

The push is usually small and short, like a breath turning shallow; **hurrying too early spends the climax before it arrives**. It often sits beside stringendo: affrettando leans on emotion, stringendo on tightening texture and force, and the two may combine or divide the work. If affrettando stands alone, keep the underlying beat so the speed-up has shape.
:::
