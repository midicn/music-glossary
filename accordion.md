## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 手风琴 |
| 英语 | accordion |
| 意大利语 | fisarmonica |
| 德语 | Akkordeon |
| 法语 | accordéon |

**手风琴** 是靠风箱送气、使自由簧片振动发声的便携键盘乐器，右手为键盘或键钮，左手为低音键钮。英语 **accordion** 与德语 **Akkordeon** 同源，意大利语 **fisarmonica**、法语 **accordéon** 是各自传统的叫法；中文「手风琴」强调双手操作风箱。

**常见混淆**：手风琴与班多钮（bandoneon）都是自由簧风箱乐器，但班多钮多为方形、键钮排列不同，音色更干涩，常与探戈联系；把两者都译作「手风琴」会掩盖形制差异（通行说法）。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 手风琴 |
| English | accordion |
| Italian | fisarmonica |
| German | Akkordeon |
| French | accordéon |

The **accordion** is a portable keyboard instrument in which bellows drive air across free reeds, with a piano or button keyboard on the right hand and bass buttons on the left. English accordion and German Akkordeon share a root; Italian fisarmonica and French accordéon are the traditional names in those languages; the Chinese 手风琴 stresses two-handed bellows work.

**Watch out**: accordion and bandoneon are both free-reed bellows instruments, but the bandoneon is usually square, with a different button layout and a drier tone, and is tied to tango; calling both 手风琴 hides the difference (a common usage).
