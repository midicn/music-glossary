::: zh
**倚音（appoggiatura）** 是从上方或下方**靠进主音**的装饰音：它先响，再把主音「解决」出来。巴洛克与古典时期的长倚音会**占主音的一部分时值**，不是一闪而过。

它和**经过音**不同：经过音是连接两个和弦音，倚音则强调一个不协和音，再解决。常见误解是「倚音越短越好」——长倚音的重量正是它的表情来源；把它弹成装饰性的小音符，和声的张力就没了。

::: en
**An appoggiatura** is an ornament that **leans into** the principal note from above or below: it sounds first, then resolves to the main note. In Baroque and Classical practice the long appoggiatura **takes part of the principal note's value**, rather than flashing past.

It differs from a **passing tone**: a passing tone connects two chord tones, while an appoggiatura stresses a dissonance and then resolves it. The usual mistake is to make it as short as possible — the weight of a long appoggiatura is exactly where its expression lies; play it as a tiny grace note and the harmonic tension disappears.
