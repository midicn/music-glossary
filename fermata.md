::: zh
**延长记号（fermata）** 是写在音符或休止符上方（或下方）的半圆加一点，意思是**把这个时值延长**，具体多久由演奏者或指挥决定。它是一个「开放时值」：谱面给出下限，不给出上限。

常见误解是把它当成「随便停多久」。延长记号仍然在音乐的时间感里：停得久，前后的呼吸与和声张力就要接得住。在独奏里它是表情的入口，在合奏里它必须由一个人统一——否则每个人停的长度不同，音乐就散了。

::: en
**A fermata** is the semicircle-and-dot placed over or under a note or rest: **hold this value longer**, with the actual length decided by the performer or conductor. It is an "open duration" — the page gives a minimum, not a maximum.

The usual mistake is to treat it as "stop for as long as you like". A fermata still lives inside musical time: a long hold must be carried by the breathing and harmonic tension around it. In a solo it is an opening for expression; in an ensemble one person must set it — otherwise everyone holds for a different length and the music falls apart.
