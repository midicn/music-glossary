::: zh
**有力地（energico）** 来自意大利语 energia，源头是希腊语 energeia「活动、作为」，由 en-「在内」与 ergon「工作」组成。它要求演奏**有劲、有推进、把节奏推着走**。

它说的是**能量的投放方式**，不是速度。energico 的段落常常中速偏稳，靠重音与低音撑起骨架。**常见误解是把 energico 从头响到尾**——持续最响会让耳朵疲劳，劲道反而消失；在段落内部留出强弱层次，能量才听得出来。

::: en
**Energico** comes from the Italian energia, from Greek energeia, "activity, working", built from en- ("in") and ergon ("work"). The playing should be **forceful, propulsive, driving the rhythm**.

It concerns **how energy is spent**, not speed. Energico passages often sit at a steady moderate tempo, held up by accent and bass. **The usual mistake is to keep it loud from beginning to end** — constant volume tires the ear and the force disappears; energy is heard only when the passage has levels.
