::: zh
**FP 编号** 是普朗克（Francis Poulenc）作品的目录编号，由 Carl B. Schmidt 编成，按创作年代排列。它给没有 Op. 号或编号混乱的作品一个稳定身份。

它和 **Op.** 不同：普朗克的作品号并不连续，FP 是**研究性目录**。**常见误解是「FP 是出版商编号」**——它是目录编号；同一作品可能既有 Op. 号又有 FP 号。

:::

::: en
**FP numbers** catalogue the works of Francis Poulenc, compiled by Carl B. Schmidt and arranged chronologically. They give a stable identity to pieces without a reliable Op. number.

They differ from **Op.**: Poulenc's opus numbers are not continuous; FP is a **scholarly catalogue**. **The usual mistake is to read FP as a publisher's number** — it is a catalogue number; a work may carry both an Op. and an FP.
