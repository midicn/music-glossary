::: zh
**尽可能（possibile）** 常与 il più 连用，构成「尽可能……」：presto possibile 是「尽可能快」，il più forte possibile 是「尽可能强」，il più legato possibile 是「尽可能连」。它给出的是一个方向上的极限，而不是一个可测量的数值。

这类记号把判断交给演奏者与乐器：**「尽可能」仍以音乐成立为前提**，不是失控的许可。管乐的 presto possibile 受指法与换气限制，弦乐受运弓限制，合奏时还要顾及整体。它和 [[glossary:piu-mosso]] 不同，后者是「更动起来」的比较级，前者是「到你所能做到的尽头」。遇到它，先找限制在哪里，再决定推多远；若写在 [[glossary:presto]] 旁，也要给 [[glossary:dynamics]] 留出余地。
:::

::: en
**Possibile** usually joins il più to form "as ... as possible": presto possibile, as fast as possible; il più forte possibile, as loud as possible; il più legato possibile, as smooth as possible. It points to a limit in one direction, not to a measurable value.

Such marks leave the judgement to player and instrument: **"as possible" still assumes the music holds together** and is not a licence for loss of control. A wind player's presto possibile is bounded by fingering and breath, a string player's by the bow, an ensemble's by the whole. It differs from più mosso, a comparative asking for more motion; possibile asks for the far end of what you can do.
:::
