::: zh
半音中音和弦（chromatic mediant）是根音相距三度、且至少一个和弦含调外音的和弦关系。比如 C 大调里的 E 大、降 A 大与降 E 大和弦：它们与主和弦共享一个音，却因三度性质改变而色彩突变。

它不同于调内的中音与下中音：vi 与 iii 仍在同一音阶里，半音中音则跨越调式。浪漫派与电影配乐常用它制造突然的明暗转换；两个和弦之间常有共同音，声部可以平滑连接。

它也不等于借用和弦。借用有明确的调式来源，半音中音只要求三度根音关系与色彩差异；同一个和弦在不同上下文里，可能被解释为借用，也可能只是色彩中音。
:::

::: en
A chromatic mediant is a chord whose root lies a third from another chord, with at least one outside the key. In C major, E major, A♭ major and E♭ major relate this way to the tonic: each shares a note with it, yet the changed third shifts the colour.

It differs from the diatonic mediant and submediant: vi and iii stay within the scale, while a chromatic mediant crosses the mode. Romantic music and film scores use it for sudden shifts of light and shade; a common tone often lets the voices move smoothly.

It is not a borrowed chord. Borrowing has a definite modal source, whereas a chromatic mediant only requires the third relation and the colour gap; the same chord may sound like borrowing in one context and pure colour in another.
:::
