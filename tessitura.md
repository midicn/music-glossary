::: zh
**常用音区（tessitura）** 是一首曲子**最常停留的音区**：不是最高到哪、最低到哪，而是**大部分时间待在哪儿**。它决定演唱/演奏的舒适度与音色负担。

它和**音域（range）**不同：音域是极限，tessitura 是重心；一首音域不宽的歌，如果 tessitura 一直卡在高音区，同样很吃力。常见误解是「音域够就行」——tessitura 才是真正的难度所在。

::: en
**Tessitura** is **where a piece mostly sits**: not how high or low it goes, but **where it spends most of its time**. It decides comfort and the colour burden of a singer or player.

It differs from **range**: range is the outer limit, tessitura the centre of gravity; a song with a modest range can still be exhausting if its tessitura stays high. The usual mistake is to check only the range — tessitura is where the real difficulty lives.
