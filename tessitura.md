::: zh
**常用音区（tessitura）** 是一首曲子**最常停留的音区**：不是最高到哪、最低到哪，而是**大部分时间待在哪儿**。它决定演唱/演奏的舒适度与音色负担。

它与**音域（range）**的侧重点不同：音域给出最低音与最高音，tessitura 说明声部主要停留的位置。即使总音域不宽，长期处于较高或较低位置也可能增加负担；实际难度还与时值、力度、音区转换及演奏者条件有关。

::: en
**Tessitura** is **where a piece mostly sits**: not how high or low it goes, but **where it spends most of its time**. It decides comfort and the colour burden of a singer or player.

It differs in emphasis from **range**: range gives the lowest and highest pitches, while tessitura concerns where a part mainly lies. A modest range can still be demanding if it remains high or low for a long time; difficulty also depends on duration, dynamics, register changes and the performer.
