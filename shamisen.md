## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 三味线（三味線） |
| 英语 | shamisen |
| 意大利语 | shamisen |
| 德语 | Shamisen |
| 法语 | shamisen |

**三味线**是日本的三弦拨弦乐器，细长琴颈配方形音箱，用大拨片（bachi）击弦，声音干脆，常用于歌舞伎、文乐与民谣。它的原型是中国三弦，经琉球传入日本（通行说法）。意、德、法没有另起专名，径用 shamisen。

**常见混淆**：三味线不是中国的三弦——音箱传统上蒙猫皮或狗皮，琴颈更细，拨片也更大；它也与冲绳的三线（sanshin）不同，后者是另一件乐器。中文「三味线」是日语「三味線」的转写，与「三弦」只差一字，容易看混。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 三味线（三味線） |
| English | shamisen |
| Italian | shamisen |
| German | Shamisen |
| French | shamisen |

The **shamisen** is Japan's three-string plucked instrument: a slim neck and a square body, struck with a large plectrum (bachi), with a dry, percussive sound heard in kabuki, bunraku and folk song. Its ancestor is the Chinese sanxian, which reached Japan through Ryukyu (the usual account). Italian, German and French have no separate name and use shamisen.

**Watch out**: the shamisen is not the Chinese sanxian — its body is traditionally covered with cat or dog skin, its neck is thinner and its plectrum much larger; it also differs from Okinawa's sanshin, a separate instrument. The Chinese 三味线 transliterates the Japanese 三味線 and sits one character away from 三弦.
