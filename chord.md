::: zh
**和弦（chord）** 是三个或更多音同时发声，或被听成一体。最常见的是三度叠置的三和弦：根音、三音、五音，按三音与根音是大三度还是小三度，分成大、小、减、增四种；再叠一个三度，就成了七和弦。

两个音同时响叫音程，通常不叫和弦——吉他手说的「强力和弦」（根音加五度）是习惯叫法，严格说它只是一个音程，见 [[glossary:interval]]。

和弦可以转位：换掉最低音不改变它是什么和弦，只改变重心与色彩。同一组音，摆在不同的低音上，功能不变而语气变了。

::: en
**A chord** is three or more notes sounding together, or heard as a single unit. The most common kind is the tertian triad: a root, a third and a fifth, which becomes major, minor, diminished or augmented depending on whether the third above the root is major or minor. Stack one more third on top and you have a seventh chord.

Two notes sounding together make an interval, not normally a chord — the guitarist's "power chord" (root plus fifth) is a habit of speech; strictly it is an interval, see [[glossary:interval|interval]].

Chords can be inverted: changing which note is lowest does not change what the chord is, only its weight and colour. The same notes over a different bass keep their function but change their tone.
