::: zh
**自由处理（ad libitum）** 是拉丁语「随其所愿」，通行说法认为它随意大利语音乐术语一起进入谱面，因此常被误认作意大利语。它交给演奏者的是一种**决定权**：速度、节奏、装饰或织体的细节由人判断，谱面只给骨架。

它描述的是**自由度**，不是某个速度值。巴洛克前奏曲、协奏曲华彩段、爵士谱上的 ad lib 都把选择权交给演奏者，但自由仍受风格、和声与合奏约束。**常见误解是把 ad libitum 当成「随便」**：真正的自由处理来自充分准备，而不是不练。

::: en
**Ad libitum** is Latin for "at one's pleasure"; by the usual account it entered musical notation with Italian terminology, which is why it is often taken for Italian. What it hands the player is a **right to decide**: tempo, rhythm, ornament or texture are judged by the performer, while the page supplies only a frame.

It names a **degree of freedom**, not a speed. A Baroque prelude, a concerto cadenza and a jazz chart marked ad lib all give the choice away, yet that freedom is still bounded by style, harmony and the other players. **The common mistake is to hear ad libitum as "anything goes"** — real freedom comes from preparation, not from skipping it.
