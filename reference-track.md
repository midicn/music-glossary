::: zh
**参考曲目（reference track）** 是混音与母带时用来校准的成熟作品：风格、编制或情绪与自己的项目相近，音色平衡、低频量感、人声位置与响度都可作为对照。它像一把尺，防止你在同一间屋子里越听越偏。

关键步骤是先做响度匹配：把参考曲目降到与当前混音相同的 LUFS 再切换，否则更响的那首永远显得更好。比较时只听一个维度——低频、人声亮度、宽度——不要一边听一边大改；参考曲目是方向，不是要复制的模板。

常见误解是参考就是照抄 EQ。每首歌的素材不同，照搬曲线往往水土不服；参考的价值在于判断目标，而不是提供预设。

::: en
A **reference track** is a finished release used to calibrate mixing or mastering: a piece in a similar style, arrangement or mood whose balance, low end, vocal position and loudness can serve as a comparison. It works like a ruler, keeping ears from drifting in one room.

The key is loudness matching: bring the reference down to the same LUFS as your mix before switching, or the louder one will always seem better. Compare one dimension at a time — low end, vocal brightness, width — and resist making large changes while listening. A reference gives direction, not a template.

The usual mistake is to treat referencing as copying EQ curves. Every song has different material, and a borrowed curve often misfits; the value of a reference is the target it clarifies, not a preset it supplies.
