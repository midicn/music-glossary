::: zh
**声部进行（voice leading）**指各声部在和弦之间的横向运动。它关注共同音是否保持、是否优先级进、跳进后是否反向补偿、倾向音是否解决。和弦标记相同而声部进行不同，听感截然不同。

传统规则禁止平行五度与平行八度，也限制外声部的隐伏五八度，以保持各声部独立。转位与重复音的选择常由声部进行决定，而非和弦本身；同一个和弦因此在不同前后文中写法不同。

**声部进行不只是「听起来顺」。** 它解释某些连接为何可行、某些为何别扭。自由织体与流行音乐也讲声部进行，只是规则放宽。逐声部画线比只看和弦符号更容易发现隐藏的平行与跳进。
:::

::: en
**Voice leading** is the horizontal motion of the individual parts from one chord to the next. It asks whether common tones are held, steps preferred, leaps balanced by contrary motion, and tendency tones resolved. The same labels can sound completely different under different voice leading.

Traditional rules forbid parallel fifths and octaves and restrict hidden ones between the outer parts, to keep the voices independent. Inversion and doubling are often decided by voice leading rather than by the chord itself, which is why one chord may be written differently in different contexts.

**Voice leading is more than "what sounds smooth".** It explains why some connections work and others do not. Free textures and popular music have voice leading too, with looser rules. Drawing a line for each part reveals parallels and awkward leaps that chord symbols conceal.
:::
