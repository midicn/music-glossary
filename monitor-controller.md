::: zh
**监听控制器（monitor controller）** 是放在桌面上的**监听中枢**：统一控制监听音量、切换多对音箱、切换单声道/立体声、接对讲与耳机。它让录音师不必在软件里反复找推子。

它和**调音台**不同：监听控制器只管**监听路径**，不处理录音与混音的全部通道；它和**声卡**也不同：声卡负责转换，控制器负责**切换与音量**。**常见误解是「软件里调音量就够了」**——硬件控制器提供**可靠的物理开关与对讲**，在录音现场尤其重要。

::: en
**A monitor controller** is the **monitoring hub** on the desk: it sets monitor level, switches between speaker pairs, toggles mono/stereo, and handles talkback and headphones. It saves hunting for a fader in software.

It differs from a **mixing console**: a controller manages only **the monitoring path**, not every record and mix channel; it differs from an **audio interface** too: the interface converts, the controller **switches and attenuates**. **The usual mistake is to think software volume is enough** — a hardware controller gives **reliable physical switching and talkback**, especially in a session.
