::: zh
**可转位对位（invertible counterpoint）**是两条或更多旋律上下互换位置后仍能合规合奏的对位。上方移到下方、下方移到上方，音程全部倒转：以八度转位为例，三度变六度，五度变四度。

常见的是八度、十度与十二度转位。八度最常用，主题与对题可以互换；十二度会把五度变成八度，需格外小心。巴赫赋格中主题与对题的互换、卡农的声部交换，都建立在这套计算上。

**可转位对位不是旋律倒影。** 倒影改变音程方向，转位改变声部高低；两者可同时使用。判断时看两条线是否交换了位置而仍然协和，而不是看旋律是否上下翻过来。
:::

::: en
**Invertible counterpoint** is counterpoint where two or more lines can exchange their vertical positions and still work. The upper goes below, the lower above, and every interval turns upside down: a third becomes a sixth, a fifth a fourth, a sixth a third. The composer must calculate the inverted intervals and avoid parallel fifths and octaves.

Inversion at the octave, tenth or twelfth is standard; the octave is commonest, letting subject and countersubject swap. Twelfth inversion turns a fifth into an octave and needs care. Bach's fugues, where subject and countersubject exchange, and canons with crossing voices, rest on this arithmetic.

**It is not melodic inversion.** Inversion reverses the direction of intervals; invertibility reverses the register of the voices, and both can operate at once. Listen for two lines swapping and still consonant, not a tune turned upside down.
:::
