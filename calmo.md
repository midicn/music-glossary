::: zh
**平静地（calmo）** 是表情术语：**安静、平稳、不起波澜**。它不规定速度：可以中速，也可以慢速；关键是**脉搏稳定、音色不刺、句尾不塌**。

它和 **tranquillo** 接近，但 tranquillo 更强调「宁静、安详」，calmo 更强调**平稳与自持**。**常见误解是把 calmo 当成「慢」**——它要求的是**稳**；把速度拖慢反而会让音乐失去平静。

::: en
**Calmo** is an expression mark: **calm, even, without disturbance**. It does not fix a tempo: moderate or slow both work; the point is **a steady pulse, an unforced tone and phrase endings that do not sag**.

It is close to **tranquillo**, but tranquillo stresses serenity while calmo stresses **evenness and self-possession**. **The usual mistake is to read calmo as "slow"** — it asks for **steadiness**; dragging the tempo actually makes the music less calm.
