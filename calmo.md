::: zh
**平静地（calmo）**是音乐中的表情记号。

它可出现在不同速度中。平稳的乐句并不要求每个音等强，也不表示可以忽略作品已有的渐强、重音或速度变化。

:::

::: en
**Calmo means calmly.** It is an expression marking in music.

It can occur at different speeds. Calm phrasing does not require identical weight on every note, nor does it cancel written crescendos, accents or tempo changes.
