::: zh
**TrV** 是特伦纳（Franz Trenner）编的施特劳斯作品目录，1985 年初版，1999 年修订。它按创作年代排，号码排到近三百，把习作、改写、配器与未完成稿都算进去；在作品目录里，它是少见的编年体。

编年不等于号码就是日期。条目包含改写与配器，号与号之间可以隔很多年；《英雄生涯》Op. 40 是 TrV 190，《最后四首歌》是 TrV 296，在作曲家身后才出版。同一作品可能同时有 Op. 号与 TrV 号；没有 Op. 号的作品另由 AV 号补充，几套号彼此并不对应。

所以 TrV 适合查「作品在生平哪个位置」，不适合反推年份。**号是条目号，不是日历。**
:::

::: en
**TrV** is Franz Trenner's Richard Strauss catalogue, published in 1985 and revised in 1999. It is arranged chronologically and contains close to three hundred entries, including juvenilia, revisions, arrangements and unfinished projects; among composer catalogues it is unusual in being chronological.

A chronological catalogue still does not make the number a date. Entries include reworkings and orchestrations, so numbers can be years apart, and Ein Heldenleben, Op. 40, is TrV 190 while the Four Last Songs are TrV 296 and appeared only after Strauss died. Many works carry both an opus number and a TrV number; works without an opus also receive an AV number from Mueller von Asow's catalogue, and the systems do not correspond.

TrV is therefore good for locating a work in Strauss's life, not for reading a year off the number. **The number is an entry, not a calendar date.**
:::
