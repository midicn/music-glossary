::: zh
**强（forte，缩写 f）** 是力度记号，表示**响**。它和 piano（弱）相对，是乐谱上最常用的两个力度端点。**f 不是固定的分贝数**：室内乐的 f 与交响乐的 f 完全不在一个声压级；同一件乐器在不同音区，f 的实际响度也不同。

它也不等于「用力」：好的 forte 仍然有音色与控制，不是把声音推到破。**常见误解是把 f 当成音量旋钮的最大档**——力度记号是相对比例，它告诉你「这一段比周围更响」，而不是「响到多少」。

::: en
**Forte (f)** is the dynamic mark for **loud**. It pairs with piano (soft) as the two most common dynamic poles. **F is not a fixed decibel level**: the f of chamber music and the f of a symphony sit at completely different sound pressures, and the same instrument's f differs between registers.

It is not "force" either: a good forte still has colour and control, not a pushed sound on the edge of breaking. **The usual mistake is to read f as the maximum setting of a volume knob** — a dynamic mark is a relative proportion: it says "louder than the surroundings", not "how many decibels".
