::: zh
**对位（counterpoint）** 是几条**各自独立的旋律**同时进行，每一条单听都成立，合起来又彼此咬合。它的核心不是「和声好听」，而是**线条的独立与配合**：一条上行，另一条可以下行；一条停，另一条走。

对位与和声是一体两面：对位关心横向的线条，和声关心纵向的结果。**赋格、卡农、经文歌都是对位的产物**；流行音乐里的副旋律、弦乐四重奏的内声部，也都在做对位。判断对位好不好，先单独听每一条线，再听它们合起来。

::: en
**Counterpoint** is several **independent melodic lines** moving at once, each complete on its own and interlocking with the others. Its core is not "nice harmony" but **the independence and cooperation of lines**: one rises while another falls, one rests while another moves.

Counterpoint and harmony are two sides of one thing: counterpoint cares about horizontal lines, harmony about the vertical result. **Fugue, canon and motet are products of counterpoint**; so are the counter-melody in a pop song and the inner voices of a string quartet. To judge counterpoint, hear each line alone, then hear them together.
