::: zh
**指向性（polar pattern）** 描述麦克风对不同方向来声的灵敏度。全指向对四周较平均，心形偏重正前方并抑制后方，8 字形则前后拾音、侧面抑制；还有超心形、宽心形等变体。

指向性随频率变化，厂商标称的图形通常是若干频率的叠加。离轴声音往往先失去高频，所以合唱、鼓组与立体声摆位中，角度会改变音色而不只是音量。心形与 8 字形在近距离还有近场效应，低频随距离增加而衰减。

常见误解是把指向性当成一个固定数值。实际拾音要同时看频率、距离与房间反射，单看背面的抑制数字容易误判。
:::

::: en
A **polar pattern** describes how sensitive a microphone is to sound arriving from different directions. Omnidirectional picks up fairly evenly around, cardioid favours the front and rejects the rear, and figure-8 hears front and back while rejecting the sides; supercardioid and wide cardioid sit between.

The pattern changes with frequency, and the published drawing usually overlays several frequencies. Off-axis sound often loses high frequencies first, so angle alters timbre, not just level, in choirs, drum setups and stereo placement. Cardioid and figure-8 also show proximity effect, boosting low end as the source moves closer.

The usual mistake is reading a pattern as one fixed number. Real capture depends on frequency, distance and room reflections; a single rear-rejection figure can mislead.
:::
