::: zh
**不太、勿过度（non troppo）** 给前面的术语加一个上限：allegro non troppo 是「快，但别太快」，adagio non troppo 是「慢，但别太慢」。它保留原术语的性格，只削去极端。

这个限定在十九世纪以后尤其常见，勃拉姆斯就常用它来防止演奏者把速度推向炫技。**non troppo 不是慢的同义词**，也不是可以随意打折扣的借口；allegro non troppo 仍然必须是快板，只是拒绝夸张。它与 [[glossary:molto]] 相对：一个加码，一个设限，分寸都要看上下文。
:::

::: en
**Non troppo** puts a ceiling on the mark before it: allegro non troppo is "fast, but not too fast"; adagio non troppo is "slow, but not too slow". It keeps the character of the original term and removes only the extreme.

The qualifier became especially common after the nineteenth century; Brahms used it to stop players from turning tempo into a display. **Non troppo is not a synonym for slow**, nor a licence to discount the mark; allegro non troppo is still an allegro, merely one that refuses exaggeration. It faces molto across the scale: one adds, one limits, and context sets the measure.
:::
