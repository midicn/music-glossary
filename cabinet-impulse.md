::: zh
**箱体脉冲（cabinet impulse，cab IR）** 是用真实**吉他箱体与麦克风**录下的脉冲响应：它记录了这个箱体在某个摆位下的频响与时间特性，卷积回放时就把同样的染色加给干声。

它和**均衡器**不同：EQ 只改频段比例，IR 记录的是**完整的线性响应**（含相位与共振）。常见误解是「IR 可以随便换」——不同箱体、麦克风与摆位的 IR 差别很大；换 IR 等于换箱体，不是换一个 EQ 预设。

::: en
**A cabinet impulse (cab IR)** is an impulse response captured from a **real guitar cabinet and microphone**: it records the cabinet's frequency and time behaviour at a given mic position, and convolution applies that colour to a dry signal.

It differs from an **EQ**: EQ changes the balance of bands, while an IR captures **the complete linear response**, including phase and resonance. The usual mistake is to swap IRs casually — different cabinets, mics and positions differ greatly; changing the IR is changing the cabinet, not loading an EQ preset.
