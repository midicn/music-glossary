## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 口琴 |
| 英语 | harmonica |
| 意大利语 | armonica a bocca |
| 德语 | Mundharmonika |
| 法语 | harmonica |

**口琴** 是含在口中吹吸、使自由簧片振动的小型乐器，常见十孔全音阶与十二孔半音阶两种。英语 **harmonica** 由 harmonic（和声的）加指小后缀构成；意大利语 **armonica a bocca** 直译「口簧」，德语 **Mundharmonika** 同义，法语 **harmonica** 从英语借入。

**常见混淆**：口琴与口风琴（melodica）都靠嘴吹、中文都带「口」，但口琴的簧片在琴身内由气流直接驱动，口风琴是带键盘的吹奏乐器；「口琴」有时泛指口吹簧片乐器，需看语境。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 口琴 |
| English | harmonica |
| Italian | armonica a bocca |
| German | Mundharmonika |
| French | harmonica |

The **harmonica** is a small instrument held to the mouth, where blowing and drawing drive free reeds; the common types are the ten-hole diatonic and the twelve-hole chromatic. English harmonica is formed from harmonic with a diminutive suffix; Italian armonica a bocca literally means "mouth harmonica", German Mundharmonika the same, and French harmonica is borrowed from English.

**Watch out**: harmonica and melodica are both mouth-blown and both carry 口 in Chinese, but the harmonica's reeds sit in the body and respond directly to breath, while the melodica has a keyboard; the short Chinese 口琴 can loosely cover mouth-blown reed instruments, so context matters.
