::: zh
**自由音（free tone）** 是**既不准备、也不级进解决**的非和弦音：它可以直接跳进或跳出，不遵循挂留音或经过音的规则。它在 19 世纪以后的音乐里越来越常见。

它和**倚音**不同：倚音有准备与解决，自由音**不受这套约束**。**常见误解是「自由音就是错音」**——自由音是**有意的放松**；在复杂的和声或旋律里，它制造的是**瞬间的摩擦**，不是事故。

::: en
**A free tone** is a non-chord tone that is **neither prepared nor resolved by step**: it may leap in or out and does not follow the rules of suspension or passing. It becomes more common after the nineteenth century.

It differs from an **appoggiatura**: an appoggiatura has preparation and resolution, a free tone **obeys neither**. **The usual mistake is to hear it as a wrong note** — a free tone is **a deliberate relaxation**; in complex harmony or melody it creates **a moment of friction**, not an accident.
