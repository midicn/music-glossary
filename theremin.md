## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 特雷门琴 |
| 英语 | theremin |
| 意大利语 | theremin |
| 德语 | Theremin |
| 法语 | thérémine |

**特雷门琴** 是最早的电子乐器之一，1920 年由俄国物理学家列夫·捷尔明（Léon Theremin）发明；意大利语写作 theremin，德语写作 Theremin，法语作 thérémine。它靠两个高频振荡器的差频发声：右手靠近垂直天线改变音高，左手靠近环形天线改变音量，音高连续，既没有品也没有键。

**常见混淆**：演奏特雷门琴不用接触琴体——手与天线之间的电容变化就是全部控制，这也是它最容易被误解的地方；它常被误认为靠光线或接近开关控制，其实原理是电容与差频，与光学无关。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 特雷门琴 |
| English | theremin |
| Italian | theremin |
| German | Theremin |
| French | thérémine |

The **theremin** is one of the earliest electronic instruments, invented in 1920 by the Russian physicist Léon Theremin; Italian writes theremin, German Theremin, French thérémine. It sounds through the difference between two high-frequency oscillators: the right hand near the vertical antenna sets pitch, the left hand near the loop sets volume, and pitch is continuous, with no frets and no keys.

**Watch out**: playing a theremin involves no contact at all — capacitance between hand and antenna is the whole control, which is also what gets misunderstood; it is not controlled by light or a proximity switch, despite the gestures.
