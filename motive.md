::: zh
**动机（motive，也写 motif）**是可辨认的短小音乐材料，可以突出节奏、音程轮廓或两者的组合。贝多芬《第五交响曲》开头的“三短一长”是常用例子：后续段落即使改变音高或配器，仍可能保留这个节奏特征。

分析动机时，先指出哪些特征被保留，再说明哪些发生变化，例如移调、时值扩大或缩小、轮廓倒置、节拍位置移动。短小并不意味着固定音数，也不能单凭材料重复了几次就判断它的重要性；要看它怎样参与乐句和段落的组织。
:::

::: en
**A motive (or motif)** is a short, identifiable musical unit, recognised through rhythm, intervallic contour or both. The short–short–short–long pattern at the opening of Beethoven’s Fifth Symphony is a familiar example: later passages can retain the rhythm while changing pitch or scoring.

In analysis, identify the retained features before naming the changes: transposition, longer or shorter durations, inversion of contour or metric displacement. There is no fixed number of notes that makes a motive, and the number of repetitions alone does not establish its structural importance.
:::
