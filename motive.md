::: zh
**动机（motive）**是音乐里最短的、还能被认出来的单位：几个音、一个节奏型，或两者的结合。它不必是完整旋律，常常只有两三个音，却有自己的轮廓与性格。贝多芬《第五交响曲》开头那四个音——三短一长、下行三度——就是最著名的例子：短到能一口记住，又强到能撑起整首交响曲。

动机的价值在于**变形**。它可以移调、扩大、缩小、逆行、倒影；可以只保留节奏而改音高，也可以只保留音高而改节奏；还可以被拆开，只取其中一格。作曲家让同一个动机在不同声部、不同调性、不同音区反复出现，听众未必逐次认出，却会感到全曲同源。**动机像种子：本身不是植物，却决定了后来长成什么。**

常见误解是把动机当成「短旋律」，以为越长、越完整才越重要。实际上动机恰恰靠短小才便于变形：长旋律改一个音就会面目全非，动机改几个音仍认得出来。另一个误解是以为动机必须原样重复；真正的动机往往每次出现都换了衣服，只留下一个可辨认的骨架。**判断它是不是动机，看它能否在变化中保持身份，而不看它出现了几次。**
:::

::: en
**A motive** is the shortest unit in music that can still be recognised: a few notes, a rhythmic figure, or a combination of both. It need not be a complete melody; often it is only two or three notes, yet it has a contour and a character of its own. The opening of Beethoven's Fifth Symphony — three short notes and one long, falling by a third — is the famous example: short enough to grasp at once, strong enough to carry an entire symphony.

A motive matters because it can be **transformed**. It may be transposed, augmented, diminished, played backwards or inverted; it may keep its rhythm and change its pitches, or keep its pitches and change its rhythm; it may also be broken up so that only one cell survives. A composer can send the same motive through different voices, keys and registers, and listeners need not recognise every return to feel that the whole work grows from one source. **A motive is like a seed: not the plant itself, but the thing that decides what the plant can become.**

The common misconception is to treat a motive as a short melody and assume that a longer, more complete shape is more important. In fact a motive is useful precisely because it is short: change one note of a long tune and it collapses, while a motive can lose several notes and still be recognised. Nor must a motive repeat unchanged; it usually returns in new clothes, leaving only a recognisable frame. **Whether something is a motive depends on whether it keeps its identity through change, not on how often it is repeated.**
