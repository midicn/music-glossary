::: zh
**雇佣作品（work for hire）** 是版权法里的一种安排：在**雇佣关系**或**明确约定的委托创作**中，作品的版权**直接归雇主或委托方**，而不是创作者。它和「转让版权」不同——转让是先有权利再转手，雇佣作品是**从一开始就归对方**。

**常见误解是「我写的就一定是我的」**：合同里的 work-for-hire 条款会改变归属；不同国家对委托创作的规定也不同。**看合同先找这三个词：work for hire、assignment、ownership。**

::: en
**Work for hire** is a copyright arrangement: in an **employment relationship** or a **specially commissioned work** with the right wording, copyright belongs **directly to the employer or commissioner**, not the creator. It differs from an assignment — an assignment transfers an existing right, work for hire belongs to the other side **from the start**.

**The usual mistake is to think "I wrote it, so it is mine"**: a work-for-hire clause changes ownership, and commissioned-work rules differ by country. **Look for three words in the contract: work for hire, assignment, ownership.**
