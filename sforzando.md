::: zh
**突强（sforzando）** 是在一个音或和弦上**突然加力**，谱面写 sfz 或 sf。它靠极快的音头：钢琴上手臂重量在一瞬间落下，弦乐上弓速与弓压同时增加，管乐上用一个硬舌起音。

音色上，它带来一个**尖锐、明亮的起音**，随后声音按乐器本身的规律衰减；所以 sfz 是一个「形状」，不是把整拍都轰响。**常见误解是「sforzando 就是更响」**：它常出现在弱奏的句子里，总音量并不大，突出的只是那一瞬间的爆发；与它相比，rinforzando 更像把音加强并撑住。
:::

::: en
**Sforzando** puts a **sudden force** on a single note or chord, written sfz or sf. It depends on an extremely fast onset: arm weight dropping in an instant on the piano, bow speed and pressure together on strings, a hard tongue stroke in wind playing.

The colour is a **sharp, bright attack**, after which the sound decays according to the instrument; sfz is therefore a shape, not a whole beat turned up. **The usual mistake is "sforzando means louder"**: it often occurs inside a soft passage, where the total volume stays modest and only the burst stands out. Compared with it, rinforzando is better heard as a reinforced, sustained push.
:::
