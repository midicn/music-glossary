::: zh
**音箱模拟（amp simulator）** 是用软件（或硬件）**模拟吉他音箱**的声音：前级增益、均衡、后级与箱体的染色，全都可以用算法或脉冲响应重建。它让录音不必把音箱开到很大声。

它和**箱体脉冲（cab IR）**不同：amp sim 通常包含**前级与后级**，cab IR 只负责箱体与麦克风的频响。常见误解是「箱模就是失真效果」——失真是其中一环；箱模的价值在于整套信号链的染色与动态。

:::

::: en
**An amp simulator** uses software (or hardware) to **imitate a guitar amplifier**: preamp gain, EQ, power stage and cabinet colouring can be rebuilt with algorithms or impulse responses. It lets you record without pushing a real amp loud.

It differs from a **cabinet impulse (cab IR)**: an amp sim usually includes **preamp and power stage**, while a cab IR handles only the cabinet-and-microphone response. The usual mistake is to equate it with a distortion effect — distortion is one stage; the value is the colour and dynamics of the whole chain.
