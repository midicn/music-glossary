::: zh
**领谱（lead sheet）** 是爵士与流行最常用的工作谱：一行旋律加和弦符号，必要时标段落字母（A、B）或曲风，不写伴奏声部。

它把「演什么」与「怎么演」分开：旋律与和声框架是确定的，节奏组的织体、转位与加音留给演奏者。同一张领谱，钢琴三重奏与独奏钢琴可以奏出很不同的版本。

别把和弦符号当成固定音型：符号只规定和声骨架，具体排列要看风格与编制。领谱也不等于和弦谱，后者通常连旋律都省掉。
:::

::: en
A **lead sheet** is the working score of jazz and popular music: one melody line with chord symbols, plus section letters or style words when needed, and no written accompaniment.

It separates what to play from how to play it. The melody and harmonic frame are fixed; texture, inversions and added notes are left to the performers, so a trio and a solo pianist can make very different versions of the same sheet.

Do not read chord symbols as fixed patterns. They define a harmonic frame, and voicing follows style and ensemble. A lead sheet is not a chord chart either: the latter usually omits the melody.
:::
