::: zh
**排练记号（rehearsal mark）** 是谱面上方的字母、数字或方框标记，让指挥一句话就能把乐队带到某处。

它按乐曲段落编排，而不按固定小节数；总谱与分谱上的位置必须完全一致，通常与谱号、拍号或段落的转折对齐。

排练记号不是小节号：小节号连续计数，排练记号可以跳号，也可以用 A1、B2 细分。改编或删节后要重编，否则旧录音与旧谱会对不上。
:::

::: en
**A rehearsal mark** is a letter, number or boxed letter above the staff that lets a conductor take an ensemble to a given point in one phrase.

Marks follow the sections of a work rather than a fixed count of bars. Their positions must match exactly in score and parts, and they usually align with a change of clef, metre or texture.

A rehearsal mark is not a bar number. Bar numbers run continuously, while rehearsal marks may skip letters and may be subdivided as A1 or B2. After cuts or reorchestration they must be renumbered, or old recordings and old parts will disagree.
:::
