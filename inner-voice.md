::: zh
**内声部（inner voice）** 是夹在**主旋律与低音之间**的声部。它不常被听见，却决定和声的**厚度、连接与色彩**：同一段旋律与低音，内声部换一种安排，音乐就从空洞变饱满。

它和副旋律不同：副旋律要被听见，内声部更多是**支撑**。常见误解是「内声部可以随便填」——内声部的平行五度、声部交错与跳进，往往比外声部更容易毁掉一段和声。

::: en
**An inner voice** sits **between the main melody and the bass**. It is rarely noticed, yet it decides the harmony's **thickness, connection and colour**: the same melody and bass sound hollow or full depending on how the inner parts are arranged.

It differs from a counter-melody: a counter-melody wants to be heard, an inner voice mostly **supports**. The usual mistake is to fill it with anything — parallel fifths, voice crossing and leaps in an inner part can ruin a progression more easily than in an outer voice.
