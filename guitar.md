## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 吉他 |
| 英语 | guitar |
| 意大利语 | chitarra |
| 德语 | Gitarre |
| 法语 | guitare |

**吉他** 是拨弦乐器，古典吉他用尼龙弦，民谣与电吉他用钢弦。中文「吉他」是音译；英语 guitar 经西班牙语 guitarra 来自希腊语 kithara——**一个名字走了两千年，指的早已不是同一件乐器**。

**常见混淆**：classical guitar 与 acoustic guitar 在中文里都叫「吉他」，但弦材、音色与演奏传统不同；「六弦琴」是旧称，今天多指民谣吉他。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 吉他 |
| English | guitar |
| Italian | chitarra |
| German | Gitarre |
| French | guitare |

The **guitar** is a plucked instrument: the classical guitar uses nylon strings, the acoustic and electric use steel. The Chinese name is a transliteration; English guitar came through Spanish guitarra from Greek kithara — **one name has travelled two thousand years and no longer names the same instrument**.

**Watch out**: classical and acoustic guitars are both 吉他 in Chinese, but differ in strings, colour and tradition; 六弦琴 is an older term, now often meaning the steel-strung acoustic.
