::: zh
**吸声（absorption）** 是把入射声能转成微小热量的处理，用来减少反射、压低混响。多孔材料如玻璃棉、矿棉、厚窗帘对中高频有效；厚度决定它能延伸到多低的频率。

实际布置多在第一反射点、天花与墙角：太薄的海绵只吃掉高频，会让声音发闷、低频比例反而上升。吸声面积与位置比材料品牌重要，左右墙尽量对称。

常见误解是把吸声当隔音。吸声管的是房间内部的声音，邻居听到多少取决于墙体质量与隔声结构，两者不是一回事。
:::

::: en
**Absorption** turns incoming acoustic energy into a little heat, reducing reflections and shortening reverb. Porous materials such as mineral wool, glass fibre and heavy curtains work on mid and high frequencies; their thickness sets how far down they remain effective.

Placement usually targets first reflection points, the ceiling and corners. Thin foam removes only the top end, leaving a dull sound with proportionally more bass. Total area and position matter more than the brand, and left and right walls should stay symmetrical.

The usual mistake is confusing absorption with soundproofing. Absorption governs sound inside the room; how much neighbours hear depends on wall mass and isolation construction.
:::
