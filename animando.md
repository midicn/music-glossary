::: zh
**animando（活跃起来）** 是「逐渐变得有生气」的指示：它既可能指**性格**更活跃，也可能带来**轻微的加速**。它和 animato（活跃地）的区别是「正在变得」而不是「已经如此」。

它和 **con moto** 接近，但 animando 更强调生命感，不只是推进。常见误解是把它当成一个固定速度——animando 是一个过程；听的是音乐有没有「醒过来」，而不是 BPM 变了多少。

:::

::: en
**Animando** means "becoming more animated": it can mean a **more lively character**, and may bring **a slight quickening**. It differs from animato ("animated") in being **in the process of becoming**, not already there.

It is close to con moto, but animando stresses life rather than mere drive. The usual mistake is to read it as a fixed tempo — it is a process; listen for whether the music wakes up, not for how much the BPM moves.
