::: zh
**开放排列（open position）** 是把和弦音**拉开到更宽的音区**：低音与高音之间距离大，内声部之间留出空隙。声音更通透、更有空间感，每个音的独立性更强。

它和**密集排列**相对：开放排列牺牲了融合，换来了**清晰与呼吸**。常见误解是「拉得越开越好」——拉得太开，和弦会散成几个不相干的点；好的开放排列仍然让听者听出一个和弦。

::: en
**Open position** spreads a chord's notes **across a wider register**: the bass and top are far apart, with gaps between the inner parts. The sound is clearer and more spacious, and each note is more independent.

It contrasts with **close position**: open voicing sacrifices blend for **clarity and air**. The usual mistake is to spread everything as far as possible — too wide and the chord falls into unrelated points; a good open voicing still lets the ear hear one chord.
