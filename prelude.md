::: zh
**前奏曲（prelude）** 最初是套曲或组曲前面的引子：试音、定调，把耳朵带进后面的乐章。巴洛克时期，前奏曲常与赋格成对，即兴式的音型为严谨的对位做准备。

十九世纪，前奏曲脱离引子身份独立成篇。肖邦的二十四首前奏曲每首只写一种情绪，长度从半分钟到数分钟不等；德彪西、拉赫玛尼诺夫延续了这种短篇集的写法。

**判断前奏曲，看它是否还有「后面」**：作为引子时，它服从后面的乐章；独立成篇时，它自己完成起承转合。听成套的前奏曲，留意调性排列与性格对照，而不是只挑熟悉的几首。

::: en
**A prelude** began as an introduction before a suite or a set of pieces: testing the instrument, establishing the key, leading the ear into what follows. In the Baroque era preludes were often paired with fugues, their improvisatory figures preparing for strict counterpoint.

In the nineteenth century the prelude left its introductory role and became a complete piece. Chopin's twenty-four preludes each hold a single mood and run from half a minute to several minutes; Debussy and Rachmaninoff continued the idea of a short collection.

**To judge a prelude, ask whether anything follows it.** As an introduction it serves the movement after it; standing alone, it must complete its own departure and return. In a set, attend to the key scheme and the contrast of characters rather than picking out the familiar few.
:::
