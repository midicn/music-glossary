::: zh
**重升号（double sharp）** 是升号的变体记号，把音升高两个半音，也就是一个全音：重升 F 等于 G，重升 C 等于 D。

它多出现在升号调与变化和声里：G♯ 小调的导音是重升 F，D♯ 大调的升五级是重升 A；重升让「导音」「升五级」这类音级名保持可读，而不是改写成另一个音名。

别把重升看成两个升号：它是一个记号、一次升高全音，且在本小节内对同一音高有效。等音写法（重升 F 与 G）在键盘上同键，但功能不同；分析时若改写成 G，导音的倾向就消失了。
:::

::: en
A **double sharp** is a variant of the sharp sign; it raises a note by two semitones, a whole tone: F double sharp equals G, C double sharp equals D.

It is common in sharp keys and chromatic harmony. The leading note of G♯ minor is F double sharp, and the raised fifth of D♯ major is A double sharp. Double sharps keep labels such as leading note or raised fifth readable instead of switching to another letter.

Do not see two separate sharps. It is one sign, one whole-tone rise, valid at that pitch for the rest of the bar. F double sharp and G share a key but not a function; respelling as G erases the leading-note tendency.
:::
