## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 簧风琴 |
| 英语 | harmonium |
| 意大利语 | armonium |
| 德语 | Harmonium |
| 法语 | harmonium |

**簧风琴** 是脚踏风箱供气的自由簧键盘乐器：气流让金属簧片振动，音量随踏板力度起伏。它诞生于十九世纪的欧洲，后来在印度成为教堂、学校与家庭常用的键盘乐器，被称作「印度风琴」。英语、德语、法语都写作 harmonium，意大利语作 armonium，只差一个字母。

**常见混淆**：簧风琴不是管风琴。管风琴靠管内空气柱发声，体积与建筑绑定；簧风琴靠簧片，箱子小、可搬动，声音带明显的簧片颤音。它也不是手风琴——手风琴用肩带与双臂拉风箱，簧风琴放在地上用脚踏板。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 簧风琴 |
| English | harmonium |
| Italian | armonium |
| German | Harmonium |
| French | harmonium |

The **harmonium** is a free-reed keyboard instrument fed by foot bellows: air sets metal reeds vibrating, and the volume follows the pumping. It appeared in nineteenth-century Europe and later became a common keyboard in Indian churches, schools and homes, where it is called the "Indian harmonium". English, German and French all write harmonium; Italian uses armonium, one letter apart.

**Watch out**: a harmonium is not a pipe organ. The organ sounds from air columns in pipes and is tied to a building; the harmonium sounds from reeds, travels in a box, and carries a clear reed vibrato. Nor is it an accordion: the accordion is pumped by straps and arms, while the harmonium sits on the floor and is pumped by foot.
