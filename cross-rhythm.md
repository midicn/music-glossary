::: zh
**交错节奏（cross-rhythm）**是一个声部的重音分组与既有节拍或另一声部不一致，例如在 4/4 中持续 3+3+2，或让 6/8 与 3/4 的脉搏叠置。听者会同时感到两套分组，原有节拍仍在，却被另一套重音拉扯。

这种手法可以只持续一小节，也可以贯穿整段。重音、时值长短与音高重复都能标记另一套分组；在流行与非洲音乐中，它常是律动的核心，而非偶发的错拍。

**并非所有切分都是交错节奏。** 切分移动个别重音，交错节奏则建立另一套稳定的分组轮廓；它也与复节奏相关但不相同，复节奏是不同等分同时进行，交错节奏是分组冲突，单声部也能成立。
:::

::: en
**A cross-rhythm** sets the accent grouping of one voice against the prevailing metre or another voice, as when 4/4 carries a repeated 3+3+2, or when 6/8 and 3/4 pulses are superimposed. The listener feels two groupings at once: the original metre is still there, but another accent pattern pulls against it.

The device may last a single bar or run through a whole section. Accents, note lengths and pitch repetition can all mark the second grouping; in popular and West African music it is often the core of the groove, not an accidental mistake.

**Not every syncopation is a cross-rhythm.** Syncopation displaces individual accents, while a cross-rhythm establishes another steady grouping. It is related to polyrhythm but not the same: polyrhythm sets different equal divisions together, whereas cross-rhythm is a conflict of grouping and can exist in one voice alone.
:::
