::: zh
**母带（mastering）** 是混音完成后的最后一道工序：把一首或整张专辑处理成可发行的成品，统一音色与响度、安排曲目间隔、生成交付格式。它站在作品与发行之间，既要忠实于混音，也要保证在不同系统上站得住。

常见处理包括小幅均衡、总线压缩、限幅与响度归一化；更关键的是判断——低频是否过多、人声是否靠前、与参考曲目相比是否偏亮。母带工程师拿到立体声混音或分轨，在专门校准的房间里工作；响度目标按平台而定。

常见误解是母带能让糟糕的混音起死回生。母带只能在混音给定的范围内微调；如果混音失衡，母带只会把问题固化。

::: en
**Mastering** is the final stage after mixing: turning one track or a whole album into a release, matching tone and loudness, setting track spacing and producing delivery formats. It sits between the work and its release, owing fidelity to the mix while making sure it holds up on systems.

The work includes small EQ moves, bus compression, limiting and loudness normalization, but the real value is judgement: whether the low end is too heavy, the vocal too far forward, the top too bright next to a reference. Engineers receive a stereo mix or stems and work in a calibrated room; loudness targets depend on the platform.

The usual mistake is to expect mastering to rescue a bad mix. It can refine what the mix provides; if the mix is unbalanced, mastering fixes the problem in place.
