::: zh
**转调（modulation）** 是把音乐的**中心**从一个调移到另一个调，并且让听众感到「已经到了」。它不是临时经过一个和弦，而是**新的主音建立了引力**：新调的属和弦解决到新调的主和弦，终止式确认了搬家。

转调常靠**共同和弦**（pivot chord）过渡：一个和弦同时属于旧调与新调，音乐借它转身。常见误解是把「出现一个变化音」当成转调——那可能只是副属和弦或临时主音化，中心并没有真的移动。判断转调，听新主音有没有站住。

::: en
**Modulation** moves the music's **centre** from one key to another and makes the arrival felt. It is not a passing chord but **a new tonic establishing gravity**: the new dominant resolves to the new tonic, and a cadence confirms the move.

Modulation often turns on a **pivot chord** shared by the old and new keys. The usual mistake is to hear one accidental as a modulation — it may only be a secondary dominant or a tonicization, with the centre never really moving. To hear a modulation, listen for the new tonic to stand.
