::: zh
三全音替代（tritone substitution）是把属七和弦换成根音相距三全音的另一个属七和弦。比如 C 大调里用 D♭7 代替 G7：两者共享同一个三全音，只是三音与七音互换身份。

替代后低音常从降二级半音下行到目标和弦的根音，声部连接更平滑。它在爵士里极常见，也用于副属和弦的替换；记号仍按新根音写，功能上却保留原属和弦的解决方向。

它和降二级那不勒斯和弦不同：后者是大三和弦，通常作为前属；三全音替代是属七和弦，直接承担属功能。两者都以降二级为根音，和弦性质与解决方式却不同。
:::

::: en
Tritone substitution replaces a dominant seventh with another dominant seventh whose root lies a tritone away. In C major, D♭7 may take the place of G7: the two share the same tritone, but the third and seventh swap roles.

The substitute bass often falls by a semitone to the root of its target chord, which smooths the voice leading. The device is common in jazz and also works on secondary dominants; the symbol takes the new root, while the function keeps the old direction of resolution.

It differs from the Neapolitan chord on the flat second: that is a major triad, usually a predominant, whereas a tritone substitute is a dominant seventh acting as the dominant. Both put a flat second in the bass, but their quality and resolution differ.
:::
