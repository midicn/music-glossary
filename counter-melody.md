::: zh
**副旋律（counter-melody）** 是与主旋律**同时进行**、又能独立成句的第二条旋律。它和普通伴奏不同：伴奏可以只是和弦或节奏，副旋律却有**自己的轮廓与呼吸**，只是音量与音区通常让位于主旋律。

它和**对位**的关系是：副旋律是对位思维在主调音乐里的日常形态——一条主旋律加一条可唱的第二线。常见误解是把副旋律当成「加花」：加花依附主旋律，副旋律则要自己站得住。

::: en
**A counter-melody** is a second line running **at the same time as the main melody** and able to stand as a phrase of its own. It differs from ordinary accompaniment: accompaniment may be only chords or rhythm, while a counter-melody has **its own contour and breathing**, usually placed lower in volume or register.

Its relation to **counterpoint** is that a counter-melody is contrapuntal thinking in everyday homophonic music — one main tune plus a second line you could sing. The usual mistake is to treat it as decoration: decoration depends on the main melody, a counter-melody must stand by itself.
