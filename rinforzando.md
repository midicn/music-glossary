::: zh
**加强（rinforzando）** 写 rfz 或 rf，意思是在某个音或某一小段上**加一把力**，让它从前后文中凸出来。它可以只覆盖一个音，也可以覆盖几个音；演奏时音量、弓压或气息略增，但不像 sfz 那样要求一记猛击。

音色上，它更像**加厚、向前推**，起音不一定尖锐，声音的「芯」更实。**常见误解是把 rfz 与 sfz 当成完全同义**：不少谱子里两者确实混用，但通行说法是 rinforzando 偏「加强一段」，sforzando 偏「突出一击」；遇到具体作品还要看上下文与版本。
:::

::: en
**Rinforzando**, written rfz or rf, means **adding force** to a note or a short passage so that it stands out from its surroundings. It may cover one note or several; volume, bow pressure or breath increase, but it does not demand the single blow of an sfz.

In colour it is more a **thickening, a push forward** than a sharp attack; the core of the sound becomes firmer. **The usual mistake is to treat rfz and sfz as exact synonyms**: many scores do use them interchangeably, but the common view is that rinforzando reinforces a passage while sforzando strikes one moment — check the context and the edition.
:::
