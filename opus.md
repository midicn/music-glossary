::: zh
**Op.**（opus，复数 opera）是拉丁语的「作品」，也是出版商给一部或一组作品的编号。它通常在出版时分配：贝多芬的 Op. 18 是一组六首弦乐四重奏，引用其中一首要加 No.，写成 Op. 18 No. 1。

关键在「出版」不等于「创作」。早年写下的曲子可以很晚才印，拿到很大的号；出版次序与写作次序并不一致。作曲家去世后才发行的作品常标 Op. posth.，同一部作品由不同出版商发行，也会带上不同的 Op. 号。

所以 Op. 既不是年表，也不是全集目录，它只记录「第几批送去印」。贝多芬的 Op. 1 是三首钢琴三重奏，Op. 135 是最后一首四重奏——**编号跟出版走，不跟人生走。**

::: en
**Op.** (opus, plural opera) is Latin for "work", and in practice the publisher's number for a work or a set. It is usually assigned when the music is printed: Beethoven's Op. 18 is a set of six string quartets, and one of them is cited as Op. 18 No. 1.

The key is that publication is not composition. A piece written early may appear late and receive a high number; publication order and composition order do not match. Works issued after the composer's death are often marked Op. posth., and different publishers can give the same piece different opus numbers.

So Op. is neither a chronology nor a complete catalogue; it records a batch sent to the printer. Beethoven's Op. 1 is a set of three piano trios, Op. 135 his last quartet — **the number follows publication, not the life.**
