::: zh
**平行进行（parallel motion）** 是两个声部**同向移动且保持同一音程**。三度、六度的平行很常见，能让和声变得厚实；**五度与八度的平行**在传统对位里则要避免，因为它们会让两个声部听起来像一条线被加厚。

它和**同向进行**的差别是：同向只看方向，平行还要求音程不变。常见误解是「平行五八度绝对禁止」——在平行主义、印象派与流行编配里，它们是合法的色彩；规则取决于风格，不是物理定律。

::: en
**Parallel motion** has two voices moving **in the same direction while keeping the same interval**. Parallel thirds and sixths are common and thicken the harmony; **parallel fifths and octaves** are avoided in traditional counterpoint because the two parts stop sounding independent and become one line doubled.

Its difference from **similar motion**: similar looks only at direction, parallel also requires the interval to stay. **The usual mistake is to treat parallel fifths and octaves as absolutely forbidden — in planing, Impressionism and pop arranging they are legitimate colour; the rule depends on style, not physics.**
