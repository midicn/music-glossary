::: zh
**恰空（chaconne）** 是在一个**反复的低音或和声型**上做变奏的曲式，通常三拍子、速度从容。它和帕萨卡利亚常被混用：两者都靠固定低音与变奏展开，区别在历史用法与重音位置，而非一刀切。

它和**主题与变奏**不同：变奏的主题在**低音/和声**，不是一条完整旋律。**常见误解是「恰空就是帕萨卡利亚」**——在巴洛克实践中两者边界模糊，但分析时最好说明你指的是哪一种。

::: en
**A chaconne** is a set of variations over a **repeated bass or harmonic pattern**, usually in triple time and at a measured pace. It is often confused with the passacaglia: both build on a ground and variations, and the difference lies in historical usage and accent rather than a hard line.

It differs from **theme and variations**: the variation frame is **the bass or the harmony**, not a complete melody. **The usual mistake is to treat chaconne and passacaglia as identical** — in Baroque practice the border is blurred, but analysis should say which one it means.
