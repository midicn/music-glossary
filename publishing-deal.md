::: zh
**出版协议（publishing deal）** 是词曲作者把作品的出版权全部或部分转让、或委托给出版方的合同。它决定**版权份额、预付、版税比例与期限**，也决定出版方是否承担推广、登记与维权。

它常与**共同出版**或**管理协议**混淆：前者分版权，后者只代管、不取得份额。常见误解是「签出版约等于有人替你宣传」：推广义务要写进合同，否则可能只剩收钱与登记。通识说明，不构成法律意见。

::: en
**A publishing deal** is a contract in which a songwriter assigns all or part of a work's publishing rights to a publisher, or engages one to exploit them. It sets **the copyright share, advance, royalty rate and term**, and whether the publisher handles promotion, registration and enforcement.

It is often confused with **co-publishing** or an **administration deal**: the first divides copyright, the second only manages it without taking a share. The usual mistake is to assume a publishing deal guarantees promotion: put promotion duties in writing, or the deal may amount to collection and registration only. This is general information, not legal advice.
