::: zh
**调式（mode）** 是一组音高，加上一个中心音，以及一套使用它们的规则。光有一组音不够：C 大调与 A 自然小调用的是同一批音，但把中心放在 C 还是 A，音乐的重力方向就不同。

西方教会调式（多里亚、弗里吉亚、利底亚等）是大调小调之外的七种安排；印度的 raga、阿拉伯的 maqam 也常被译作「调式」，但它们连上行下行、装饰音与情绪都规定，比音阶更接近一套语法。

常见误解：把调式与音阶当同义词。音阶只说「有哪些音、怎么排」，调式还说「哪个音是家」——家在哪，见 [[glossary:tonality]]。

::: en
**A mode** is a set of pitches plus a centre plus a set of rules for using them. A mere collection of notes is not enough: C major and A natural minor use the same notes, but put the centre on C or on A and the music's gravity points elsewhere.

The Western church modes (Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian and the rest) are seven arrangements beyond major and minor. India's raga and the Arab maqam are often translated as "mode" too, but they also govern ascending and descending lines, ornament and mood — closer to a grammar than to a scale.

The usual mistake is to treat mode and scale as synonyms. A scale says which notes exist and in what order; a mode adds which note is home — see [[glossary:tonality|tonality]].
