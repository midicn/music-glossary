::: zh
**调性（tonality）** 是一首曲子围绕一个中心音建立的引力：其他音按远近排开，主和弦最稳，属和弦最想回到主。说一首曲子「在 C 大调」，是同时指定了中心音（C）和调式（大调）；小调同理，两者合起来才是一次完整的定位，见 [[glossary:mode]]。

调号不等于调性：C 大调与 A 小调共用没有升降号的调号，要靠中心和终止式才能分开；一首曲子还可以中途转调，把中心搬到另一个音上。

常见误解：把调性与音阶混为一谈。音阶是材料，调性是材料之间的等级关系——没有中心的七个音，只是七个音。

::: en
**Tonality** is the pull a piece builds around a central note: the other notes arrange themselves by distance from it, the tonic chord feels most settled, the dominant most eager to return home. To say a piece is "in C major" names both a centre (C) and a mode (major); minor works the same way, and only the two together give a full address — see [[glossary:mode|mode]].

A key signature is not a key: C major and A minor share the signature with no sharps or flats, and only the centre and the cadences tell them apart. A piece can also modulate, moving its centre to another note mid-way.

The usual mistake is to equate tonality with a scale. A scale is the material; tonality is the hierarchy among that material — seven notes with no centre are just seven notes.
