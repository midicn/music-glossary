::: zh
**缩混（mixdown）** 是把多轨素材合成两声道或多声道成品的过程：调整每轨的音量、声像、均衡、动态与效果，让各元素在频谱与空间里各就各位。它既是技术活，也是审美判断——决定听众先听到什么、什么被藏在后面。

工作顺序通常是先定鼓与贝斯的低频骨架，再放人声或主奏，然后补和声与色彩，最后用自动化做段落起伏。混音时要在音箱、耳机与小喇叭之间切换；总线上留出余量，导出前确认采样率、位深与起始点。

常见误解是缩混就是导出。导出只是最后一步，缩混是无数次取舍。另一个误解是越响越好——响度留给母带，混音阶段先把平衡做对。

::: en
**Mixdown** is summing tracks into a stereo or multichannel master: setting each track's level, pan, EQ, dynamics and effects so every element finds its place in spectrum and space. It is craft and taste — deciding what the listener hears first and what stays behind.

The usual order is to fix the low-end skeleton of drums and bass, place the vocal or lead, add harmony and colour, then use automation for movement. Switch between speakers, headphones and a small system; leave headroom on the bus, and confirm sample rate, bit depth and start point before export.

The usual mistake is to think mixdown means export. Export is the last step; the real work is choices. Another is making it as loud as possible — loudness belongs to mastering, while the mix should get the balance right first.
