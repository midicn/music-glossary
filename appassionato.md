::: zh
**热情地（appassionato）** 来自意大利语 passione「激情」，其拉丁词根 passio 原意是「承受、受苦」。它要求演奏带**灼热的情感投入**：线条有方向，重音有理由，音色偏浓。

它说的是**热度**，不是速度，也不是音量。appassionato 可以很轻，只要句子内部绷紧；也可以在中速上成立。**常见误解是把 appassionato 弹成「最响、最慢、揉弦最多」**——堆料只会让热情变成吵闹，真正的情感来自句子重心的安排。

::: en
**Appassionato** comes from the Italian passione, "passion", whose Latin root passio first meant "suffering". It asks for **heated emotional commitment**: a line with direction, accents with a reason, a fuller colour.

It speaks of **heat**, not of speed or volume. Appassionato can be quiet as long as the phrase is taut inside, and it works at a moderate tempo. **The usual mistake is to play it as "loudest, slowest, heaviest vibrato"** — piling on makes passion noisy; real feeling comes from where the phrase puts its weight.
