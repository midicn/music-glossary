::: zh
**热情、充满激情地（appassionato）**是音乐中的表情记号。

可从乐句的推进、重音和动态对比理解。它没有固定的速度或音量要求；改变速度、延长音值或增加揉弦，应有谱面与风格依据。

:::

::: en
**Appassionato means passionately.** It is an expression marking in music.

It can be understood through phrase direction, accent and dynamic contrast. It does not fix a tempo or volume. Changes of speed, lengthening of notes or added vibrato should be supported by the score and style.
