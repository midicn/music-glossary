::: zh
**出版权分割（publishing split）** 是一首**词曲**的版权份额在作者与出版方之间怎么分。一首歌可能有多位作者，每个人的份额要在**分割协议**里写清楚；出版方通常按合同取得一部分或代理全部。

它管的是**词曲**，不是录音：录音的分成（master split）是另一份协议。常见误解是「谁写的谁就全拿」——署名与份额不是一回事；合作写作、雇佣写作、采样与改编都可能改变份额。没有写下来的分割，等于把未来的争议留给运气。

::: en
**A publishing split** is how the **composition** rights in a song are divided between writers and publishers. A song may have several writers, and each share must be set down in a **split agreement**; a publisher usually takes a share or administers the whole under contract.

It covers the **composition**, not the recording: the recording's share (the master split) is a separate agreement. The usual mistake is to think "whoever wrote it takes all" — credit and share are not the same thing; co-writing, work-for-hire, sampling and adaptation can all change the split. A split that is not written down leaves the future to luck.
