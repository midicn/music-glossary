::: zh
**宫调** 是中国传统音乐理论里「调高」与「调式」的合称：宫是调高的基准，调是调式的中心。同一个宫可配不同调式，同一个调式也可旋宫移位，即「旋宫转调」。

在戏曲与说唱里，宫调常具体化为**调门**：笛子用「小工调」「正宫调」等指法命名，演员按嗓子选调。**宫调不等于西方大小调**：它不预设大、小两种色彩，而把音阶、主音与笛色放在一张关系网里。

::: en
**Gongdiao** is the Chinese pairing of **pitch level** and **mode**: gong fixes where the scale sits, while diao decides which note is the centre and which way the melody turns. One gong can carry several modes, and the same mode can be moved by xuangong, hence the idea of rotating the gong and modulating.

In opera and narrative song, gongdiao becomes a practical **key or fingering**: flute parts are named after fingerings such as xiaogongdiao and zhenggongdiao, and singers choose a key for their voice and the scene. **Gongdiao is not Western major or minor**: it does not assume two fixed colours but places scale, centre and flute key on one relational map. Gongche notation is read together with gongdiao; change the gong and the whole piece moves.
