::: zh
**揉弦（vibrato）** 是让**同一个音**的音高轻微、快速地起伏，给声音加一层温度与生命感。弦乐靠手指揉动，管乐靠气息与喉部，声乐靠喉部肌肉；它的宽度与速度因风格而异。

它和 **trill（颤音）** 不是一回事：trill 是两个**不同音高**的快速交替，vibrato 是一个音的音高波动。vibrato 也不是「越多越好」：巴洛克与早期音乐常用得很省，20 世纪的交响演奏则更宽；风格变了，标准就变。

::: en
**Vibrato** makes **a single note** fluctuate slightly and rapidly in pitch, adding warmth and life. Strings produce it with the finger, winds with breath and throat, singers with the laryngeal muscles; its width and rate vary with style.

It is not a **trill**: a trill alternates two **different pitches**, vibrato fluctuates one. Vibrato is not "the more the better": Baroque and early music use it sparingly, twentieth-century orchestral playing more widely; change the style and the standard changes.
