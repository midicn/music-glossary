::: zh
**360 合约（360 deal）** 是唱片公司除录音收入外，还参与艺人**演出、周边、赞助、词曲出版**等多渠道收入的合约。作为交换，公司通常提供更高的预付、制作与营销投入，把艺人当作整体品牌经营。

它不同于只分录音销售的传统唱片合约。常见误解是「360 只是多给钱」：它让公司分享艺人其他事业，期限与范围过宽会限制独立性。**谈判重点：纳入哪些收入、是否扣除成本、期限能否缩短、业绩未达标能否退出。**

::: en
**A 360 deal** gives a label a share of the artist's income beyond recordings — **touring, merchandise, sponsorship and publishing**. In exchange the company usually offers a larger advance and more spending on production and marketing, treating the artist as a whole brand.

It differs from a traditional record deal that shares only recording sales. **The usual mistake is to see 360 as simply more money**: it lets the label share other careers, and a broad, long term can limit independence. **Negotiate which income is included, whether costs are deducted, whether the term can shrink, and whether missed targets allow exit.**
