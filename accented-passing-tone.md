::: zh
**强拍经过音（accented passing tone）** 是**落在强拍上**的经过音：它仍然级进连接两个和弦音，但因为占据重拍，听起来更像**倚音**，带一点不协和的张力。

它和**弱拍经过音**不同：弱拍经过音是「填充」，强拍经过音是「强调」。常见误解是「经过音都在弱拍」——强拍经过音是古典与浪漫派旋律里很常见的表情手段；位置一换，功能就变了。

::: en
**An accented passing tone** is a passing tone **on a strong beat**: it still connects two chord tones by step, but because it occupies the beat it sounds more like an **appoggiatura**, with a touch of dissonant tension.

It differs from an **unaccented passing tone**: the unaccented one fills, the accented one stresses. The usual mistake is to assume passing tones always fall on weak beats — the accented passing tone is a common expressive device in Classical and Romantic melody; change the position and the function changes.
