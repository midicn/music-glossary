::: zh
**可回本成本（recoupable costs）** 是合同允许从艺人版税中**先扣回**的支出：预付、录音、混音、MV、宣传与巡演支持等。它不是公司的普通经营开销，而是写进合同、由艺人未来收入承担的项目。

常见误解是「公司出的钱都是投资，不用还」：只要列为可回本，就会在分成前扣除；有些合约甚至把超出直接成本的费用计入。**看合约要拿到完整清单，问清哪些不可回本、是否计息、能否跨作品抵扣。**

::: en
**Recoupable costs** are expenses a contract allows the label to **recover first** from artist royalties: advances, recording, mixing, videos, promotion and tour support. They are not ordinary business overheads but items written into the deal and borne by the artist's future income.

**The usual mistake is to think money spent by the company is a non-repayable investment**: anything listed as recoupable is deducted before profit is shared, and some deals include costs beyond direct expenses. **Get the full schedule and ask what is not recoupable, whether interest applies, and whether costs cross-collateralise across works.**
