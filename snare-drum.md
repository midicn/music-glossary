## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 小军鼓 |
| 英语 | snare drum |
| 意大利语 | tamburo militare / rullante |
| 德语 | kleine Trommel |
| 法语 | caisse claire |

**小军鼓** 是圆柱形双面鼓，鼓面下绷着一组金属响弦，击打时响弦振动，发出清脆而带沙沙声的音色。它没有固定音高，是管弦乐、军乐与鼓组的常见成员；意大利语称 tamburo militare 或 rullante，德语 kleine Trommel，法语 caisse claire。

**常见混淆**：snare 指的是鼓面下的响弦，不是鼓的尺寸或鼓棒；中文「小军鼓」与「大鼓」相对，但两者并非只差大小。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 小军鼓 |
| English | snare drum |
| Italian | tamburo militare / rullante |
| German | kleine Trommel |
| French | caisse claire |

The **snare drum** is a cylindrical double-headed drum with wires stretched under the lower head; when struck, the wires buzz and give the crisp, rattling tone. It has no definite pitch and is common in orchestras, military bands and drum kits; Italian calls it tamburo militare or rullante, German kleine Trommel, French caisse claire.

**Watch out**: "snare" names the wires under the head, not the drum's size or a stick. Chinese 小军鼓 is paired with 大鼓, but the two differ in more than size.
