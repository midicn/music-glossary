::: zh
**平衡连接（balanced connection）** 用**三根导体**（热端、冷端、地）传输信号：冷端是热端的反相副本，接收端把两者相减，**共模噪声互相抵消**。它适合长距离、复杂电磁环境。

它和**非平衡连接**不同：非平衡只有两根线，噪声无法抵消，适合短距离。**常见误解是「平衡线一定更好听」**——平衡的优点是**抗噪与长距离**，不是音色；**短距离、干净环境里，非平衡完全够用。**

::: en
**A balanced connection** uses **three conductors** (hot, cold, ground): the cold carries an inverted copy of the hot, and the receiver subtracts them, so **common-mode noise cancels**. It suits long runs and electrically noisy rooms.

It differs from **unbalanced**: two conductors cannot cancel noise and suit short runs. **The usual mistake is to assume balanced always sounds better** — its advantage is **noise rejection and distance**, not tone; **for short, clean runs, unbalanced is perfectly adequate.**
