::: zh
**版税（royalty）** 是按**使用或销售**支付给权利人的钱：机械版税按复制/发行，表演版税按公开播放，同步版税按音画授权，录音版税按唱片销售或流媒体播放。它不是固定工资，而是**随使用量波动的分成**。

版税要分清**付给谁**：词曲作者与出版方拿的是词曲版税，录音权人（常是唱片公司）拿的是录音版税，表演者可能另有分成。**常见误解是「流媒体播放一次就有固定收入」**——平台、合同、预付与 recoupment 都会影响实际到手。**看到「版税率」先问：按什么基数、扣什么成本、分给谁。**

::: en
**A royalty** is money paid to a rights holder **per use or sale**: mechanical royalties per reproduction, performance royalties per public play, sync royalties per audio-visual licence, master royalties per record sale or stream. It is not a salary but a **share that moves with usage**.

Keep clear **who is paid**: writers and publishers receive composition royalties, the master owner (often a label) receives master royalties, performers may have a separate share. **The usual mistake is to assume a fixed income per stream** — platform, contract, advances and recoupment all shape what actually arrives. **When you see a "royalty rate", ask: on what base, after what costs, to whom.**
