::: zh
**回本（recoupment）** 是用版税把**预付、录音成本、营销费用**等合同允许的扣项扣回来；只有回本之后，艺人才可能拿到额外的版税分成。它决定了「作品卖了钱」与「艺人拿到钱」之间那段距离。

**常见误解是「播放量高就一定有钱」**：如果合同把大量成本计入可回本项，播放收入可能长期停在回本线以下。**看合同要问：哪些成本可回本、回本顺序是什么、跨作品能不能互相抵。**

::: en
**Recoupment** is the recovery of **advances, recording costs, marketing** and other contractually allowed deductions from royalties; only after recoupment may the artist receive additional royalty shares. It is the distance between "the work earned money" and "the artist got paid".

**The usual mistake is to assume high streams mean income**: if the contract counts large costs as recoupable, streaming income can sit below the line for a long time. **Ask the contract: which costs recoup, in what order, and whether they cross-collateralise across works.**
