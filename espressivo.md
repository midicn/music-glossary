::: zh
**富有表情地（espressivo）**是音乐中的表情记号。

常缩写为 espress.。它要求注意乐句的重心、连贯和对比，并不指定一种装饰方法。演奏者可以结合旋律走向与和声处理音色、力度和时间，不必一律增加揉弦或渐强。

:::

::: en
**Espressivo means expressively.** It is an expression marking in music.

Often abbreviated espress., it draws attention to phrasing, continuity and contrast rather than one decorative technique. Colour, dynamics and timing should follow the melodic and harmonic context; more vibrato or crescendo is not a universal solution.
