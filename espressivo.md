::: zh
**富有表情地（espressivo）** 要求演奏者把**语气**放在第一位：不是把音弹对就完，而是让每个音都有话说。它和 cantabile 相近，但更宽——cantabile 强调「像唱」，espressivo 强调「有内容」，可以包括停顿、重音、音色变化与速度弹性。

它是一个**开放术语**：谱面无法规定「多少表情」，只能提示方向。**常见误解是把 espressivo 当成「加很多揉弦/加很多渐强」**——真正的表情来自句子的重心，而不是装饰的堆叠。

::: en
**Espressivo** asks the player to put **tone** first: not merely the right notes, but every note saying something. It is close to cantabile but broader — cantabile stresses "singing", espressivo stresses "having content", and may include pause, accent, colour and flexibility of speed.

It is an **open term**: the page cannot specify "how much expression", only a direction. **The usual mistake is to treat espressivo as "more vibrato, more crescendo"** — real expression comes from the weight of the phrase, not from piled-on decoration.
