::: zh
**神秘地（misterioso）** 来自意大利语 mistero「秘密、神秘」，源自拉丁语 mysterium，再上溯希腊语 mystērion（秘仪）。它要求音色**蒙上一层纱**：音量收敛，轮廓略糊，和声的指向不明说。

它描述**色彩与气氛**，不是速度。misterioso 可以在中速上，也可以极慢；关键是保持张力，不让音乐散掉。**常见误解是把 misterioso 演成「慢、弱、踏板全踩」**——糊成一片只会变成困倦；神秘需要控制过的模糊，底下仍要有方向。

::: en
**Misterioso** comes from the Italian mistero, "secret, mystery", from Latin mysterium and further Greek mystērion, a secret rite. The colour should be **veiled**: volume drawn in, outline slightly blurred, harmonic aims left unspoken.

It describes **colour and atmosphere**, not speed. Misterioso can move at a moderate pace or very slowly; what matters is that the tension holds and the music does not fall apart. **The usual mistake is to play it "slow, soft, pedal down"** — a single blur only becomes drowsy; mystery needs controlled blur with direction underneath.
