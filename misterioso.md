::: zh
**神秘地（misterioso）**是音乐中的表情记号。

音色、动态与织体均可参与表现这种气氛，但不应把它直接等同于轮廓模糊或踏板延长。保留旋律、低音和内声部之间的区别，才能听出作品怎样制造悬念。

:::

::: en
**Misterioso means mysteriously.** It is an expression marking in music.

Colour, dynamics and texture may all contribute to the atmosphere. The term does not simply mean blurred articulation or prolonged pedalling. Distinguishing melody, bass and inner parts helps reveal how the passage creates suspense.
