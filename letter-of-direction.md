::: zh
**指示函（letter of direction，LOD）** 是权利人写给付款方的一份指令：请把某笔版税支付给指定账户或主体。它常见于出版管理、发行变更与集体管理登记，用来**改变收款路径**，而不是转让版权。

付款方通常要求权利人签字、写明作品与比例、附账户信息，并可能要求确认后才生效。常见误解是「发了指示函就完成权利转让」：它只是付款指令，权利归属仍看合同；错误或重复的指示还可能造成错付。**通识说明，不构成法律意见。**

::: en
**A letter of direction (LOD)** is a written instruction from a rights holder to a payor: send a particular royalty to a named account or party. It is common in publishing administration, distribution changes and collective registration, and it **redirects payment** rather than transferring copyright.

Payors usually require the owner's signature, the works and shares, and account details, and may act only after confirmation. **The usual mistake is to treat an LOD as a copyright assignment**: it is a payment instruction, ownership still follows the contract, and wrong or duplicate letters can cause misdirected funds. **This is general information, not legal advice.**
