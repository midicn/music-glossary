::: zh
**悲伤、忧伤地（mesto）**是音乐中的表情记号。

注意不要与 maestoso（庄严、宏伟地）混淆。它不规定必须慢奏、弱奏或在每个句尾叹息；具体速度、力度和表情要结合上下文。
深入阅读 → [[glossary:doloroso]]
:::

::: en
**Mesto means sadly or mournfully.** It is an expression marking in music.

Do not confuse it with maestoso, meaning majestically. It does not prescribe slow or soft playing, or a sigh at every phrase ending. Read speed, dynamics and expression in context.
