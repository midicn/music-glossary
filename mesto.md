::: zh
**悲伤地（mesto）** 来自拉丁语 maestus「哀伤」。它要求音色**暗下来、重心下沉**：音量不必大，句子要有分量，句尾带一点未说完的叹息。

它容易和 maestoso 看混，两者词形相近却不同源：maestoso 是**庄严宏伟**，mesto 是**哀伤**。**常见误解是把 mesto 弹成一路慢弱的哭腔**——悲伤需要形状与节制的音色。

它最常见于慢乐章与葬礼性段落；速度通常偏慢，但决定语气的是**重量的分配**。可与 [[glossary:patetico]]、[[glossary:espressivo]] 对读。
:::

::: en
**Mesto** comes from Latin maestus, "sorrowful". The colour should **darken and the weight settle low**: volume need not be great, but phrases must carry mass, and their ends often hold an unfinished sigh.

It is easily confused with maestoso, a similar shape from a different root: maestoso is **majestic**, mesto is **mournful**. **The usual mistake is to play mesto as one long soft weeping** — sorrow needs a shape, clear phrasing and restrained colour, or it becomes mere gloom.

It is most at home in slow movements and funeral passages; the tempo is usually slow, yet what fixes the tone is **the distribution of weight**, not the metronome. Compare patetico and espressivo.
:::
