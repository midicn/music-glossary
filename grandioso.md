::: zh
**宏伟地（grandioso）** 是表情术语：以**宏大、壮阔、气势磅礴**的方式演奏。它常出现在全奏、高潮或结尾，配合宽广的速度、饱满的音色与厚重的织体。

它和 maestoso 接近，但侧重点不同：**maestoso 庄重有威仪，grandioso 更强调规模与壮阔**。**常见误解是把 grandioso 弹成「最响」**——宏伟来自层次与空间，不是单纯的音量；一直最响，反而没有「宏伟」可言。

::: en
**Grandioso** is an expression mark: play with **grand, expansive sweep**. It usually appears at a full tutti, a climax or a close, matched by broad tempo, full tone and thick texture.

It is close to maestoso but differently weighted: **maestoso is stately and commanding, grandioso stresses scale and expanse**. **The usual mistake is to play grandioso as "loudest"** — grandeur comes from layering and space, not volume alone; always loud leaves nothing grand.
