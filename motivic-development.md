::: zh
**动机发展（motivic development）**是从一个短动机里不断长出新材料：移调、倒影、逆行、扩大、缩小、分裂、模进，都是它的手段。动机每次出现都换一件衣服，却保留可辨认的轮廓，整段音乐因此像从同一颗种子长出来。

它最常出现在奏鸣曲式的展开部：主题被拆成碎片，在不同调性与声部间传递，紧张累积到属准备，再把材料交还再现部。赋格的插段、贝多芬晚期四重奏也大量使用。

**动机发展不等于变奏。** 变奏保留完整的主题与结构骨架，只改表面；动机发展则把主题拆开，只取其中一格重新组合，听众听到的是「同源」而非「同一段」。判断它，先找出那个反复出现的小轮廓。
:::

::: en
**Motivic development** grows new music from a short motive: transposition, inversion, retrograde, augmentation, diminution, fragmentation and sequence are its tools. The motive returns each time in new clothes but keeps a recognisable outline, so that a whole passage seems to grow from one seed.

It is most at home in a sonata development, where the theme is broken into cells, passed through keys and voices, and driven toward a dominant preparation before the recapitulation. Fugue episodes and Beethoven's late quartets use the same procedures.

**Motivic development is not variation.** Variation keeps a complete theme and its structural frame while changing the surface; motivic development takes the theme apart, keeps one cell and rebuilds from it, so one hears a common source rather than the same passage. Find the small shape that keeps returning.
:::
