::: zh
**Poco** 意为“稍微、一点”，修饰另一个记号的程度。例如 poco ritardando 表示稍作减速，poco più mosso 表示稍快一些。

**Poco a poco** 才表示“一点一点地、逐渐地”，例如 poco a poco crescendo 要求逐渐增强。poco 本身不等于渐变；实际变化的方式还要看它修饰的词。可与 [[glossary:molto]]、[[glossary:assai]]、[[glossary:non-troppo]] 对读。
:::

::: en
**Poco** means “a little” and qualifies the degree of another instruction. Poco ritardando asks for a slight slowing; poco più mosso asks for a little more movement.

**Poco a poco** means “little by little” or “gradually”, as in poco a poco crescendo. Poco alone does not mean gradualness; the complete instruction determines the change. Compare [[glossary:molto]], [[glossary:assai]] and [[glossary:non-troppo]].
:::
