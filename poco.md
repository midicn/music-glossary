::: zh
**稍、一点（poco）** 是缩小程度的修饰词：poco allegro 比 allegro 收敛，poco forte 比 forte 轻。它也可以单独使用，表示「稍微」。在速度变化里，poco a poco 是最常见的组合，意为「一点一点地」。

**poco 的关键是渐变**，不是把目标值直接调小。poco a poco accelerando 要求速度分许多小步走，而不是一次跳到位；写成 poco rit. 时，也要让减速听得出来是过程。它与 [[glossary:molto]]、[[glossary:assai]] 相对，与 [[glossary:non-troppo]] 不同：后者设上限，poco 只把程度往回收一点。
:::

::: en
**Poco** is a diminisher: poco allegro is more restrained than allegro, poco forte lighter than forte. It can also stand alone as "a little". In tempo changes the commonest pairing is poco a poco, "little by little".

**The point of poco is gradualness**, not simply a smaller target. Poco a poco accelerando asks the speed to move in many small steps rather than one jump; a poco rit. must likewise be heard as a process. It is opposite to molto and assai, and different from non troppo: the one caps a mark, while poco winds it back only slightly.
:::
