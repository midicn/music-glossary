::: zh
**近场监听（nearfield monitor）** 是摆在一米左右、供人近距离工作的音箱。直达声比房间反射先到耳朵，因此对房间的依赖较小，成为项目棚与家庭工作室的常见选择。

摆位通常讲究对称：两只音箱与听音位成等边三角形，高音单元齐耳高，离墙留出距离，桌面反射用垫材或支架压低。近场箱低频有限，别用均衡硬补；需要时加超低音并单独校准。

常见误解是买了近场箱就不必处理房间。它减少的是反射比例，不是驻波；墙角与后墙的问题依旧存在。
:::

::: en
**Nearfield monitors** sit about a metre away, close enough that direct sound arrives before most room reflections. That lower dependence on the room made them standard in project and home studios.

Set them up symmetrically: tweeters at ear height, the two speakers and the listening position forming an equilateral triangle, with some distance from the wall. Desk reflections can be reduced with stands or pads. Nearfields have limited low bass; do not force it with EQ. Add a subwoofer and calibrate it separately if needed.

The usual mistake is assuming nearfields remove the need for room treatment. They reduce the ratio of reflected sound, not standing waves; corners and the rear wall still matter.
:::
