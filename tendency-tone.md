::: zh
**倾向音（tendency tone）**是调内有强烈方向感的音级：导音向上半音倾向主音，四级音向下倾向三级音，和弦七音通常向下级进。它们像被磁铁吸住，一出现，耳朵就预期它走向哪里；这正是调性张力的来源。

倾向音不一定是外音。属七的导音与七音都是和弦音，却仍带强烈倾向：导音上行到主音，七音下行到三级音，形成终止式最典型的收束。小调中导音常需升高，才能保留上行拉力。

**倾向音的力量来自半音与级进。** 若跳进解决、停在原处或换到别音，预期就落空；作曲家正用这种落空制造悬置与意外。分析时先标出导音、四级音与七音，再听是否按倾向走。
:::

::: en
**A tendency tone** is a scale degree with a strong pull: the leading tone pulls up to the tonic, the fourth degree tends down to the third, and a chordal seventh normally resolves downward by step. The ear expects where they will go, and that expectation creates tension.

It need not be a non-chord tone. In a dominant seventh the leading tone and seventh are both chord members, yet both still pull, giving the cadence its close. In minor the leading tone is usually raised to keep the upward pull.

**The force lies in semitones and stepwise motion.** Resolve it by leap, leave it hanging or send it elsewhere and the expectation is denied; composers use that denial for suspension and surprise. Mark the leading tone, fourth degree and sevenths, then hear whether they move as they tend.
:::
