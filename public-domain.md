::: zh
**公有领域（public domain）** 指**不受版权保护**或**版权已到期**的作品：可以自由复制、改编与使用，通常不需要授权。但音乐里要分清**作品**与**录音**：一首十九世纪的曲子（作品）可能在公有领域，而它的某一次现代录音仍受保护。

公有领域**因国家而异**：保护期、登记要求与过渡规则不同；同一作品在一个国家公版、在另一个国家未必。**常见误解是「网上能找到就是公版」**——能找到只说明它被上传过，不说明它没有版权。**使用前先确认作品与录音两层，并确认适用法域。**

::: en
**Public domain** means **not protected by copyright** or **out of copyright**: free to copy, adapt and use, usually without permission. In music, separate the **composition** from the **recording**: a nineteenth-century piece may be public domain while one modern recording of it is still protected.

Public domain **varies by country**: terms, registration and transitional rules differ; the same work may be free in one country and not in another. **The usual mistake is to think "it is online, so it is public domain"** — being online only means someone uploaded it, not that no rights exist. **Check both layers and the jurisdiction before use.**
