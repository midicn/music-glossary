::: zh
**分轨母带（stem mastering）** 是母带阶段拿到按组划分的 stems，而不是一条立体声混音：工程师可对鼓、贝斯、人声等组分别做均衡、动态与电平调整，再汇总成最终母带。

它适合影视、游戏或需要小幅修正的项目，也便于保留器乐版与清唱版。前提是各组来自同一混音、起始点与采样率一致，汇总后与原始立体声混音逐段比对；组间处理不当会带来相位与响度偏差。

常见误解是分轨母带等于重混。若需要大幅改平衡，那已经回到混音；母带只做有限修正，并保留可回溯的立体声版本。
:::

::: en
**Stem mastering** takes grouped stems rather than a single stereo mix into the mastering stage. The engineer can adjust EQ, dynamics and level on drums, bass, vocals and other groups separately before summing them into the final master.

It suits film, game or projects needing small corrections, and makes instrumental and vocal versions easier to keep. The stems must come from the same mix with matching start points and sample rates. Compare the summed result with the original stereo mix section by section; careless group processing can introduce phase and loudness shifts.

The usual mistake is treating stem mastering as remixing. If the balance needs large changes, the work belongs back in the mix; mastering makes limited corrections and keeps a stereo version for reference.
:::
