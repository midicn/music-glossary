::: zh
**模进（sequence）**是把一个动机或短句整体移到另一音高重复，通常每次移动相同的音程或音级，形成有方向感的推进。它可上行也可下行，常见于过渡、展开与尾声中。

严格模进保持音程不变，可能暂时离开本调；调性模进则按音阶调整音程，留在调内。和声模进是同一和弦进行的平移，低音常呈五度循环或级进，连续几次后由终止式收住。

**模进不是重复。** 重复保持原高度，模进改变高度；模进也不等于转调，它可能经过新调性却不建立新主音。听模进时留意它的起点、每次移动的步长，以及在何处被打断。
:::

::: en
**A sequence** repeats a motive or short phrase at a different pitch, usually moving by the same interval or scale step each time, so that the music gains direction. It may rise or fall and is common in transitions, development sections and codas.

A real sequence keeps the intervals exact and may leave the key; a tonal sequence adjusts them to the scale. A harmonic sequence shifts the same progression, often over a circle of fifths or a stepwise bass, until a cadence closes it.

**A sequence is not a repetition.** Repetition stays at the same pitch, while a sequence changes it; nor is a sequence necessarily a modulation, since it may pass through a new key without establishing a new tonic. Listen for where it starts, how far each step moves, and where it breaks off.
:::
