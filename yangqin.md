## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 扬琴 |
| 英语 | yangqin（Chinese hammered dulcimer） |
| 意大利语 | —（无通行专名，借 yangqin） |
| 德语 | Yangqin（chinesisches Hackbrett） |
| 法语 | yangqin（tympanon chinois） |

**扬琴** 是中国击弦乐器，梯形音箱上张多根钢丝弦，双手各持琴竹击奏，传统音位按五声或七声音阶排列。英语常作 **yangqin**，并注明 Chinese hammered dulcimer；德语用 **Yangqin**，也描述为 chinesisches Hackbrett，法语说 **tympanon chinois**。意大利语无固定译名，一般借 yangqin（通行说法）。

常见混淆：扬琴是击弦，不是弹拨——它与古筝外形都宽大，但古筝用手指弹、雁柱可移动，扬琴用琴竹打、琴码固定；它和欧洲的 cimbalom 同属击弦扬琴族，但音位与音色不同。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 扬琴 |
| English | yangqin (Chinese hammered dulcimer) |
| Italian | — (no settled term; yangqin is borrowed) |
| German | Yangqin (chinesisches Hackbrett) |
| French | yangqin (tympanon chinois) |

The **yangqin** is a Chinese hammered dulcimer: a trapezoidal soundbox strung with many steel strings, struck with two flexible beaters, with notes traditionally laid out in pentatonic or heptatonic patterns. English writes **yangqin** (Chinese hammered dulcimer); German **Yangqin** (chinesisches Hackbrett); French **tympanon chinois**. Italian has no settled term and generally borrows yangqin (a common practice).

Watch out: the yangqin is struck, not plucked — it shares a broad body with the guzheng, but the guzheng is plucked with movable bridges, while the yangqin is hit with beaters over fixed bridges; it belongs to the same hammered-dulcimer family as the cimbalom, though layout and tone differ.
