::: zh
**达布（dub）** 是**雷鬼的器乐重混版**：制作人把原曲的人声拿掉或切成碎片，把鼓与贝斯推到前景，用**混响、延迟、滤波与突然的静音**重新塑造空间。它起源于 1970 年代的牙买加 sound system 文化。

它和**混音（mixdown）**不同：dub 不是「把歌混好」，而是把已有的录音当成材料重新作曲。常见误解是「dub 只是加很多混响」——它的核心是减法与空间调度；混响只是工具之一。

::: en
**Dub** is the **instrumental remix of reggae**: the producer removes or fragments the vocal, pushes drums and bass forward, and rebuilds the space with **reverb, delay, filtering and sudden silences**. It grew out of 1970s Jamaican sound-system culture.

It differs from a **mixdown**: dub is not "mixing the song properly" but treating an existing recording as material for a new composition. The usual mistake is to think dub is just lots of reverb — its core is subtraction and the choreography of space; reverb is only one tool.
