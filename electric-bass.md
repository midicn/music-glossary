## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 电贝斯 |
| 英语 | electric bass（bass guitar） |
| 意大利语 | basso elettrico |
| 德语 | E-Bass |
| 法语 | basse électrique |

**电贝斯** 又叫电贝司、低音吉他，是四弦（也有五弦、六弦）的拨弦乐器，定弦 E1–A1–D2–G2，比吉他低一个八度。意大利语 basso elettrico、德语 E-Bass、法语 basse électrique 都是「电的低音」。1951 年 Fender Precision Bass 问世后，它逐渐取代低音提琴，成为流行乐队的地基。

**常见混淆**：电贝斯不是电吉他的「低音版」那么简单——它承担低音声部，弦更粗、有效弦长更长，演奏思路也不同；中文「贝斯」有时也指低音提琴（double bass），两者一电一木，不要混用。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 电贝斯 |
| English | electric bass (bass guitar) |
| Italian | basso elettrico |
| German | E-Bass |
| French | basse électrique |

The **electric bass**, also called bass guitar, is a plucked instrument with four strings (sometimes five or six) tuned E1–A1–D2–G2, an octave below the guitar. Italian basso elettrico, German E-Bass and French basse électrique all mean "electric bass". After the Fender Precision Bass appeared in 1951, it gradually replaced the double bass as the foundation of popular bands.

**Watch out**: it is not simply a low guitar — it carries the bass line, with thicker strings, a longer scale and a different playing approach. Chinese 贝斯 can also mean the double bass, a wooden instrument; the two should not be mixed up.
