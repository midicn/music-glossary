## 五语对照

| 语种 | 名称 |
|---|---|
| 中文 | 短号 |
| 英语 | cornet |
| 意大利语 | cornetta |
| 德语 | Kornett |
| 法语 | cornet à pistons |

**短号** 是降 B 调（也有降 E 调）铜管乐器，三支活塞，管径比小号更偏圆锥，音色柔和，是铜管乐队与军乐队的常客。意大利语 cornetta、德语 Kornett、法语 cornet à pistons 都指这件乐器；法语名字里的「活塞」点明了它靠活塞改变音高，与旧式无活塞的号不同。

**常见混淆**：中文「短号」的「短」通常理解为外形紧凑，并不是说管长比小号短——同为降 B 调，两者管长其实相近。它与富鲁格号、小号常被混叫：短号音色居中，富鲁格号更圆更暗，小号更亮。

## Five languages

| Language | Name |
|---|---|
| Chinese | 短号 |
| English | cornet |
| Italian | cornetta |
| German | Kornett |
| French | cornet à pistons |

The **cornet** is a B flat (or E flat) brass instrument with three valves and a more conical bore than the trumpet, which gives it a softer tone; it is a staple of brass and military bands. Italian cornetta, German Kornett and French cornet à pistons all name it, the French adding "with pistons" to distinguish it from the valveless horn of the period.

**Watch out**: the Chinese 短号, "short horn", describes its compact shape, not a shorter tube — in B flat the cornet and trumpet have nearly the same tubing length. Among cornet, flugelhorn and trumpet, the cornet sits in the middle in brightness.
