::: zh
**滑奏（glissando）** 是从一个音滑到另一个音，谱面上常写 gliss. 加一条斜线。关键区别在**中途的音算不算数**：钢琴、竖琴的滑奏是快速掠过一个个琴键或弦，听到的是「一串音」；长号、提琴、人声的滑奏是连续改变音高，听到的是「一条线」。

它和 **portamento** 常被混用：portamento 更强调表情性的、带目标的滑入，glissando 更强调滑的过程本身。**同一个记号，在键盘上是音阶，在弦乐上是一条带子**——写谱的人必须知道这件乐器会把它实现成哪一种。

::: en
**Glissando** is a slide from one note to another, usually written gliss. with a diagonal line. The key difference is **whether the notes in between count**: on piano or harp the slide sweeps quickly through keys or strings, so you hear "a series of notes"; on trombone, strings or voice the pitch changes continuously, so you hear "a line".

It is often confused with **portamento**: portamento stresses an expressive, goal-directed slide into a note, glissando the sliding process itself. **The same mark is a scale on a keyboard and a band on a string** — the writer must know which one the instrument will produce.
