::: zh
**赋格（fugue）** 是一种对位曲式：一个短主题（subject）在一个声部出现，随后其他声部在相隔五度或八度上**接住它**（答题 answer），主题与答题交替进入，像几个人轮流追同一句话。中间可以插段（episode）、改变调性，最后常回到主题与主调。

赋格不是「严格重复」：主题可以扩大、缩小、倒影、紧接（stretto），每一次进入都是一次变奏。**巴赫《平均律》里的四十八首赋格，没有两首用同一种写法**——赋格是一套做法，不是一张表格。

::: en
**A fugue** is a contrapuntal form: a short subject appears in one voice, other voices **take it up** a fifth or an octave away (the answer), and subject and answer enter in turn, like several people chasing one sentence. Episodes and modulations separate the entries, and the ending usually returns to the subject and the tonic.

A fugue is not "strict repetition": the subject can be augmented, diminished, inverted, overlapped in stretto — every entry is a variation. **No two of Bach's forty-eight fugues in the Well-Tempered Clavier work the same way**: a fugue is a set of procedures, not a form to fill in.
