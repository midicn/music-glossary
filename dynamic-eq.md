::: zh
**动态均衡（dynamic EQ）** 是带阈值的 EQ：某个频段超过设定电平时才提升或衰减，安静时几乎不动。相当于把压缩器的检测装进单个 EQ 频段。

它适合处理只在偶尔出现的毛病：某句人声的鼻音、某颗鼓的共振、贝斯某个音的突起。与静态 EQ 相比，它不会削薄不需要处理的段落；与多段压缩相比，它只动指定频段。

常见误解是动态 EQ 可以代替压缩。它管的是频段平衡，不是整体动态；用过头会让音色随音量变化，听感不稳，EQ 可参 [[glossary:eq]]。
:::

::: en
**Dynamic EQ** is an equalizer with a threshold: a band boosts or cuts only when its level passes the setting, and stays almost still when the material is quiet. It is close to fitting a compressor's detector into a single EQ band.

It suits problems that appear only occasionally: a nasal phrase in a vocal, a resonant drum hit, a note that jumps out on the bass. Unlike a static EQ it does not thin the passages that need no correction; unlike a multiband compressor it moves only the chosen band.

The usual mistake is to use dynamic EQ as a substitute for compression. It balances frequency ranges, not overall dynamics; pushed too far, the tone shifts with level and the sound becomes unstable.
:::
