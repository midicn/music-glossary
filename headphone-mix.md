::: zh
**耳机混音（headphone mix）** 是在耳机上完成或检查混音的做法。耳机不受房间影响，能听清细节、噪声与声像位置，适合小空间、夜间工作与逐轨编辑。

局限也很明确：左右声道完全隔离，没有串音，声像常显得过宽；低频靠耳罩密封，量感与音箱不同；长时间佩戴还会疲劳。做法是用中性耳机，加一点串音，并频繁切到音箱与单声道检查。

常见误解是耳机混音可以取代音箱。耳机擅长查错，不擅长判断整体平衡；最终决定仍应在可信的房间与系统上复核。
:::

::: en
A **headphone mix** is a mix made or checked on headphones. Headphones bypass the room, exposing detail, noise and panning, which suits small spaces, night work and track-by-track editing.

Their limits are clear: the channels are fully isolated with no crossfeed, so images often seem too wide; bass depends on the earcup seal and differs from speakers; long sessions tire the ears. Work on neutral headphones, add a little crossfeed, and switch to speakers and mono checks often.

The usual mistake is treating headphone mixing as a replacement for monitors. Headphones are good at finding faults, not at judging overall balance; the final call should still be confirmed in a trustworthy room.
:::
