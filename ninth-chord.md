::: zh
九和弦（ninth chord）是在七和弦上再叠一个三度，成为五个音：根、三、五、七、九。常见类型有大九、小九与属九，区别在三音与七音的性质；属九是大三和弦加小七度再加大九度，最为常见。

九音常被当作色彩音，实际弹奏会省掉五音，甚至把根音交给低音乐器；但七音通常保留，否则它就成了加音和弦。降九音会带来更尖锐的紧张感，常解决到主和弦或继续半音下行。

九和弦不是「三和弦加九音」的同义词。后者是加音和弦，没有七音；九和弦的七音仍在，功能上属于七和弦的延伸。
:::

::: en
A ninth chord adds one more third above a seventh chord, making five notes: root, third, fifth, seventh and ninth. The common types are major, minor and dominant ninths, distinguished by their third and seventh; the dominant ninth, a major triad with a minor seventh and a major ninth, is the commonest.

The ninth is usually heard as colour. Performers often drop the fifth, or hand the root to the bass, but they normally keep the seventh; without it the chord becomes an added-tone chord. A flat ninth sharpens the tension and often resolves to the tonic or slides down chromatically.

A ninth chord is not simply a triad plus a ninth. That description fits an added-tone chord, which has no seventh; the ninth chord keeps its seventh and belongs to the extended seventh-chord family.
:::
