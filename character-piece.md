::: zh
**特性小品（character piece）** 是短小的器乐作品，一件乐器（多半是钢琴）用一页到几页的篇幅抓住一种性格、情绪或场景：即兴曲、音乐瞬间、无词歌、摇篮曲都可以归入。

它不靠大型曲式展开，而靠一个鲜明的乐思反复打磨；标题常提示性格而非故事，如「间奏曲」「册页」。舒伯特、门德尔松、舒曼、勃拉姆斯与福雷把这门体裁写成私人化的日记。

**不要把「小」当成「简单」。** 特性小品的难点在于用极少的材料建立完整的气氛，句法、音区与踏板的微小变化都会改变性格。听的时候，先抓住它反复出现的那个乐思，再看每次回来有什么不同。
:::

::: en
**A character piece** is a short instrumental work in which one instrument, usually the piano, seizes a single character, mood or scene in a page or two: impromptus, moments musicaux, songs without words and berceuses all belong here.

It unfolds not through a large form but through the repeated polishing of one vivid idea; titles usually point to a character rather than a story, as in "intermezzo" or "album leaf". Schubert, Mendelssohn, Schumann, Brahms and Faure turned the genre into a private diary.

**Do not mistake small for simple.** The difficulty lies in building a complete atmosphere from very little material; small changes of phrasing, register and pedalling alter the character. Listen first for the idea that keeps returning, then for what is different each time it comes back.
:::
