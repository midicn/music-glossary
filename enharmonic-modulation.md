::: zh
等音转调（enharmonic modulation）是借等音改写和弦，把音乐转到远关系调。最典型的是减七和弦：它对称、可等音重拼，同一个音响能被听成不同根音的减七，从而滑向不同的调。

增六和弦与属七和弦也可等音互转：德国六和弦听起来像属七，重拼后就能指向另一个主音。转调的关键不是写下不同的音，而是让听者接受新的解决方向。

它不同于单纯的等音记谱。等音记谱只是同一音的不同写法，功能不变；等音转调则借重拼改变和弦身份，使新调的主音获得引力，常需后续的属与主来确认。
:::

::: en
Enharmonic modulation respells a chord to turn the music toward a remote key. The clearest case is the diminished seventh: it is symmetrical and can be respelled, so one sound may pass for diminished sevenths on different roots and slide into different keys.

Augmented sixth and dominant seventh chords can also trade enharmonically: a German sixth sounds like a dominant seventh, and respelling it can point to another tonic. The point is not the different spelling but the new direction of resolution the ear accepts.

It is not mere enharmonic notation. Respelling alone changes how a note is written, not what the chord does; enharmonic modulation changes the chord's identity and gives a new tonic its pull, usually confirmed by the new dominant and tonic.
:::
