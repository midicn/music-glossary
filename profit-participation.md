::: zh
**利润参与（profit participation）** 是合同赋予某方**分享项目收入或利润**的权利，常见于制作人、投资方、艺人或关键合作者。它可以是毛收入分成，也可以是扣除成本后的净利润分成，取决于合同定义。

关键在**参与基数、优先回收顺序与是否交叉抵扣**：同样的百分之几，挂在毛收入还是净利润上差别巨大。常见误解是「利润参与等于版权份额」：它通常只是**合同债权**，不带来版权。通识说明，不构成法律意见。

::: en
**Profit participation** is a contractual right to **share in a project's revenue or profit**, common for producers, investors, artists and key collaborators. It may be a share of gross income or of net profit after costs, depending on how the contract defines it.

What matters is **the base, the order of recoupment and whether claims cross-collateralise**: the same few per cent behaves very differently on gross and on net. **The usual mistake is to equate participation with a copyright share**: it is usually a contractual claim that carries no ownership. This is general information, not legal advice.
