::: zh
**如歌地（cantabile）** 要求演奏者把乐器当成**人声**：句子要连、要有呼吸、要有自然的起伏。它不是一种音色，而是一种**句法态度**——同样的音，用 cantabile 演奏会更强调旋律线、更少强调颗粒感。

它常与 dolce 连用（dolce cantabile），但两者不同：**dolce 管音色甜不甜，cantabile 管句子像不像在唱。** 钢琴上的 cantabile 尤其难，因为钢琴本质上是「打击」乐器，要靠触键与踏板把音连成一条线。

::: en
**Cantabile** asks the player to treat the instrument as a **voice**: phrases connected, breathed, naturally shaped. It is not a timbre but an **attitude to phrasing** — the same notes played cantabile stress the melodic line and play down the grain.

It often appears with dolce (dolce cantabile), but the two differ: **dolce governs whether the tone is sweet, cantabile whether the phrase sings.** Cantabile is especially hard on the piano, a struck instrument that must use touch and pedal to make a line.
