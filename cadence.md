::: zh
**终止式（cadence）** 是和声的标点：它告诉耳朵「这句话完了」「这里只是停顿」「这里还在问」。最常见的是**正格终止**（属 → 主，像句号）、**半终止**（停在属，像逗号）、**变格终止**（下属 → 主，像感叹号）与意外终止（deceptive cadence，属 → 六级，预期落空）。

终止式不只是「最后两个和弦」：它的分量由旋律位置、节奏、低音与前后文共同决定。同一个 V–I，放在乐句中间是逗号，放在结尾才是句号。

::: en
**A cadence** is harmony's punctuation: it tells the ear "this sentence is over", "this is only a pause", or "this is still a question". The common types are the **authentic cadence** (dominant to tonic, a full stop), the **half cadence** (stopping on the dominant, a comma), the **plagal cadence** (subdominant to tonic, an exclamation) and the deceptive cadence (dominant to the sixth degree, an expected resolution denied).

A cadence is not merely "the last two chords": its weight comes from melody, rhythm, bass and context. The same V–I is a comma mid-phrase and a full stop only at the end.
