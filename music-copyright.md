::: zh
**版权（copyright）** 是法律给创作者的一组专有权利：复制、发行、公开表演、改编等。音乐里最容易混的是**两层权利**：**作品**（词曲，composition）与**录音**（master）各自有版权，可以分属不同的人。翻唱别人的歌，需要词曲授权；直接使用别人的录音，还需要录音授权。

版权不是「旋律的指纹」：它保护的是具体的表达，不是音阶、和弦进行这类想法。保护期因国家而异，通常为作者死后若干年。常见误解是「买了唱片就拥有版权」——买唱片只买到听的权利。这是通识说明，具体事务请咨询专业人士。

::: en
**Copyright** is the set of exclusive rights the law gives creators: to copy, distribute, perform publicly and adapt. In music the easiest thing to confuse is **two layers of rights**: the **composition** (words and music) and the **master** (the recording) are separately owned and may belong to different people. Covering a song needs a composition licence; using an existing recording also needs a master licence.

Copyright is not a "fingerprint of melody": it protects specific expression, not ideas such as scales or chord progressions. The term varies by country, usually a number of years after the author's death. The usual mistake is to think buying a record buys the copyright — it buys a licence to listen. This is general information, not legal advice.
