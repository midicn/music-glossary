::: zh
**合理使用（fair use）** 是美国版权法里的抗辩原则：在**评论、教学、新闻报道、研究**等目的下，可以在一定范围内使用受版权保护的材料，而不必逐次取得授权。判断通常看四个因素：使用目的与性质、作品性质、使用比例、对市场的影响。

它**不是一张通用许可证**，也**不是「注明出处就没事」**：每一次使用都要单独判断，而且合理使用是美国法的概念，其他国家的对应规则不同。常见误解是「非商业就算合理使用」——非商业只是因素之一，不是护身符。有疑虑时，清权比赌抗辩稳。

::: en
**Fair use** is a defence in US copyright law: for **criticism, teaching, news and research**, limited use of copyrighted material may not require permission each time. Courts weigh four factors: purpose and character, nature of the work, amount used, and effect on the market.

It is **not a blanket licence**, and **not "credit the source and you are fine"**: every use is judged on its own, and fair use is a US doctrine — other countries have different rules. The usual mistake is to think "non-commercial equals fair use" — non-commercial is only one factor, not a shield. When in doubt, clearing rights is safer than betting on a defence.
