::: zh
**quasi（仿佛、近似）** 是意大利语的修饰语：**quasi una fantasia** 是「像一首幻想曲」，**quasi recitativo** 是「像宣叙调」。它不改变曲式，只是提示**性格与语气**。

它和**真正的体裁标记**不同：写了 quasi una fantasia 的奏鸣曲仍然是奏鸣曲，只是某一乐章要**带幻想曲的自由语气**。**常见误解是把 quasi 当成「就是」**——它恰恰是「近似而不是」；**看到 quasi，听的是像不像，而不是是不是。**

::: en
**Quasi** ("as if, almost") is an Italian modifier: **quasi una fantasia** is "like a fantasia", **quasi recitativo** "like recitative". It does not change the form; it signals **character and manner**.

It differs from a **real genre label**: a sonata marked quasi una fantasia is still a sonata, but one movement should carry **the free manner of a fantasia**. **The usual mistake is to read quasi as "is"** — it means "almost, not quite"; **hear whether it seems like it, not whether it is it.**
