::: zh
**共同出版（co-publishing）** 是作者与出版方**共同持有**词曲版权份额的安排：作者通常保留一半出版权，出版方取得另一半，双方按份额分出版收入。它比传统出版转让给作者更多份额，也常伴随预付与期限。

常见误解是「共同出版等于共同署名」：署名是贡献认定，份额是合同安排，两者不必一致。**谈共同出版要看清：版权比例、管理权归谁、预付如何回收、到期后份额是否回归。通识说明，不构成法律意见。**

::: en
**Co-publishing** is an arrangement in which the writer and the publisher **share the copyright** in the compositions: typically the writer keeps half the publishing and the publisher takes the other half, with income split accordingly. It gives the writer a larger share than a traditional assignment and often comes with an advance and a term.

**The usual mistake is to confuse co-publishing with co-writing credit**: credit reflects authorship, while the share is a contract term, and the two need not match. **Check the copyright split, who controls administration, how the advance is recouped, and whether the share reverts at the end. This is general information, not legal advice.**
