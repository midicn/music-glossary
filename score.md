::: zh
**总谱（score）** 把各声部按小节纵向对齐，一组组谱表自上而下叠放；指挥读它，是为了在同一时刻看到所有人在做什么。

它不是把分谱装订成册：拍号、调号、排练记号必须统一，纵向对齐才成立。移调乐器的声部可以写成记谱音高，也可以写成实际音高，后者常称 C 谱或实际音高总谱。

容易误会的是把总谱只当资料。它首先是工作工具：横向追旋律，纵向看和声与节奏的咬合，再把结论带回分谱排练。
:::

::: en
**A score** aligns every part bar by bar, stacking groups of staves one above another so that a conductor can see, at a single moment, what everyone is doing.

It is not a binding of parts: time signatures, key signatures and rehearsal marks must agree before vertical alignment means anything. A score may print transposing instruments at sounding pitch, which is called a C score, or at their written pitch.

The common mistake is to treat a score as archive material. It is a working tool: follow melody across, read harmony and rhythm down, then carry those findings back to the parts.
:::
