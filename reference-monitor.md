::: zh
**参考监听（reference monitor）** 指以中性、可复现为目标的监听音箱，常作为工作室里固定的判断基准。它追求的是暴露问题，而不是把音乐放得好听；常见于母带间与主控室。

参考级监听往往频响更宽、解析力更高，能在中等音量下听清低频与细节；但价格不等于可信，房间校准与摆位仍是前提。它与近场监听是角色与形态的区别，许多工作室两者并用。

常见误解是把「参考」当成参考曲目。参考监听是硬件基准，参考曲目是拿来对照的成品录音；两者配合，才能判断混音是否站得住。
:::

::: en
A **reference monitor** is a speaker built to be neutral and repeatable, used as a fixed point of judgement in a studio. Its job is to expose problems rather than make music sound flattering; it is common in mastering and control rooms.

Reference-grade monitors tend to offer wider bandwidth and higher resolution, revealing bass and detail at moderate levels. Price alone does not make them trustworthy: room calibration and placement still come first. They differ from nearfields in role and size, and many studios use both.

The usual mistake is confusing a reference monitor with a reference track. The monitor is the hardware baseline; the reference track is a finished recording used for comparison. Together they show whether a mix holds up.
:::
