::: zh
**幻想曲（fantasia）** 不依附固定的曲式，乐思按作曲家的想象连缀，段落之间可以突然转调、变速或换织体。文艺复兴与巴洛克的幻想曲常是复调即兴的产物，巴赫《半音阶幻想曲与赋格》把自由与严谨并置。

十九世纪，幻想曲常以现成主题为材料：舒曼、李斯特、萨拉萨蒂写有基于歌剧旋律的幻想曲。它和变奏曲的区别在于，变奏曲围绕一个主题逐层变化，幻想曲则让材料在段落间自由变形。

听幻想曲，别急着找「主题—发展—再现」。**先标记段落之间的接缝**：哪里换速度，哪里换调，哪里从自由走向严格；接缝就是作曲家安排想象力的地方。
:::

::: en
**A fantasia** does not follow a fixed form: ideas are joined by the composer's imagination, and the music may change key, speed or texture without warning. Renaissance and Baroque fantasias were often products of improvised counterpoint; Bach's Chromatic Fantasia and Fugue sets freedom beside strictness.

In the nineteenth century fantasias often took existing themes as material: Schumann, Liszt and Sarasate wrote fantasias on operatic tunes. The difference from variations is that variations reshape one theme step by step, while a fantasia lets its material change freely between sections.

Listen without hunting for "theme, development, recapitulation". **Mark the seams between sections instead**: where the tempo turns, where the key shifts, where freedom gives way to strict writing. The seams are where the composer stages the imagination.
:::
