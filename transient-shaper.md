::: zh
**瞬态整形器（transient shaper）** 单独调整声音的起音与尾音：加起音让鼓点更硬，减起音让它变软；加尾音延长共振，减尾音收干净。它看包络形状，不是电平。

它不按阈值工作，也不会自动拉平整体音量。鼓组、拨弦、循环素材上最见效；放在平行压缩旁边，可以一个管冲击、一个管密度。

常见误解是把它当快启动压缩。压缩器压低峰值后整体变平，它则只改起音、保留尾音，两者听感与用途不同，压缩参 [[glossary:compressor]]。
:::

::: en
**A transient shaper** adjusts a sound's attack and tail separately: more attack makes a drum hit harder, less attack softens it; more tail extends resonance, less tail tightens it. It reads the shape of the envelope rather than the level.

Unlike a compressor, it does not work from a threshold and does not flatten the overall level. It is most useful on drums, plucked instruments and loops; beside parallel compression, one can handle punch while the other adds density.

The usual mistake is to call it a fast-attack compressor. A compressor turns peaks down and flattens the sound; a transient shaper can change only the attack and leave the tail intact, which sounds and behaves differently.
:::
