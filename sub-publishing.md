::: zh
**转授出版（sub-publishing）** 是出版方在**境外或特定地区**委托当地公司代理其词曲版权：登记作品、加入当地集体管理组织、收取并汇回版税。原出版方仍是权利人，转授方取得的是**地域性代理权**。

常见误解是「转授就是卖出版权」：它只是区域代理，期限与范围由合同限定；转授方的管理费与汇回周期会直接影响作者实际到账。**跨境合作要问清：覆盖哪些国家、费率多少、对账频率如何。通识说明，不构成法律意见。**

::: en
**Sub-publishing** appoints a local company to represent a publisher's compositions **in another country or territory**: registering works, joining local collecting societies, and collecting and remitting royalties. The original publisher remains the rights holder; the sub-publisher receives a **territorial agency right**.

**The usual mistake is to read sub-publishing as a sale of copyright**: it is regional representation limited by term and scope, and the sub-publisher's fee and remittance schedule directly affect what the writer receives. **For cross-border deals, ask which countries are covered, what the fee is, and how often statements arrive. This is general information, not legal advice.**
