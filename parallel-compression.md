::: zh
**平行压缩（parallel compression）** 把信号复制一份重度压缩，再与原信号混合：干声保住起音与峰值，压过的那份抬起弱音尾音，声音更厚却不失冲击。

做法有两条：用发送送入压缩后的返回，或复制一轨单独压。鼓组最常用，人声与贝斯也适用。关键在比例：压缩那路压得很狠，再藏在干声下面。

常见误解是平行压缩只是变响。它改变强弱比例：峰值基本不动，低电平细节变多，听感更密而非更炸；压缩可参 [[glossary:compressor]]。
:::

::: en
**Parallel compression** copies a signal, compresses the copy hard, then blends it under the dry original. The dry path keeps the attack and peaks, while the compressed copy lifts quiet detail and tails, so the sound grows thicker without losing punch.

There are two common methods: feed a compressed return with a send, or duplicate the track and compress the duplicate. It is most associated with drums, but vocals and bass benefit too. The blend matters more than the compressor setting; the compressed path is often crushed and then hidden low.

The usual mistake is to hear parallel compression as simply louder. It changes the ratio between strong and weak moments: peaks barely move while low-level detail rises.
:::
