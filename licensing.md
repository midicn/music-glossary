::: zh
**许可（license）** 是权利人给出的**使用允许**：在约定的范围、地域、期限与用途内，被许可方可以使用作品或录音。许可可以是**独家**或**非独家**，可以有版税，也可以是一次性买断；但它不等于转让版权——权利仍然归原权利人。

常见误解是「买了许可就拥有版权」：许可只买到合同写明的使用权，超出范围仍然侵权。看一份许可，先看四件事：许可什么、用在哪里、用多久、能不能转授。

::: en
**A licence** is **permission to use** granted by a rights holder: within an agreed scope, territory, term and purpose, the licensee may use a work or recording. It can be **exclusive** or **non-exclusive**, paid by royalty or bought out; but it is not a transfer of copyright — ownership stays with the rights holder.

The usual mistake is to think a licence buys the copyright: it buys only the use described in the contract, and going beyond that is infringement. **When you read a licence, check four things: what is licensed, where it is used, for how long, and whether it can be sublicensed.**
