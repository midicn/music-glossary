::: zh
**拍号（time signature）** 写在谱号与调号之后，用上下两个数字说明每小节怎么数：上面的数字是**每小节几拍**，下面的数字是**以哪种音符为一拍**。4/4 是每小节四拍、四分音符为一拍；3/4 常见于圆舞曲。

关键在复合拍：6/8 并不是「八分音符打六下」，而是**两个附点四分音符的大拍**，每拍再分成三小拍。它和 3/4 的差别不在音符总数，而在重音怎么分组——听感上一个是两拍子，一个是三拍子。

常见误解：把拍号当速度。拍号只规定每小节的结构，快慢由 tempo 决定；同一个 4/4 可以从极慢奏到极快。

::: en
**A time signature** stands after the clef and key signature and uses two numbers to say how each bar is counted: the top number is **how many beats to the bar**, the bottom is **which note value gets one beat**. 4/4 means four quarter-note beats per bar; 3/4 is the waltz's usual frame.

The crucial case is compound metre: 6/8 is not "six taps of an eighth note" but **two big beats of a dotted quarter**, each split into three. Its difference from 3/4 is not the total number of notes but how the accents group — one feels like two beats, the other like three.

The usual mistake is to read a time signature as tempo. It fixes the bar's structure only; speed comes from the tempo marking, and the same 4/4 can go from very slow to very fast.
