::: zh
**和弦外音（non-chord tone）**是出现在某个和弦背景上、却不属于该和弦的音。它带来短暂的紧张，通常以级进解决到和弦音。按进入与离开的方式，可分为经过音、辅助音、倚音、延留音与先现音等。

分析外音要问三件事：落在强拍还是弱拍，以级进还是跳进进入，是否由前一和弦保持而来。挂留与延留正是后一类：前一和弦的音留在新和弦里，先协和、后不协和，再分别向下或向上解决。

**常见误解是把所有不协和音都当成外音。** 七音、九音在不少风格里已是和弦成员；同一音在浅层分析里是外音，在更深层的和声骨架里可能是结构和弦音。先确定和弦，再谈外音。
:::

::: en
**A non-chord tone** is a note that sounds against a chord without belonging to it. It creates brief tension and normally resolves by step. The main kinds are passing tones, neighbors, appoggiaturas, suspensions, anticipations and escape tones.

To analyse one, ask: is it on a strong or weak beat, is it approached by step or leap, and is it held over? Suspensions and retardations are the held kind: the note stays while the harmony changes, turns dissonant, then resolves down or up.

**The common mistake is to treat every dissonance as a non-chord tone.** Sevenths and ninths are chord members in many styles, and a note that looks foreign on the surface may belong to the structural harmony beneath. Fix the chord first, then label the tone.
:::
