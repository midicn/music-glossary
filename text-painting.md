::: zh
**词画（text-painting）** 是用**音乐**把歌词里的动作、方向或画面「画」出来：唱到「上升」旋律上行，唱到「叹息」用下行或停顿，唱到「光」用明亮的大调与高音区。

它和**标题音乐**不同：词画依附**歌词**，标题音乐依附标题或故事；它也不等于「图解」——好的词画服务于情绪，不是逐字翻译。常见误解是「每个词都要画」——画得太多，音乐就变成猜谜；词画要选重点。

::: en
**Text-painting** uses **music** to illustrate the action, direction or image in the words: "rising" climbs, "sighing" falls or pauses, "light" turns to a bright major and a high register.

It differs from **programme music**: text-painting depends on **words**, programme music on a title or story; nor is it literal illustration — good text-painting serves emotion, not word-by-word translation. The usual mistake is to paint every word — too much turns the music into a puzzle; choose the important words.
