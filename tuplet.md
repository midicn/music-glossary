::: zh
**连音组（tuplet）**是把本应分成若干份的时值改分成另一数目，例如三连音、五连音、七连音，用数字与括号标出。它改变的是划分比例，不改变总时值：三连音用三份等值取代两份，总长不变。

在复合拍子里也会出现二连音、四连音，用来把三份的拍子临时分成两份或四份。跨拍、跨小节的连音要写清覆盖范围；演奏时先找公分母，再让每个音落在正确的比例位置上。

**三连音不是「弹快一点」**，而是三份等分取代两份，速度由总时值决定。连音组也不等于自由节奏，其时值比例是严格的。嵌套连音，例如三连音里再分五份，需要按比例继续细分。
:::

::: en
**A tuplet** divides a duration into a number of equal parts that the metre does not normally allow, such as a triplet, quintuplet or septuplet, and is marked with a number and a bracket. It changes the proportion, not the duration: a triplet replaces two parts with three equal ones but still fills the same span.

In compound metre, duplets and quadruplets appear to divide a dotted beat into two or four. Tuplets crossing beats or bars should show their span; performers find a common denominator, then place each note proportionally.

**A triplet does not mean "play faster"**: it means three equal parts instead of two, and its speed follows from the total duration. A tuplet is not free rhythm either: its proportions are strict. Nested tuplets, such as five within a triplet, must be subdivided again.
:::
