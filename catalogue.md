::: zh
**版权曲库（catalogue）** 指一批词曲版权或录音版权的集合，可以整体估值、买卖、抵押或授权。买家看的是**持续现金流**：曲库的历史收入、权利人结构、合约期限与到期回收风险，都会影响价格。

常见误解是「曲库越大越值钱」：权利是否清晰、有无未结诉讼、是否临近终止权行使期，比数量更关键。**尽职调查要覆盖权利链、登记状态与收入审计。通识说明，不构成法律意见。**

::: en
**A catalogue** is a collection of song copyrights or master rights that can be valued, bought, sold, pledged or licensed as a whole. Buyers look at **durable cash flow**: historical income, the mix of rights holders, contract terms and the risk of rights reverting all affect the price.

**The usual mistake is to assume a bigger catalogue is worth more**: clean title, pending disputes and how soon termination rights may be exercised matter more than volume. **Due diligence should cover the chain of title, registration status and an audit of income. This is general information, not legal advice.**
