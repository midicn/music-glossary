::: zh
**宁静地（tranquillo）** 来自拉丁语 tranquillus「平静、无扰」。它要求音乐**平稳地流动**：脉搏匀，音色澄澈，句子不被突如其来的重音打断。

它说的是**状态**，不是速度；可以在行板，也可以在较快的段落里保持从容。**常见误解是把宁静弹成停滞**——没有向前的呼吸，安静会变成走神。

它常出现在慢乐章与尾声，也常与 andante、adagio 并置；可与 [[glossary:sostenuto]]、[[glossary:dolce]] 对读。
:::

::: en
**Tranquillo** comes from Latin tranquillus, "calm, undisturbed". The music should **flow evenly**: a steady pulse, clear colour, phrases that no sudden accent breaks into.

It names **a state**, not a speed; tranquillo can sit in an andante or keep its composure in a faster passage. **The usual mistake is to let calm become a standstill** — without forward breath, quiet turns into absent-mindedness; under the stillness a small current must still run.

It appears in slow movements and codas, often beside andante and adagio; with sostenuto and dolce it marks off sustain, sweetness and calm from one another.
:::
