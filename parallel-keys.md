::: zh
同主音调（parallel keys）是主音相同、调式不同的一对大调与小调，例如 C 大调与 c 小调。它们共用一个主音，却有不同的三音、六音、七音与调号，像同一个家的明暗两面。

同主音调不是关系调。关系调共享调号而主音不同；同主音调共享主音而调号不同。大调向同主音小调借用 iv、降 VI 等和弦，就是常见的调式交替。

两调之间可以直接切换，也可以通过共同和弦或属和弦过渡。直接并置时色彩鲜明，常出现在奏鸣曲式的对比与流行歌曲的副歌转调中。
:::

::: en
Parallel keys are a major and a minor key on the same tonic, such as C major and C minor. They share the tonic but differ in third, sixth, seventh and key signature, like the light and dark sides of one home.

They are not relative keys. Relative keys share a key signature but not a tonic; parallel keys share a tonic but not a signature. A major key borrowing iv or ♭VI from its parallel minor is the familiar case of modal mixture.

The two keys can switch directly or pass through a shared chord or dominant. Direct juxtaposition is vivid and appears in sonata contrasts and in the key change of a pop chorus.
:::
