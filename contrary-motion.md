::: zh
**反向进行（contrary motion）** 是两个声部**一上一下**：一个升高，另一个降低。它是最容易听出**声部独立**的进行方式，也是避免平行五八度的常用手段。

它和**斜向进行**不同：斜向是一个声部不动、另一个动；反向是两个都动。常见误解是「反向一定好」——反向也会造成声部交错或音域失衡；它的价值在于让两条线分开，不是一条万能规则。

:::

::: en
**Contrary motion** has two voices moving **in opposite directions**: one rises while the other falls. It is the clearest way to hear **independent parts**, and a common way to avoid parallel fifths and octaves.

It differs from **oblique motion**: oblique keeps one voice still while the other moves; contrary moves both. The usual mistake is to think contrary motion is always good — it can also cause voice crossing or an unbalanced range; its value is separating two lines, not a universal rule.
