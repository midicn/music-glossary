::: zh
**副属和弦（secondary dominant）** 是**某个非主和弦的属和弦**：它不属于当前调，却以属的身份指向一个临时主音。记法像分数：**V/V** 表示「属和弦的属和弦」，V/ii 表示「二级和弦的属和弦」。

它的作用是**给一个和弦加临时的重力**，让进行更有方向。**常见误解是把副属和弦当成转调**：它通常只主音化下一个和弦，不建立新调；只有被终止式确认、中心真正搬走，才叫转调。**副属和弦是「借来的重力」，不是「搬了家」。**

::: en
**A secondary dominant** is **the dominant of a chord that is not the tonic**: it lies outside the current key yet points to a temporary tonic as a dominant. The notation is fractional: **V/V** is "the dominant of the dominant", V/ii "the dominant of the second degree".

Its job is **to lend a chord temporary gravity** and give the progression direction. **The usual mistake is to hear it as a modulation**: it usually tonicizes the next chord without establishing a new key; only a confirming cadence and a real move of centre make a modulation. **A secondary dominant is borrowed gravity, not a move.**
