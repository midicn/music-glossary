::: zh
**挂留（suspension）**是前一和弦的音在换和弦时保持，先协和、后不协和，再下行级进解决；**延留（retardation）**形态相同，但向上解决。完整过程分三个阶段：准备、挂留、解决，三者缺一不可。

数字标记如 9–8、7–6、4–3，表示挂留音与解决音的音程。挂留常落在强拍，是复调与终止式中的常客；延留较少见，多出现在上声部。挂留音也可以发生在内声部，形成更隐蔽的张力。

**任何长音都不是挂留。** 它必须有准备与解决，且解决方向通常向下；先现音是提前出现后一和弦的音，时间上与挂留相反，倚音则没有准备。分析时把三个阶段逐一写出，才能看清不协和从何而来、往何处去。
:::

::: en
**A suspension** holds a note from the previous chord into a new one, where it is first consonant and then dissonant, and finally resolves downward by step; a **retardation** has the same shape but resolves upward. The process has three stages — preparation, suspension and resolution.

Figures such as 9–8, 7–6 and 4–3 show the intervals of suspension and resolution. Suspensions usually fall on a strong beat and are common in counterpoint and cadences; retardations are rarer and usually in an upper voice. A suspension can also occur in an inner voice, with subtler tension.

**Not every long note is a suspension.** It needs preparation and resolution, normally downward; an anticipation arrives early instead of staying late, while an appoggiatura has no preparation. Write out all three stages to see where the dissonance comes from and goes.
:::
