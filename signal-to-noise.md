::: zh
**信噪比（signal-to-noise ratio）** 是**有用信号与噪声底的差值**：差值越大，声音越干净。它通常用分贝表示，但「干净」还要看噪声的**频谱与性质**。

它和**噪声底**不同：噪声底是绝对电平，信噪比是相对差；它和动态范围不同：动态范围是最大不失真信号与噪声底的差，信噪比通常指当前信号与噪声的差。常见误解是「信噪比高就一定好听」——噪声的类型也会影响听感；数字设备的信噪比很高，模拟设备则常带「有味道」的噪声。

::: en
**Signal-to-noise ratio** is **the gap between the wanted signal and the noise floor**: the bigger the gap, the cleaner the sound. It is usually expressed in decibels, but "clean" also depends on **the spectrum and character of the noise**.

It differs from the **noise floor**: the floor is absolute, the ratio relative; it differs from dynamic range: dynamic range is the gap between the loudest undistorted signal and the floor, while SNR usually compares the current signal with the noise. The usual mistake is to assume a higher number always sounds better — the type of noise matters; **digital gear has a high SNR, while analog gear often carries "characterful" noise.**
