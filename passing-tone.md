::: zh
**经过音（passing tone）** 是**在两个和弦音之间级进穿过**的非和弦音：它不占重拍时，通常只是把两个音连起来；占重拍时，才成为表情性的倚音。

它和**邻音**不同：经过音是**从 A 走到 B 的中间站**，邻音是离开再回来。常见误解是把经过音当成「填充音」——它决定旋律的方向与流畅度；没有经过音，跳进会显得生硬。

::: en
**A passing tone** is a non-chord tone **stepping between two chord tones**: unaccented, it usually just connects them; on a strong beat it becomes an expressive leaning tone.

It differs from a **neighbour tone**: a passing tone is **a station between A and B**, a neighbour leaves and returns. The usual mistake is to treat it as "filler" — it decides the melody's direction and flow; without passing tones, leaps feel stiff.
