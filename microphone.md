::: zh
**麦克风（microphone）** 是把声波造成的空气压力变化转成电信号的换能器。它是录音链路的第一环，后面所有的放大、均衡与混音都建立在它拾到的声音上。

按换能方式，常见的有动圈、电容与铝带三类；按用途还可分人声、乐器、测量等。选麦先看声源、声压级与所需指向性，再看手头的前置放大器。摆位改变往往比换一支更贵的麦更明显。

常见误解是以为贵麦就能录好一切。麦克风只忠实记录它所在位置听到的声音，房间、距离与角度同样决定结果；先学会听位置，再谈型号。
:::

::: en
**A microphone** converts changes in air pressure into an electrical signal. It is the first link in the recording chain; every later stage of gain, equalisation and mixing rests on what it captures.

Common types are dynamic, condenser and ribbon, grouped by transduction, with models for voice, instruments and measurement. Choose by source, sound pressure level and desired polar pattern, then by the preamp on hand. Moving a microphone often changes the result more than spending more on one.

The usual mistake is assuming a costly microphone records everything well. It reports only what reaches its position, so room, distance and angle matter just as much. Learn to listen to placement before shopping for models.
:::
