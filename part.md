::: zh
**分谱（part）** 是从总谱抽出的单人声部，只印这位演奏者要奏的音；其他声部至多以提示乐句出现。

分谱为使用而生：翻页要留出休止，长休止要写清小节数，排练记号与总谱一致，独奏或关键进入前常加提示。移调乐器的分谱按记谱音高写，演奏者自行移调。

分谱并非总谱的等比例裁切。它经过编辑：分页、提示、换谱号都为减少台上出错，同一段音乐在总谱与分谱上未必长得一样。
:::

::: en
**A part** is the music of one player, extracted from the score; it prints only what that player must play, and other lines appear at most as cue notes.

Parts are made for use. Page turns need rests, long rests need bar counts, rehearsal marks must match the score, and entries after solos or long silences often carry cues. For transposing instruments the part is written at written pitch, and the player transposes.

A part is not a proportional crop of the score. It is edited: page layout, cues and clef changes all exist to prevent errors on stage, so the same music may look different in score and part.
:::
