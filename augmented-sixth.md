::: zh
**增六和弦（augmented sixth chord）**的核心是降六级与升四级之间的增六度，两声部向外扩张到属音的八度。常见三种：意大利六和弦为降六级、主音、升四级；法国六和弦再加二级；德国六和弦再加降三级。

它们都是前属变和弦，常接属和弦或终止四六。德国六和弦听起来像属七和弦，但低音是降六级而不是属音，功能也属下属准备；直接解决到属时容易产生平行五度，常借终止四六缓冲。

**名称里的「六」指增六度音程**，不是三和弦的第一转位；三种类型也不是三个不同根音的和弦，而是同一框架上的不同变体。等音重拼能改变记谱与去向，听感相近而功能不同。
:::

::: en
**An augmented sixth chord** is built around the augmented sixth between the lowered sixth degree and the raised fourth, the two voices expanding outward to the octave on the dominant. Three forms are common: the Italian sixth (♭6–1–♯4), the French sixth (adding 2) and the German sixth (adding ♭3).

All three are chromatic predominants, normally leading to the dominant or a six-four. The German sixth sounds like a dominant seventh, but its bass is ♭6, not 5, and it functions as subdominant preparation; resolving it directly can produce parallel fifths, so a six-four is often used.

**The "sixth" in the name is an interval**, not the first inversion of a triad, and the three types are not chords on different roots but variants of one framework. Enharmonic respelling can change the notation and destination while sounding the same.
:::
