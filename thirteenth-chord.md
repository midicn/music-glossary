::: zh
十三和弦（thirteenth chord）是叠三度到十三音的和弦。十三音与六音同音级，但在七、九、十一音的叠置里，它是延伸色彩，不是附加的六度；十三和弦通常含七音，加六和弦则不含。

完整叠置有七个音，实际弹奏几乎总要省略：五音与十一音最常被省掉，尤其在属十三里，自然十一度会与三音冲突。常见 voicing 是根、三、七、九、十三，听感饱满而不浑浊。

大十三、小十三与属十三的色彩不同：大三度配大十三明亮，小三度配大十三柔和，属十三则带解决倾向。记谱上的 13 只是上限，不代表每个音都必须出现。
:::

::: en
A thirteenth chord stacks thirds up to the thirteenth. That note shares its pitch class with a sixth, but in a chord built through seventh, ninth and eleventh it is an extension, not an added sixth; a thirteenth chord normally includes the seventh, an added-six chord does not.

A full stack has seven notes, and players almost always omit some: the fifth and eleventh go first, especially in a dominant thirteenth, where the natural eleventh clashes with the third. A common voicing is root, third, seventh, ninth and thirteenth, full but not muddy.

Major, minor and dominant thirteenths differ in colour: a major third with a major thirteenth is bright, a minor third with a major thirteenth is soft, and the dominant thirteenth leans toward resolution. The 13 is a ceiling, not an order to sound every note.
:::
