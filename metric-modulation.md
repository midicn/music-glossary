::: zh
**节拍转调（metric modulation）**通过重新解释一个共同音值来改变节拍与速度：旧速度里的附点四分，变成新速度的一拍，两套脉搏按精确比例衔接。这一手法常与艾略特·卡特联系在一起。

它不同于渐快或渐慢，比例是算好的，可以记谱，也可以由演奏者准确执行。共同音值像枢纽和弦一样承担桥梁作用，鼓点或固定音型能帮听者抓住新旧脉搏。

**换拍号不等于节拍转调。** 直接改拍号未必改变一拍的速度；节拍转调的关键是脉搏被重新定义。它也与 rubato 不同：后者是时间弹性，前者是结构性的比例转换。
:::

::: en
**Metric modulation** changes metre and tempo by reinterpreting a shared note value: a dotted quarter from the old tempo becomes one beat of the new one, so the two pulses join in an exact ratio. The device is closely associated with Elliott Carter.

Unlike an accelerando or ritardando, the ratio is calculated, notated and can be performed precisely. The shared value works like a pivot chord, and a drum pattern or ostinato helps the ear catch the new pulse.

**A change of time signature is not metric modulation.** The new signature need not alter the speed of the beat; what matters is that the pulse itself is redefined. It also differs from rubato: rubato is flexible timing, while metric modulation is a structural exchange of proportions.
:::
