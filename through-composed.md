::: zh
**通谱体（through-composed）**指音乐随歌词或剧情不断向前，不把整段旋律按节反复。它常见于艺术歌曲、歌剧场景与音乐剧：人物情绪一变，音乐就换材料，必要时才让动机回来提醒旧事。舒伯特《魔王》是名例，四个角色各有自己的声音。

通谱体与分节歌相对，但不是「完全不重复」。它允许动机、和声型或伴奏音型回来，只是不把整节歌词套进同一段旋律。判断时看段落是否按节再现，而不是听有没有重复的音。

**通谱体也有风险：缺少反复，听众少了落脚点。** 作曲家常用调性回归、主导动机或副歌式的短句来维系统一，让不断向前的音乐仍然像一首完整的作品。
:::

::: en
**Through-composed** music moves forward with its text or drama instead of repeating a whole melody for each stanza. It is common in art song, opera scenes and musical theatre: when the emotion changes, so does the music, and motives return only when memory needs stirring. Schubert's "Erlkönig" is the famous example, with distinct music for narrator, father, son and Erlking.

Through-composed is the opposite of strophic, but not a ban on repetition. Motives, harmonic patterns or accompanimental figures may return; what does not return is a whole stanza-shaped melody. Judge it by whether sections recur per stanza, not by whether anything repeats.

**The risk is that constant change leaves the ear without a foothold.** Composers hold such pieces together with tonal return, a recurring motive or a refrain-like phrase, so constant motion still feels like one work.
:::
