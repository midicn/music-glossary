::: zh
**采样清权（sample clearance）** 是使用别人录音片段前取得的许可。它通常要**两层**：**词曲**（采样里包含的旋律/歌词）与**录音**（那段具体的声音）；两层可能属于不同的人，必须分别谈。

常见误解是「改一改就没事」：改变音高、速度或剪辑不自动消除权利；少量也不等于合理使用。清权谈不拢时，常见替代是重录/重奏（只清词曲）或换用版权库素材。采样前先问：我用的是一段录音，还是一段作品，还是两者都有。

::: en
**Sample clearance** is permission to use a fragment of someone else's recording. It usually takes **two layers**: the **composition** (the melody or lyric inside the sample) and the **master** (that specific sound); they may belong to different owners and must be negotiated separately.

The usual mistake is to think "change it a bit and you are safe": pitch-shifting, speeding up or editing does not automatically remove rights; a short fragment is not automatically fair use. When clearance fails, common alternatives are re-recording/replaying (composition only) or using library material. Before sampling, ask: am I using a recording, a work, or both.
