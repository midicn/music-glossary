::: zh
**还原号（natural sign）** 写作 ♮，用来取消前面出现的升号或降号，把音恢复到本位。它既可以取消本小节的临时变音，也可以取消调号带来的默认升降。

在调号之后写还原号，等于临时把这个音拉回本位：D 大调里的 C♮ 就是降七级。现代乐谱还常用提示性还原号：前一小节改过的音，到了新小节即使按规则已失效，也会再写一次提醒演奏者。

♮ 并非只对当前音符有效：它与其他变音记号一样，在小节内对同一音高持续有效。还原号的方形轮廓来自中世纪的 b 字形，读古谱时要结合上下文判断。
:::

::: en
A **natural sign**, written ♮, cancels a preceding sharp or flat and restores the unaltered note. It removes both a chromatic accidental in the bar and a default alteration carried by the key signature.

After a key signature, a natural pulls that degree back to its natural form: C♮ in D major is the flattened seventh. Modern scores also use courtesy naturals: a note altered in the previous bar is marked again in the new bar, even though the alteration has already lapsed, to warn the player.

♮ does not affect only its own note. Like other accidentals it lasts for that pitch to the end of the bar. The square shape of the natural descends from the medieval letter b, so read old sources in context.
:::
