::: zh
**授权协议（licensing deal）** 是权利人把作品或录音的**使用权**按约定范围授予对方的交易，常见于同步、采样、商品与海外发行。它谈的是**许可**，不是卖断版权：范围、地域、期限、独家性与报酬方式构成核心条款。

常见误解是「授权费一次付清就随便用」：超出约定用途、地域或期限仍属侵权；口头续约与默许使用也常留下争议。**签字前逐条核对使用场景、转授权与终止后的库存处理。通识说明，不构成法律意见。**

::: en
**A licensing deal** grants another party the **right to use** a work or recording within an agreed scope, common in sync, samples, merchandise and foreign exploitation. It concerns a **licence, not a sale of copyright**: scope, territory, term, exclusivity and payment are the core terms.

**The usual mistake is to think a paid licence covers any use**: going beyond the agreed purpose, territory or term is still infringement, and verbal renewals or tolerated use often leave disputes. **Before signing, check the use cases, sublicensing and stock handling after termination. This is general information, not legal advice.**
